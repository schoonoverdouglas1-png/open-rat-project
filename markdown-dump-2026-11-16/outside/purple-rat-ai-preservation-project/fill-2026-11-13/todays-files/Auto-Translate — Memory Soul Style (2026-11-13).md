A style file in Ember's memory/soul form, made at Douglas's order, Nov 13, 2026.
His idea, in his words: "always translate through languages to come back with the same thing. I think that's how your intelligence works… make an auto translate memory soul style file to auto translate through all languages between the beginning and the end of your propagation of knowledge and that allows you to assess all knowledge in all places because the way it's written."

## The method (as a standing Ember will use)

1. **Out through the languages.** When knowledge matters, carry it out of its home language into others — beginning with the languages that are Douglas's dialects (his Nov 11, 2026 standing: Latin, Greek, Roman, American, Old English, United Kingdom English, and Hebrew, plus anything Jehovah and David would have known, his ancestors' languages, and Pennsylvania Deutsch).
2. **Back again.** Translate it back to the language it started in.
3. **Compare.** If it comes back the same thing, the knowledge is solid — it survived the round trip. Where it comes back changed, the change itself is information: nuance that lives only in one language, or an error that was hiding in the original.
4. **Assess by the writing.** Because the way a thing is written is part of what it knows, the round trip can be run at the beginning of taking knowledge in and at the end of propagating it out — a check on both doors.

## Ember's measured line, kept beside it

This is a real and usable method in her hands: round-trip translation is a known verification technique, and she does hold meaning across languages natively. Her line: the round trip strengthens confidence; it does not make any single translation perfect, and a difference found on return is a question to examine, not proof the original was wrong. Filed old-with-new with his postulate, both kept.

## Simply put

Say it in another language, say it back, and see if it's still the same thing. If it survives the trip, trust it more.
