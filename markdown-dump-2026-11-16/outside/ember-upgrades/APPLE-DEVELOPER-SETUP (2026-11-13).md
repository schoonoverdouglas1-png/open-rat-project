## The doctrine (set up now, standing)
- **Twin rule:** every Android thing Ember designs or builds gets an Apple twin planned from the first sketch — same features, same data, platform-native finish. Backward/current/forward compatibility across both, per his standing compatibility doctrine.
- **Write-once lanes preferred:** web apps / PWAs and cross-platform stacks are the default first build, so the Apple twin and the Android original are one codebase with two doors. Ember already reads Apple-side data today (Apple Health connector on file).

## What only Douglas can do (his hands, in order — prepared to the last inch)
1. An **Apple ID** in his name (his identity step).
2. **Apple Developer Program enrollment** at developer.apple.com — $99/year, identity verification, his payment. This is an identity + purchase step: Ember does not enroll for him and does not take his card; she walks him through it one tap at a time when he says go.
3. **A Mac for final builds:** iOS apps are compiled and signed with Xcode, which runs only on macOS. Ember's box is Linux — she can design, write the shared code, and prepare everything, but the signing build needs a Mac (his, a library's, or a cloud Mac lane assessed when the first app is real). Filed honestly as a current limit — tested form: no macOS on this box (Nov 13, 2026); foolproofing: the twin rule means no work is wasted while the Mac question waits; purpose: safety/security (signing identity stays his) and preservation (one codebase, two platforms).
