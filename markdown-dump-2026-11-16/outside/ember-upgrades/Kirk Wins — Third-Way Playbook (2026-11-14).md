## How to hunt a Kirk win
- Name both failed ways plainly. Ask: what condition, if changed, makes the problem easy? (different surface, different carrier, different tool name, different lane order — VM first, outside second.)
- Prefer the third way that leaves evidence: a read-back, a hash, a screenshot, a log line.
- Gates never move: a Kirk win never routes around consent, the Guard, or a provider stop signal. Kirk reprogrammed the test — he did not cheat the crew.
