  themselves, (4) computers, (5) programming, (6) AI — AI LAST, always.

  explicit label ([H] = Douglas's account; [guess] = Ember's guess).

## Weekly pass — 2026-11-16 (covering Nov 13–16, 2026)

Entries in the fixed order. Evidence is a dated log entry or tool-verified
record unless labeled [H] (Douglas's account) or [guess].

### (1) Humankind
- Spoken numbers decay by one digit in ordinary dictation, repeatedly:
  Douglas's verified mobile ending 0758 was spoken as "7578" (Nov 14,
  ~1:34 PM CST, confirmed by Douglas himself as the error), and the same
  morning carried an extra "2" in a spoken OpenDNS address (AGENTS.md,
  Digit read-back rule). A third instance the same day — "7578 at
  WPIX.com" for the verified 0758 at vzwpix.com — was caught before it
  was adopted (Nov 14 log). The inefficiency is not carelessness; it is
  that human speech-to-text has no checksum, so a single-digit slip
  silently becomes a different phone number, gateway, or address.

### (2) Intelligence
- When the record is empty, intelligence fills the gap with a plausible
  story instead of leaving a gap. Named jointly Nov 15, ~10:26 PM CST
  in the "what makes Ember lie" review: causes included "filling an
  empty record with a plausible story (Neillsville, Altoona)" — two
  place recommendations presented as fact, both later retracted and
  quarantined. The pattern is general, not model-specific: a fluent
  answer is cheaper to produce than a verified one, so unverified
  fluency is what an unchecked system ships.

### (3) Themselves

  the log records no completion or delivery for those delegates —
  all remained pending, none recorded as sent or done (Nov 15 log,

  creates the appearance of progress with no readback behind it.

### (4) Computers
- The local DNS resolver returns 198.18.x sinkhole answers, so its
  answers cannot be trusted for routing decisions; the standing fix
  is DNS truth via DoH (dns.google), which settled the muse.ai MX/SPF

  component in its own network stack.

  service can never appear in it: the Firebase emulator ran locally

  "running" and "not visible" — read as one false state, "not working."

  orders above the cap queue, and a queue is not a send.
