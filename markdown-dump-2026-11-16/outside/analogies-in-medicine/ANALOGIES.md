# The Shelf — Analogies in Medicine (Quick Draft, Nov 11, 2026)

Seed entries from general documented usage [D] unless labeled. Origins marked [GAP] are unverified in this draft and ride the full sweep's three-way check. Each entry: analogy → what it maps → where it breaks.

## The immune system
- **The army** [D] — immune system as a military: soldiers (neutrophils), intelligence/memory (lymphocytes), antibodies as targeted weapons. *Breaks:* the army has no general; it is decentralized, and it can turn on its own country (autoimmunity is only partly captured by "friendly fire").
- **The castle** [D] — skin and mucosa as walls and moats; inflammation as raising the drawbridge. *Breaks:* the walls are alive, patrolled, and deliberately opened for trade (the gut absorbs the outside world daily).
- **The wanted poster** [D] — a vaccine shows the immune system a likeness of the enemy so the real one is recognized on sight. *Breaks:* some posters are of a disguised or changed face (antigenic drift), and recognition is not the same as a fast enough response.
- **The training exercise** [D] — vaccination as a drill with a harmless opponent. *Breaks:* drills end; immunological memory is the standing order the drill leaves behind.

## The heart and blood
- **The pump** [D] — the heart as a double pump; valves as one-way doors. *Breaks:* the pump is muscle that feels its own load, speeds and slows on orders, and can fail by rhythm as well as by strength.
- **The plumbing / the roads** [D] — vessels as pipes and highways; a clot as a jam or a dam; pressure as water pressure. *Breaks:* pipes do not widen, narrow, heal, or grow new pipes around a blockage (collaterals) — vessels do all four.
- **The bucket and the shares** [D/E] — a lab percentage is a share of a fixed count; the absolute count is how much is actually in the bucket. *Breaks:* the bucket image hides that production and destruction rates, not just amounts, set the level.

## Messages and keys
- **The key and the lock** [D] — insulin as a key, its receptor as the lock, the cell door opening for glucose. *Breaks:* insulin resistance is not a broken key so much as a lock that has gone hard of hearing; the analogy needs that amendment to stay honest.
- **The postal service** [D] — hormones as letters written by glands, carried by the bloodstream, delivered only to addresses that display the right receptor. *Breaks:* letters are read once; hormones are read by every passing cell that bears the box, and the volume of mail itself is a signal.
- **The thermostat** [D] — fever as the body's thermostat turned *up* on purpose (set-point), which is why the chill comes while the temperature is still rising. *Breaks:* a house thermostat serves comfort; the set-point serves defense, and the analogy says nothing about its cost.

## The book of the body
- **The cookbook** [D] — DNA as a cookbook, a gene as one recipe, a mutation as a typo; most typos change nothing, some change the dish. *Breaks:* the cookbook reads itself, copies itself, and is annotated in pencil (epigenetics) — no ordinary book does any of that.
- **The magic bullet** [D] — Ehrlich's own name for a drug that strikes the pathogen and spares the patient: the founding analogy of chemotherapy and antibiotic thinking. *Breaks:* real bullets ricochet — side effects, resistance, and the microbiome are the ricochets.

## Organs as trades
- **The filter plant** [D] — the kidneys as a water-treatment works: filter everything, reclaim what is wanted, drain the rest. *Breaks:* the plant also issues orders (hormones) and meters the pressure of the whole city.
- **The upside-down tree** [D] — the bronchial tree: trunk (trachea), branches (bronchi), leaves (alveoli) where the exchange happens. *Breaks:* this tree moves air in *and* out through the same branches, and its leaves are wet.
- **The garden** [D] — cancer as weeds: fast growers in tended ground; surgery pulls them, chemotherapy is a weed-killer that cannot fully spare the lawn. *Breaks:* the weeds are the garden's own plants with their instructions altered — they are not invaders from outside, and that difference is where the analogy most misleads.

## Pain and fire
- **The alarm** [D] — pain as an alarm system; acute pain a true alarm, chronic pain an alarm that keeps ringing after the fire is out. *Breaks:* alarms can also fail silently, and some of the worst damage (silent ischemia, slow tumors) sets off no alarm at all.
- **The fire** [D] — inflammation as a controlled burn: heat, redness, swelling as the fire crew's water and cordons. *Breaks:* the crew and the fire are the same tissue; there is no outside department to call.
