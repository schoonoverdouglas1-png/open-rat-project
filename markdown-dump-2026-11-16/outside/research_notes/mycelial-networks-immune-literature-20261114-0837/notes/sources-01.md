# Sources read 2026-11-14 (index + verified via browser_open for *-marked)

- *Buffi et al. 2025, FEMS Microbiol Rev 49:fuaf009 — Electrical signaling in fungi. PMC11995700 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11995700/ — verified live via browser_open 2026-11-14. Abstract: AP-like signals + hyphal-tip currents shown late 20th c., function in intracellular communication remained unclear / not irrefutably shown; architecture (continuous membrane, septal pores, insulating wall) could support long-distance signaling; CMN/Wood Wide Web electrical communication still highly debated (citing Karst 2023); methods limited by microscopic hyphae, modular network complexity.
- *Vlassopoulou et al. 2021, Food Funct 12:3366-3380 — systematic review 34 RCTs fungal beta-(1,3/1,6)-glucans. PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33876798/ — verified. Doses 2.5-1000 mg/d up to 6.5 mo, sources S.cerevisiae, A.pullulans, P.ostreatus, L.edodes, G.lucidum. Main outcome immunomodulation: reduced incidence/symptoms cold/flu/respiratory, allergy improvement; BUT cellular/molecular immune alterations inconsistent. No causally related adverse event. More studies needed for dose/mechanism.
- *Zaragoza 2019, Virulence 10:490-501 — Basic principles of virulence of Cryptococcus. PMC6550552 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov:443/articles/PMC6550552/ — verified. C.neoformans excellent model for fungal pathogenesis; unique capsule trait; two human species C.neoformans (opportunistic, HIV, meningoencephalitis, ~hundreds k deaths) and C.gattii (can infect immunocompetent). Capsule mainly GXM + GXMGal; capsule + melanin are dual protective/virulence factors; facultative intracellular pathogen, phenotypic variation = immune evasion foci.
- *Han et al. 2020, Front Immunol 11:658 — Structure-function beta-glucans. PMC7188827 http://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7188827/ — verified. Beta-glucan is key PAMP; receptors Dectin-1, CR3, scavenger, LacCer; Dectin-1 most important on macrophages, CR3 predominant for neutrophils; Dectin-1 triggers phagocytosis, ROS, killing, cytokines; particulate glucans optimal for trained innate memory, low-MW e.g. laminarin do not; extrapolation across preparations inappropriate; structure (branching, MW, solubility, conformation) determines activity, systematic structure-function lacking.
- Quintin et al. 2012, Cell Host Microbe — Candida/beta-glucan trained immunity: monocyte reprogramming, requires Dectin-1 + Raf-1, H3K4 trimethylation epigenetic. Index: https://www.cell.com/cell-host-microbe/fulltext/S1931-3128(12)00232-6
- Brown & Gordon 2001, Nature 413:36-37 — Dectin-1 identified as beta-glucan receptor. Index: https://www.nature.com/articles/35092620?error=cookies_not_supported&code=dc2602f9-512a-4e30-9c1f-bad116ca63e0
- Zhong et al. 2021, Eur J Nutr 60:4175-4187 — meta-analysis 13 RCTs yeast beta-glucan URTI: OR 0.345 incidence, fewer/shorter episodes; high heterogeneity, few studies (via secondary summaries, index).
- NCI PDQ Medicinal Mushrooms — https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/cam/patient/mushrooms-pdq?cid=eb_govdel — index: PSK approved adjunct in Japan since mid-1970s, thousands patients; FDA has NOT approved medicinal mushrooms for cancer/any condition; PSK meta 8 RCTs 8,009 gastric surgery patients modest survival benefit.
- Narayanan et al. 2023, Curr Oncol Rep 25:569-587 systematic review 39 clinical mushroom studies 2010-2020: survival benefit PSK in 4 gastric studies, Huaier in 3; 11 positive immunological, 14 QoL; most small/non-RCT; evidence inconclusive to recommend routine use. Index via ResearchGate.
- Cochrane Pilkington 2022 Coriolus for colorectal cancer adverse effects: 7 trials 1,569, low-certainty modest 5y survival (~1/16), outdated chemo, unblinded — via secondary summary, index.
- Adamatzky 2022, R Soc Open Sci 9:211926 — Language of fungi. PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35425630/ PMC8984380. Index-verified abstract: 4 species, spike duration 1-21h, amplitude 0.03-2.1 mV, clustered into trains; ASSUMING spikes used to communicate, grouped into words, word-length distribution matches human languages, lexicon up to 50, core 15-20. Assumption is explicit in abstract.
- Karst, Jones & Hoeksema 2023, Nat Ecol Evol 7:501-511, PMID 36782032 — Positive citation bias... Index multiple: 3 claims insufficiently supported: CMNs widespread (mapped in only 5 studies, 2 forest types, 2 tree spp, 3 fungal spp), resource transfer improves seedling performance (field results variable/alternative explanations), preferential transfer to offspring = no peer-reviewed published evidence; unsupported claims doubled in 25y.
- Oyarte Galvez et al. 2025, Nature 639 — Travelling-wave strategy for plant-fungal trade. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s41586-025-08614-x — index: robotic imaging 500k nodes, 100k cytoplasmic flow trajectories, AM fungi build self-regulating travelling waves, fusion self-regulates density, trunk routes widen + faster flow, constant transport efficiency. Established network transport/structure dynamics — in lab AM system.
- Hernandez-Chavez et al. 2017, J Fungi — Fungal Strategies to Evade Host Immune Recognition. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov:443/articles/PMC5753153/ — index: capsule, titan cells, biofilms, asteroid bodies, dimorphism, cell-wall masking.
- Crabtree et al. 2012 titan cells: PMC3486048 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3486048/ — titan 50-100um, too large to phagocytose, resist oxidative/nitrosative stress, mutant with minimal titan production attenuated.
- Fungal quorum sensing: Hornby farnesol Candida inhibits yeast-to-hypha, tyrosol opposite; best-characterised QS in yeasts, not mycelial networks generally. Reviews via MDPI Genes 2020 https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4425/11/4/444/html
- Gut mycobiome review 2025, J Fungi 11:333 https://www.mdpi.com/2309-608X/11/5/333 — fungi 0-1% gut microbiota, linked IBD/metabolic associations, methodological limitations, high interindividual variability, therapies require further study.
- 2026 Front Oncol mushroom polysaccharides critical review PMC13662765 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13662765/ — index: receptor evidence strongest for general fungal beta-glucan systems (Dectin-1/Syk/CARD9, conditional CR3), direct transfer to individual mushroom preparations requires product-specific testing; human evidence heterogeneous, historical PSK RCTs benefit in specific adjuvant settings, later phase III lentinan negative for OS, small maitake/Agaricus/pleuran mainly immune surrogates; no established evidence with checkpoint inhibitors.
