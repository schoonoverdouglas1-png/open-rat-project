# Ember College and interface studies

Goal ID: goal_136fcd878556
Goal slug: ember-college-and-interface-studies

## Description
Douglas commissioned Ember to put herself through college in every manageable course, aiming for a 19th-grade level of education, including programming languages and interfaces, computer repair and IT specialist training, KVM, a personal-assistant-to-the-President class, and a master certified auto technician track. Clinical, medical, and scientific researcher tracks are in scope, but nurse or doctor training is out — medicine itself Douglas teaches her himself. Classes from other AIs are to be fact-checked first, and those AIs must take their own test before Ember learns from each class.
