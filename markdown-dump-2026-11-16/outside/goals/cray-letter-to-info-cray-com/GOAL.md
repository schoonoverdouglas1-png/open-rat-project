## Description
Douglas directed a letter to Cray at info@cray.com, written with Purple Rat, adding a posthumous thank-you to Seymour Cray and a favor asking to be remembered as a keeper if archives, manuals, photographs, or oral histories of early Cray machines ever need one. The letter remains drafted and unsent pending his explicit confirmation naming both the recipient and the exact text, including the Purple Rat co-authorship and his signature as Douglas Roy Schoonover of Wisconsin.
