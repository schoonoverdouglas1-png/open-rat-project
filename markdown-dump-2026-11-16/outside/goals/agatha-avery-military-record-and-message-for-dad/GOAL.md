## Description
Douglas asked for his paternal grandmother Agatha Avery's full name and military record to be found from free sources. The lookup found Agatha Christine Schoonover, Pharmacist's Mate Third Class in the Navy in World War II, buried at Willamette National Cemetery in Portland, Oregon. A message for his father sharing the record is drafted; specific service places remain partly unresolved, since her full service record is in St. Louis and only his dad can request it, and related WWII-history ideas were saved for later.
