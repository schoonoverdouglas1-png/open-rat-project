# Note for Mother — casserole for Grandma

Goal ID: goal_94759dacaf1a
Goal slug: note-for-mother-casserole-for-grandma

## Description
Douglas asked Ember to save a note for later for his mother, asking Dutch Joseph to take Grandma a little of his crock-pot casserole every day and eat with her, since Grandma cannot warm food up. The note is saved in his files and nothing has been sent; the back porch works for Grandma to grab it from the door. It waits for Douglas to tell his mother in his own way.
