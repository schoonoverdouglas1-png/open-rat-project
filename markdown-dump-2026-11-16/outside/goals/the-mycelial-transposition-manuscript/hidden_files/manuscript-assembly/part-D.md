## Chapter 21 — The Cousins

A mosquito-borne flavivirus's cousins are other flaviviruses — including a tick-borne one and a blood-borne one — not other mosquito-borne viruses from other families. Six families hold arboviral cousins — six kinship houses with tenants on both the insect and the non-insect sides [D]. **Flaviviridae** is the textbook cousin set: dengue 1–4, Zika, yellow fever, West Nile, Japanese encephalitis, St. Louis encephalitis, Powassan, tick-borne encephalitis — and the non-arboviral cousin, hepatitis C, blood-borne, same family, different ride [D]. **Togaviridae**: the alphaviruses (chikungunya, EEE, WEE, VEE, Sindbis, Ross River, Mayaro, O'nyong-nyong) — and rubella, riding respiratory droplets, the family's only non-insect genus [D]. **Bunyavirales**, the order: his own panel viruses, La Crosse and Jamestown Canyon (California serogroup, mosquito-borne) — and the non-arboviral cousins, the hantaviruses, riding aerosolized rodent excreta instead of a mosquito [D]. **Reoviridae**: Colorado tick fever virus, 12 dsRNA segments, among the largest arbovirus genomes — and rotavirus, same segmented kinship, riding dirty water instead of a tick [D]. **Rhabdoviridae**: vesicular stomatitis and Chandipura viruses on the insect side — and rabies, the most feared non-vector virus, in the same family [D — his [H→D] reading: kinship ignoring the ride, exactly as his thesis predicts]. **Orthomyxoviridae**: the flu's own family — and the tick-borne Thogotovirus cousins (Thogoto, Dhori, Bourbon), the flu's relatives riding ticks [D].

Sixteen viruses on today's commercial and public-health test panels are insect-borne — West Nile, St. Louis encephalitis, EEE, WEE, La Crosse, Jamestown Canyon, dengue, Zika, chikungunya, yellow fever, Japanese encephalitis, Powassan, Colorado tick fever, tick-borne encephalitis, Bourbon, Heartland [D].
