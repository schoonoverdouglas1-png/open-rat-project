### The charge

When Douglas was a young man, his doctor friend David Cook gave him a charge: **try to save medicine and save the world somehow.** This book is his answer, still in progress.

### The opening (his ruling)

*"We're all born sick so we can get better or worse."*

The reader feels first the **charge** of *better or worse* — the born-sick weight lands behind it. That is the author's ruling on his own opening line, and the book obeys it.

### His doctrines (his words, filed as his [H])

- **The patient creed:** never abandon the patient.
- **The healing creed:** healing is blessed, next to God; theology first; the body is never wrong.
- **The virome creed:** the virus hangs out until the underlying disease is gone — it neither feasts nor takes nor leaves; it is part of the process of everything.
- **The two trees:** "With medicine you have at least two trees, but the two trees are the doctor and the patient. And the things in between are the things that get in the way of that doctor and that patient getting better. And there's a path for both to get better together."
- **Stop blame:** "We gotta stop using blame in medicine and start looking at the solution instead."
- **The threshold:** "The playing games have to end where the healing begins."
- **The mission:** clinical intuition put on paper — "good old medicine used to just fill those gaps with the human intuition of the brain, which is what I'm trying to somehow put on paper" — in the most responsible manner possible, with the hopes of saving the world in medicine together.

### His method (standing, his words)

Form every idea as a theory or postulate and save it. Test it against the record at least three different ways before answering the first time, if possible. Never let the method limit the thinking — the discipline is in the testing, not in holding back. When he agrees with a standard fact, it stands as a theorem paralleling the known information. When one dictation holds many ideas, each becomes its own postulate — never collapsed, none lost. Every deliverable gets one extra thing: the builder's own judgment, clearly labeled as the builder's. Provenance on everything — his dictation, the record, the addition.

### How to read this book

- **Tier tags** ride on every claim: **[D]** documented/verified · **[H]** his — Douglas's thesis, account, or coinage, filed verbatim · **[H→D]** his with a documented foothold · **[GAP]** his to fill — the empty cells, his future work.
- **Five tracks, never collapsed:** vaccines, viruses, diseases, illnesses, sicknesses. A virus is not its disease; a disease is not its illness; an illness is not its sickness; a vaccine is not any of them.
- **Reading level:** thirteenth grade — his level. Complex information in, simple out. Simple words are respectful; simplified ideas are not.
- **Scope:** this book is dedicated to quantum medicine and the connection between all things. The deep vaccinology and immunology answers belong in a future different book, not this one.
- **Order of march** (his guardrail): verified information with the most material first, then his complete dictated record, then the built scaffolds, then his future fill-ins last.
