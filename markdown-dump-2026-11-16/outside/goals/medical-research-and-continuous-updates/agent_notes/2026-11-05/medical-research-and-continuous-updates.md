On November 5, 2026, at about 1:51 AM CST (07:51 UTC), Douglas dictated a synthesis of fungal analogues to complete the comparative matrix, followed at about 1:52 AM CST (07:52 UTC) by an eight-row vaccine/toxoid comparative table. The main agent tested both, filed them, and pushed them to the GitHub repository `vaccine-toxoid-research` the same morning. The study's reading of this, for this goal, is about the shape of the whole, not the individual rows:

- Bacterial pathogens rely on diverse capsular polysaccharides (CPS); major fungal pathogens use analogous cell-wall and capsular polymers to evade host immunity. The continuum Douglas named runs from nutritional soy polysaccharides, through bacterial CPS, to fungal virulence armor.
- *Cryptococcus neoformans / gattii*: glucuronoxylomannan (GXM) capsule alongside galactoxylomannan (GalXM), functionally analogous to pneumococcal CPS; experimental GXM-protein conjugates using CRM197 or TT are researched to bridge bacterial conjugate architecture with fungal capsular targets.
- *Candida albicans / auris*: cell wall rich in mannan and beta-glucan complexes; outer fibrillar layer of phosphopeptidomannan, acting similarly to bacterial surface antigens in triggering pattern recognition receptors.
- *Aspergillus fumigatus*: galactomannan as a key diagnostic biomarker and cell-wall component, another high-molecular-weight polysaccharide structure posing similar clearance challenges to encapsulated bacteria.
- Verdicts recorded in main chat that morning: Cryptococcus holds (GXM 90% / GalXM 10%, GXM-TT protects mice, CRM197 semisynthetic M2 motif gives modest protection), with the limit that no fungal vaccine is licensed for people yet; *Candida albicans* holds (mannan / beta-glucan wall, phosphopeptidomannan outer layer, recognized by dectin-1, mannose receptor, and others); *Aspergillus fumigatus* holds (galactomannan an emblematic diagnostic biomarker, ELISA, three forms in the cell wall).
- One correction filed beside the table, not hidden: the pneumococcal carrier cell as listed breaks — PCV7 / PCV13 / PCV20 in Prevnar use CRM197 only (CDC lists all 20 PCV20 serotypes individually linked to a nontoxic variant of diphtheria toxin known as CRM197). TT and DT are not Prevnar carriers; they belong as a separate note for other conjugate products. Everything else in the table held at the 51% bar, including MenB capsule resembling human NCAM (hence protein antigens instead of CPS conjugation), Hib PRP with CRM197, TT, or OMPC, Vi with rEPA or TT (Vi-rEPA not commercially available, Vi-TT licensed), and GBS serotypes Ia, Ib, II, III, IV, V with CRM197, investigational, no GBS vaccine licensed yet.
