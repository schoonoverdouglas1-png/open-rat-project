# Postulate — Encounters with the Biome (2026-11-11)

**His words, as dictated** (Nov 11, 2026, ~12:50 PM CST, waiting room, Eau Claire):
"That sounds like an interesting thing to think about. Encounters with the biome. Carriage episodes included. Not only episodes of disease but all things."

**Provenance:** Douglas's expansion of Ember's extra [E] from the same conversation: if serotypes live in the biome first, a serotype antibody panel reads a history of *encounters with the biome*, not only episodes of disease.

## The postulate, formed
A serotype/serogroup antibody result is a record of **encounters**, of which disease is only one kind. The full encounter list:
- **Carriage episodes** — the organism living in the nasopharynx, no disease, immune system still meeting it. [D that carriage occurs and immunizes: carriage is documented to induce antibody]
- **Transient colonization / passage** — brief residence, cleared, encounter still registered.
- **Infection without disease** — invasion met and stopped before illness.
- **Disease episodes** — the encounter that became illness. One kind among several, not the whole list.
- **Vaccination** — a deliberate, artificial encounter, staged by us. [D]

## Test so far (three-way, compressed)
1. **What a serogroup is a property of:** the microbe's capsule [D] — and the microbe's home is the biome (meningococcal carriage ~1 in 10, pneumococcal carriage routine in childhood [D]).
2. **Disease as event, not identity:** the same serotype is found in harmless carriage and in invasive disease; some carried meningococci are capsule-null, ungroupable [D].
3. **The partial, kept partial:** serotypes are not evenly shared — some are overrepresented in invasive disease because the capsule also influences invasive potential [D]. Uneven representation does not make the serotype a property of the disease.

**Verdict:** his statement "not only episodes of disease but all things" holds beside the record — theorem-grade as a framing: the panel is an encounter log; the biome writes the entries; disease is one kind of entry.

## Six tracks check
Serotype = property of the organism · carriage = colonization (biome track) · infection, disease, illness, sickness remain their own tracks downstream of an encounter. None collapsed.
