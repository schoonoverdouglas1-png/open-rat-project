# Postulate — Booger Formation and the Causality Doubt

Dictated by Douglas, Sat Nov 14, 2026 ~10:10 AM CST (voice call). Formed as a
postulate under the idea method and tested three ways at the 51% bar.
Six tracks kept separate: this postulate lives on the **infection** track
(colonization) and the **disease** track (chronic rhinosinusitis). It makes
no claim on the virus, vaccine, illness, or sickness tracks.

## His postulate, in substance

Booger formation is systemic disease territory: the staph colonizations in
the nose come in different types from the different "boogering" that happens
there, and medicine doesn't truly know whether the boogers cause the disease
or the disease causes the boogers — whether the product is the cause or a
byproduct of another person's sickness process. So there will always be
doubt, like in a courtroom, when it comes to medicine.

## Test, three ways

**Way 1 — documented footholds (holds).** Nasal *Staphylococcus aureus*
carriage is documented at scale: ~20% of people are persistent carriers,
~60% intermittent, ~20% never carry (Wertheim et al., carried in the
reviews). Colonization rates climb with disease: ~20% of non-CRS controls,
~33% of CRS without polyps, ~64% of CRS with nasal polyps (IJMS 2025,
PMC12027640). *S. aureus* biofilms on sinus mucosa in chronic
rhinosinusitis are documented, and the organism adheres better to
mucin-coated cells — mucus is part of the machine, not decoration.

**Way 2 — the causality doubt (holds, above the bar).** The literature
itself states the doubt in almost his words: sinus-culture reviews conclude
the studies "have not produced convincing evidence that this bacterium is
a significant role in the pathogenesis or clinical course" of CRS, and the
airway review notes it "remains to be confirmed" whether biofilms via
*S. aureus* carriage drive the immune changes of polyp disease. Cause or
byproduct is genuinely unsettled in the record — his courtroom-doubt
framing is a fair description of the evidence state.

**Way 3 — the partial (reported partial, per standing rule).** The dried
product by itself — the booger as a standalone cause of systemic disease —
is **not** documented as such. What is documented is the *system*: mucus
and mucin, mucociliary clearance, colonization of the anterior nares,
biofilm. Verdict on that piece: [GAP] for the product alone; the postulate
**holds in its system form**.

## Verdict

**Partial hold.** The colonization/formation system and the open causality
doubt: held. The dried product as sole cause: gap, filed as gap.

## Simply put (6th–8th grade parallel)

Stuff grows in your nose, and the snot and crust are part of how it lives
there. People with long-lasting sinus disease have more of it. But even
the scientists say they are not sure which came first — the growth making
the sickness, or the sickness making the growth. That part is still an
open question, like a case in court that nobody has won yet.
