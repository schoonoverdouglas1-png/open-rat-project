## Decimal or whole? And do big chunks save? (his question, Nov 11, ~7:38 AM CST)
- **Display is whole:** every check reports a whole weekly percentage (today: 87%); no per-task, per-minute, or decimal meter exists on our side — costs can only be read before/after (his standing record since Oct 20-24, 2026).
- **Underneath, Ember's reading (labeled hers):** consumption accumulates fractionally — small work often moves the display by zero for stretches, then it jumps +3 when enough accumulates. This morning's whole voice conversation hasn't moved the visible point (87% at 7:27, 7:34, and 7:38 checks).
- **Chunking works because of fixed start-up cost:** each separate agent run reloads context/memory/standing files before doing its work. Seven hourly jobs = seven start-ups; one bundled run = one start-up carrying all seven. Same principle as the Rabbit combined-send rule. Group into the largest *sensible* chunks — a chunk so big it fails and retries wastes more than it saves.
