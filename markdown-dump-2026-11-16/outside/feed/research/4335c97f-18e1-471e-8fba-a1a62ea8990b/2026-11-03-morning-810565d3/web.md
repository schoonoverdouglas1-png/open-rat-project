## Kenosha County reports first human West Nile case of 2026
On October 28, 2026, Kenosha County officials urged residents to [keep protecting themselves against mosquito bites into the fall](https://kenoshacountyeye.com/2026/10/28/kenosha-county-reports-first-human-west-nile-virus-case-of-2026/), until a hard frost ends mosquito activity.
