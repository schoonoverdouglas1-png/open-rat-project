## Vaccine-type pneumococci persisted after PCV10
In a study published October 26, 2026, [38% of confirmed pneumococci in Ugandan children aged five and under carried vaccine-associated capsular targets](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-026-08090-0) five years after PCV10 introduction. The assay grouped some targets, so it could not separate 7F from 7A.
