## GitHub issue types and fields reach personal accounts
Issue types and issue fields are now [generally available on repositories owned by personal accounts](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-11-issue-types-and-fields-are-available-for-personal-accounts/). The settings live in your personal account settings under Planning, where the default types are Bug, Task and Feature.
