## Moderna/Merck mRNA vaccine cuts melanoma recurrence or death by 42%
Moderna and Merck's personalized mRNA vaccine intismeran, given with Keytruda, [cut the risk of melanoma recurrence or death by 42%](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/3996705-merckmoderna-cancer-vaccine-cuts-risk-of-melanoma-death-or-recurrence-by-over-40?amp) in a Phase 3 trial of more than 1,100 patients. It is the first large trial to show that a vaccine built from the mutations unique to a patient's own tumor can keep the cancer from coming back.
