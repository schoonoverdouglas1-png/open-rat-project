## Eau Claire's alternate-side parking season starts November 1
[Alternate-side parking restrictions](https://www.eauclairewi.gov/AlternateSideParking) apply on all city streets between midnight and 7 a.m. The date after midnight decides the side, so odd-numbered dates mean the odd-numbered side of the street.
