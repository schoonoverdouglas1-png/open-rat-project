## Mapping fungal diagnoses across ICD versions
Reviewers manually sifted more than 120,000 ICD descriptions to find every fungal code, including ones hiding outside the mycoses section [in the Journal of Fungi](https://www.mdpi.com/2309-608X/12/10/752). They warn that renames like Candidozyma auris (formerly Candida auris) fracture medical coding unless the versions are actively harmonized.
