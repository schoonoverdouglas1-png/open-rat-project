## Newfound soil fungus brews rare antifungals
A fungus pulled from Rotterdam garden soil has been [formally described as Garciamycella chlamydospora](https://bioengineer.org/new-soil-fungus-garciamycella-chlamydospora-yields-rare-antifungal-papulacandins/), the founding species of an entirely new genus. The most potent of the three papulacandins it makes, Mer-WF3010, stopped Candida albicans at just 0.2 micrograms per milliliter by jamming the cell-wall enzyme (1,3)-beta-D-glucan synthase.
