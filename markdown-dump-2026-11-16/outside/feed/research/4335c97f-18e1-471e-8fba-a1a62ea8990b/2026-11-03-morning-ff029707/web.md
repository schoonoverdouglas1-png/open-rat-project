## Normal genomes carry more 'pathogenic' variation than you'd think
[University of Exeter researchers](https://www.diagnosticsworldnews.com/news/2026/11/03/normal-genomes-carry-more--pathogenic--variation-than-you-d-think) found the median person differs from the reference genome at about 4.4 million positions. Because the cohorts are largely healthy, Caroline Wright argues most variants of uncertain significance are likely benign.
