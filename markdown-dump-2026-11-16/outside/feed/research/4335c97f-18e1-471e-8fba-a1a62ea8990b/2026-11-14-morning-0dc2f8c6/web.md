## US loses measles-free status after continued spread
The Pan American Health Organization has [revoked the United States' measles-elimination status](https://www.kz1023.com/2026/11/13/us-loses-measles-free-status-after-continued-spread-of-the-disease-wsj-reports/), ending a 26-year run. PAHO reviewed CDC evidence to determine whether the same strain had circulated uninterrupted for 12 months or longer.
