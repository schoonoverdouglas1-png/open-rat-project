## Dianjunus rex anchors a new fungal family
On September 29, researchers [formally described Dianjunus rex](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/fungal-biology/articles/10.3389/ffunb.2026.1940464/full) as the type species of the new family Dianjunaceae. Its genus name joins the Dian Kingdom of present-day Yunnan, where the specimens were collected, with jūn, meaning mushroom or fungus.
