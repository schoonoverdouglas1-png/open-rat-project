## Mosquito-borne Potosi virus confirmed in fatal brain infection
Postmortem examination of a woman on rituximab gave the first tissue proof that [Potosi virus](https://consultqd.clevelandclinic.org/neuroinvasive-threat-for-patients-on-b-cell-depleting-therapy) causes severe central nervous system infection. Metagenomic sequencing of her spinal fluid matched all three segments of the virus genome.
