## One blood draw screens 68,000 pathogens
[Delve Detect Plasma](https://www.labmedica.com/molecular-diagnostics/articles/294813912/blood-based-metagenomic-test-detects-wide-range-of-pathogens-from-single-sample.html) reads one blood draw with metagenomic sequencing and screens for more than 68,000 bacteria, fungi, viruses and parasites, with results the day after the sample arrives and no target the clinician must name in advance. The clinical data behind it lands at IDWeek 2026 in Washington, October 21–24, including posters on hard-to-detect neuroinvasive arboviruses and central nervous system infections.
