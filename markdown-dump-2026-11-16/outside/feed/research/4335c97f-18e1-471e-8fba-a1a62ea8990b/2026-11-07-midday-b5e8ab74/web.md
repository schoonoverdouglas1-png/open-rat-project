## Serotype 19A rose when PCV7 skipped it
PCV7 covered six of the seven serotypes behind most resistant invasive pneumococcal disease in the United States, but not 19A. [PCV13 added 19A in 2010](https://www.cdc.gov/pneumococcal/php/drug-resistance/index.html), and the CDC's October 27 update says its invasive disease then fell greatly.
