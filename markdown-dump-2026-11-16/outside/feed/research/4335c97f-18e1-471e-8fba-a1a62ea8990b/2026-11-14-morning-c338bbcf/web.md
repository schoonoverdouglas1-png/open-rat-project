## Serotype coverage fell across PCVs in Serbian children
In a study published April 21, 2026, [PCV10 coverage of invasive pneumococcal disease in Serbian children fell to 35.6% from 67.7%](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2026.1801133/full) after the PCV10/PCV13/PCV10 sequence took effect. Non-PCV20 serotypes climbed to 22.9% of invasive isolates from 7.2%, and that ongoing replacement is why the authors call for continued integrated surveillance.
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2026.1801133/full (published 2026-04-21)
