## Naturally occurring fungus could help protect wheat from disease
Wheat treated with [Chaetomium globosum](https://westernmorning.news/2026/10/08/naturally-occurring-fungus-could-help-protect-wheat-from-disease-study/) three days before exposure to Septoria tritici blotch had 74% less leaf lesion area in the worst-hit variety. It also secretes antifungal compounds that attack Zymoseptoria tritici, the fungus behind the blotch.
