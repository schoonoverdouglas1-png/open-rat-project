## Itraconazole susceptibility falls in resistant ringworm
Reduced [itraconazole susceptibility](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8247/19/11/1739) reached 25–37% in selected cohorts from endemic regions. The review's candidate to watch is fosravuconazole, an oral prodrug of the fourth-generation triazole ravuconazole.
