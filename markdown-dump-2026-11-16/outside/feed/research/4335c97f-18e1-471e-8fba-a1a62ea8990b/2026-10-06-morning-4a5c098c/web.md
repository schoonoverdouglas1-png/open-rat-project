## Penicillium strain cuts heavy metals in field rice
In contaminated paddy fields in Hunan, rice inoculated with a Penicillium strain carried significantly less chromium, cadmium, lead, and antimony in its roots, shoots, and grains than untreated controls, according to [the field study write-up](https://scienmag.com/soil-fungus-slashes-heavy-metal-buildup-in-rice-by-rewiring-roots-and-microbes/). Sequencing and metabolomics trace the shift to a reshaped rhizosphere microbiome and reprogrammed root metabolism.
