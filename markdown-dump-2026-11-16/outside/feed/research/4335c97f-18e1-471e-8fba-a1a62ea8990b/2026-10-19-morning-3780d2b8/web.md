## Candida auris evades antifungals without FKS1 mutations
Serial bloodstream isolates from two critically ill London patients [failed anidulafungin therapy](https://www.contagionlive.com/view/candida-auris-evades-antifungals-without-fks1-mutations) despite susceptible MICs and no non-synonymous mutations in FKS1 or FKS2. The escape routes were elsewhere: heteroresistant subpopulations growing at 256-fold drug concentrations, CRZ1 loss-of-function mutations, chitin-thickened cell walls, and drug-induced ploidy shifts.
