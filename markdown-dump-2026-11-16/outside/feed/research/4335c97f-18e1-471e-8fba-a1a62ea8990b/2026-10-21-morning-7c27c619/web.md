## Oil and gas construction lifts Valley fever risk
Living within 3 miles of a new well meant a [12.5% higher Valley fever risk](https://eos.org/articles/oil-and-gas-development-heightens-risk-of-valley-fever) in the 90 days after construction began. The UC Berkeley team matched anonymized state case addresses against Kern County build-outs from 2007 to 2022.
