## Meningococcal disease is rising in adults
Invasive meningococcal cases [more than doubled by 2024](https://www.hhmglobal.com/interviews/meningococcal-disease-is-not-just-a-college-problem-dr-jana-shaw-on-rising-adult-cases-and-prevention-gaps) after their pandemic-era decline. A hypervirulent serogroup Y strain is driving the shift out of college settings and into communities.
