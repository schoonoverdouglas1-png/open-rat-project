## No pediatric data for five new antifungals
The five novel antifungals in late-stage development or newly approved, rezafungin, ibrexafungerp, fosmanogepix, olorofim and opelconazole, are backed by no pediatric efficacy data, per [the International Pediatric Association working group's synthesis, published October 8](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/tropical-diseases/articles/10.3389/fitd.2026.1970689/full). Its audit of the whole pipeline found exactly one registered pediatric pharmacokinetic study, closed early after enrolling two adolescents.
