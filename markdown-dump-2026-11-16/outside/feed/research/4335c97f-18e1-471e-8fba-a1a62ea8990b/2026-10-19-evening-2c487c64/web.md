## Lichen compound usnic acid supercharges posaconazole in lab tests
Azole-resistant fungi may not need a new drug: a lichen compound with no antifungal activity of its own turned posaconazole [synergistic against 74 of 110 clinical isolates](https://scienmag.com/lichen-compound-usnic-acid-supercharges-a-key-antifungal-drug-in-lab-tests/). The effect was strictly azole-specific: synergy fell to 11.82 percent with itraconazole, and the pairing turned outright antagonistic against Cryptococcus neoformans when combined with voriconazole or fluconazole.
