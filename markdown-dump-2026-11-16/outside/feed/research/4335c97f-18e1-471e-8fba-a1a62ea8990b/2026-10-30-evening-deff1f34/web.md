## Uranium ditelluride shows both superconductivity flavors at once
[Cornell researchers showed](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/10/two-flavors-superconductivity-material-has-right-ingredients-twist) uranium ditelluride can exhibit both flavors of superconductivity at the same time. The two flavors have not yet been shown to mix into a topological superconductor, so the quantum-computing payoff is still several steps away.
