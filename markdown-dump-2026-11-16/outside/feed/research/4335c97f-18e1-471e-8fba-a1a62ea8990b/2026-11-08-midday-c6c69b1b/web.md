## MenB vaccine with PCV13 was immunogenic in US infants
In a phase 3b trial, [4CMenB given alongside PCV13 and routine infant vaccines](https://academic.oup.com/ofid/advance-article/doi/10.1093/ofid/ofag709/9015908?searchresult=1) left 86.1–99.6% of infants seroprotected against MenB indicator strains after the fourth dose. PCV13 met its non-inferiority criterion in the same schedule, with no immunological interference from the co-administration.
