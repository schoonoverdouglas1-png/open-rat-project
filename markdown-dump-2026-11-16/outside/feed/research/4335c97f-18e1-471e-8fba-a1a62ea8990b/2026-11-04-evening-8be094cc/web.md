## ICU Candida infections killed 54.6% within 28 days
Among ICU patients with [Candida bloodstream infections](https://www.mdpi.com/2077-0383/15/21/8597), 54.6% died within 28 days. Patients presenting with severe sepsis or septic shock faced a sharply higher risk of dying within 28 days.
