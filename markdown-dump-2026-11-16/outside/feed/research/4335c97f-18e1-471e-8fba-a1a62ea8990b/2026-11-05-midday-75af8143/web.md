## Early measles vaccine could help babies as Wisconsin outbreak grows
[State health officials are recommending](https://www.jsonline.com/story/news/health/2026/11/04/wisconsin-health-officials-urge-early-measles-vaccine-for-some-babies/92613112007/) infants 6 months and older who are likely to be exposed to measles receive the measles, mumps and rubella vaccine early. An infant who gets that early dose still needs two more doses to count as fully vaccinated.
