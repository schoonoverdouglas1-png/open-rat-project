## Capsular switching found in Arabian Gulf pneumococci
In a study published September 3, 2026, [whole-genome sequencing of 250 pneumococcal isolates from the United Arab Emirates and Kuwait](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cellular-and-infection-microbiology/articles/10.3389/fcimb.2026.1832677/full) found 51 capsular serotypes, with serotype 3 the most common. Five sequence types each carried two distinct serotypes, consistent with capsule switching within the same lineage.
