## Doctors urge early flu shots as mutated H1N1 rises
Positive flu tests reported to the CDC [hit a 10-year high for this time of year](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/early-us-flu-season-current-strain-may-signal-risk-younger-people-doctors-urge-2026-10-20/), driven by an early surge on the West Coast. The season's dominant H1N1 carries a new G155E mutation that slips past last year's antibodies, hardest in teens and young adults.
