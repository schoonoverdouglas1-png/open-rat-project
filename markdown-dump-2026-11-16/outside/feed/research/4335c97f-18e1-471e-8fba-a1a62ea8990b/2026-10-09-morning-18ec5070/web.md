## Cladophialophora bantiana detections rose fourfold in US
In its October 2 [report](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/75/wr/mm7540e1.htm), a University of Washington reference laboratory counted 48 confirmed cases and one probable case of Cladophialophora bantiana in the United States from January 2009 to July 2026, most of them in brain tissue. CDC authors say systematic reporting is still needed to explain the recent increase.
