## More than half of Novi Sad isolates were non-PCV20 serotypes
In Novi Sad, [27.0% of 3,373 children aged 2 to 60 months carried pneumococci](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.11.26367886v1) in surveillance from October 2022 to September 2026. PCV20's theoretical coverage was 47.3%, leaving more than half of typeable isolates outside it.
