## Cancer vaccine combos top 1-year survival in second-line NSCLC
In the phase 2 Combi-TED trial, the off-the-shelf therapeutic cancer vaccine OSE2101 (Tedopi), combined with either docetaxel or nivolumab, [hit its primary endpoint of one-year overall survival](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/cancer-vaccine-combinations-improve-1-year-survival-in-second-line-nsclc), nearly doubling the rate versus docetaxel alone. The phase 3 registrational ARTEMIA trial of Tedopi monotherapy is already recruiting, with results expected in early 2028.
