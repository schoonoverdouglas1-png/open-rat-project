## Dengue virus leaves DNA traces in mosquitoes
After infection with dengue virus serotype 1, two widely used Aedes mosquito cell lines [produced detectable DNA copies of the viral genome](https://scienmag.com/dengue-virus-leaves-dna-traces-and-a-rare-genomic-integration-in-mosquito-cells/) in a study reported October 29. One line also yielded a single chimeric sequence joining a fragment of viral DNA to mosquito genomic DNA.
