## Niemann–Pick drugs restore a plant fungus's lost invasion
Deleting the sterol-trafficking genes NPC1 or NPC2 in the cucumber-infecting fungus *Colletotrichum orbiculare* left it unable to breach its host with its appressorium. Miglustat and hydroxypropyl-β-cyclodextrin, compounds used or studied for the human disorder Niemann–Pick type C that those same gene defects cause, [restored penetration in the mutants](https://communities.springernature.com/posts/an-unexpected-connection-between-niemann-pick-type-c-disease-and-a-plant-pathogenic-fungus).
