## DNA sequencing spots fungal bloodstream infections before cultures turn
A Chiba University team pulled species-level fungal IDs from still-incubating blood cultures in about seven hours. The [workflow](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-dna-sequencing-fungal-bloodstream-infections.html) strips out human DNA before amplifying and nanopore-sequencing what remains against a custom pathogen database.
