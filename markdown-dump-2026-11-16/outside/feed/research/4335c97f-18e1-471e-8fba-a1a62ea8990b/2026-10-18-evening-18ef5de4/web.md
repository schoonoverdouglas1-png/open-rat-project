## Enzyme lets wheat bunt fungus slip past plant defenses
Researchers at the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences [identified TlCDA](https://scienmag.com/fungal-enzyme-strips-chitin-disguise-to-let-wheat-bunt-pathogen-slip-past-plant-defenses/), the chitin deacetylase that the wheat bunt fungus uses to turn chitin into chitosan. Deleting the gene suppressed both the growth and the pathogenicity of the fungus, promoting the enzyme from a correlated virulence factor to a demonstrated requirement for full disease.
