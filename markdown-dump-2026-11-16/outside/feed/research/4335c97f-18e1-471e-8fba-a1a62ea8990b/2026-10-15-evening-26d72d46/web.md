## Tolypocladium split: new genus, new species, new names
Five genes' worth of DNA have redrawn the map of Tolypocladium, a fungus genus taxonomists long treated as one natural group. The [MycoKeys revision](https://doi.org/10.3897/mycokeys.141.204222) erects a new genus, Tolypocladiopsis, for the misplaced Tolypocladium extinguens.
