## Model tracks each pneumococcal serotype separately
A University of Melbourne team built an [agent-based model that tracks pneumococcal carriage across individual serotypes](https://bioengineer.org/agent-based-model-tracks-multi-serotype-pneumococcal-carriage-and-vaccine-strategy-impacts/) without relying on aggregate serotype groupings. The break matters because newer vaccines are not nested supersets of earlier ones, so vaccine-versus-non-vaccine groupings no longer fit them.
