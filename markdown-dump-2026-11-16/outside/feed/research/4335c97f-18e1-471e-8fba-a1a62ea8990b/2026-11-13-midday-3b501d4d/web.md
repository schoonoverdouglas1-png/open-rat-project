## Don't install the November Pixel Drop yet
Tech Advisor on November 13 [urged Pixel owners to hold off on the November Pixel Drop](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/3284801/psa-dont-install-google-november-pixel-drop-yet.html) after early adopters reported bugs within hours of installing it. The most concerning report is cameras completely freezing, with no viewfinder available in the main Camera app or in third-party apps.
