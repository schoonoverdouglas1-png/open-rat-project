## Wisconsin reports second measles-associated death
A second Grant County child has [died in connection to Wisconsin's worsening measles outbreak](https://www.jsonline.com/story/news/health/2026/11/13/second-measles-associated-death-reported-in-grant-county-wisconsin/92688780007/). As of November 13, the state has seen 651 measles cases, according to state and local health departments.
