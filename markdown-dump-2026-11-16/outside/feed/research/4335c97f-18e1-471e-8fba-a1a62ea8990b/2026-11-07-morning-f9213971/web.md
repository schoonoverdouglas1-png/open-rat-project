## Resistant Trichophyton indotineae spills into companion animals
A [study published November 5, 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2026.1974916/full) found every human isolate was terbinafine-resistant Trichophyton indotineae. Resistance-linked SQLE mutations also turned up in animal isolates, including cats, pointing to spillover of human-adapted lineages into companion animals.
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2026.1974916/full (published 2026-11-05)
