## O-acetylation splits 15A capsule into four variants
A paper published November 10 found [four distinct serogroup 15 capsule structures](https://academic.oup.com/jid/advance-article/doi/10.1093/infdis/jiag551/9026193) differing by their O-acetylation pattern. Available serotyping antisera left 15F indistinguishable from 15D and 15z indistinguishable from 15A.
