## Deleting one protein disarms a deadly crop fungus
A Zhejiang University team [deleted the Lsm8 protein](https://scienmag.com/fungal-killers-achilles-heel-found-in-rna-splicing-machinery/) from the crop-killing mold Fusarium graminearum, and its growth, virulence and toxin production all collapsed at once. In Candida albicans, the human fungal pathogen, Lsm loss already impairs pathogenicity.
