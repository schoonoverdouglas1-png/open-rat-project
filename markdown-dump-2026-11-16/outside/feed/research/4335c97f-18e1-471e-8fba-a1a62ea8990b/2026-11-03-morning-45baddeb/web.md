## Chippewa County measles cases reach 33 as local spread is confirmed
The county's count roughly tripled in about 17 days, from 11 confirmed cases on October 13 to 33 on October 30. That shift from travel-linked cases to [local spread](https://www.medicaldaily.com/chippewa-county-measles-local-spread-33-cases-480684) means a case can now be caught from someone in the community, with no trip involved.
