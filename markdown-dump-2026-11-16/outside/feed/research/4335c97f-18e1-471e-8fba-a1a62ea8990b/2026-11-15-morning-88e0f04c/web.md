## Over 580,000 young people still to get first MenB dose
Figures up to 31 October 2026 show [just 28.2% of 18 and 19 year olds have had a first dose](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/time-running-out-for-over-580000-young-people-to-get-1st-dose-of-menb). Eligible 18 and 19 year olds and first-year students have until 31 December 2026 to get that first dose under the one-off offer.
