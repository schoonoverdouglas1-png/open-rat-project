## Fatal Powassan infection spread beyond the brain in transplant patient
A 38-year-old liver transplant recipient in Pennsylvania died after [Powassan virus lineage 2](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/32/12/26-0193_article) was confirmed. Postmortem tests found the virus in her brain, lungs, and kidneys, showing it can infect tissue well beyond the nervous system.
