## How Andes hantavirus grips human cells
Cryo-electron microscopy at near-atomic resolution caught the Andes hantavirus spike [locking the human receptor protein inside a deep pocket](https://cen.acs.org/biological-chemistry/andes-hantavirus-spike-pocket-binds-to-receptor/104/web/2026/10). For drug designers, the structure could be a starting point for treatments against Andes virus and other New World hantaviruses, including North America's Sin Nombre virus.
