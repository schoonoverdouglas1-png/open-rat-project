## Antifungals in pregnancy linked to altered hormone levels in babies
Across 589 Danish mother–child pairs, children whose mothers used azole antifungals during pregnancy showed altered adrenal hormone levels at three months, according to [the new study](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-antifungal-pregnancy-link-hormone-babies.html). The medicines were mostly over-the-counter creams and vaginal tablets, long treated as safe during pregnancy.
