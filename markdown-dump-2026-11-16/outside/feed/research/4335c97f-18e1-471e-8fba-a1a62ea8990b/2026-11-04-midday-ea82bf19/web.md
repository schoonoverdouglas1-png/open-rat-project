## Acellular pertussis vaccines show strong early protection, then wane
[Three-component or higher acellular vaccines](https://scienmag.com/acellular-pertussis-vaccines-show-strong-early-protection-but-waning-immunity/) showed efficacy of approximately 84–85% against typical childhood pertussis. The review stresses that comparisons should not rest on component number alone because the pertussis toxin detoxification method differs between products.
