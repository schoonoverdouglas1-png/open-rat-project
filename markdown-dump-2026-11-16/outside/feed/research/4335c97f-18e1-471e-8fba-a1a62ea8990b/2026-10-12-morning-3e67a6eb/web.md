## Two fungal strains slash pepper blight by 90 percent
In greenhouse pots, a paired application of two Trichoderma harzianum strains (T891 and T58) [cut red pepper blight incidence by 90.9 percent](https://scienmag.com/fungal-duo-slashes-pepper-blight-by-90-percent-while-rewiring-soil-microbiome/) against pathogen-infected controls. The advantage of the pairing ran through the soil: it recruited protective Sphingomonas bacteria into the root zone, an ally that showed up only when both strains were present.
