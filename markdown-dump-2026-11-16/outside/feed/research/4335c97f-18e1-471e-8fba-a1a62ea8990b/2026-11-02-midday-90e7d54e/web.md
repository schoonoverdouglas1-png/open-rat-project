## Singapore approves CAPVAXIVE for adults 18 and older
Singapore's Health Sciences Authority has [approved MSD's CAPVAXIVE for adults aged 18 and older](https://www.newsflashasia.com/capvaxive-approval-in-singapore-targets-rising-pneumococcal-threat/). Its 21-serotype design answers the shifting serotype mix behind adult invasive pneumococcal disease.
