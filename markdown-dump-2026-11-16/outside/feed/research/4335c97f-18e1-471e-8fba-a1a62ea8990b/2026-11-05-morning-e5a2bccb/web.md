## Beijing pertussis shifted to vaccinated 5- to 9-year-olds
[A study of pertussis in Beijing's Xicheng District](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12879-026-14772-8) found cases shifting from infants to children aged 5 to 9, who made up 67.8% of cases in 2024. Among 126 cases in that age group, 98.4% had received four valid doses.
