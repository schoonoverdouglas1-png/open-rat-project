## Tribal leaders oppose proposed Census changes
Tribal nations and Native organizations have until today, Monday, November 2, to [comment on the proposed Census Bureau rule](https://www.genocidewatch.com/single-post/tribal-leaders-oppose-proposed-census-changes-warn-of-harm-to-native-communities). The National Congress of American Indians said the bureau gave only two weeks' notice for its October 2 consultation, rather than its standard 30 days.
