## Fat-bubble phage system targets drug-resistant bacteria
In mice infected with Pseudomonas aeruginosa, a [fat-bubble phage system](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aeh7105) cut gut bacterial load about 1,000-fold versus the antibiotic alone. The package is doubly targeted, so it steers to the germ and slips inside infected cells where bacteria shelter.
