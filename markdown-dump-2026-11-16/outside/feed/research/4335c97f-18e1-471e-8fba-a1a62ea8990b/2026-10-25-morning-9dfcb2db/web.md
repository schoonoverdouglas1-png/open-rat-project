## Experimental E. coli vaccine discontinued after ineffective results
The [E.mbrace trial](https://healthandfamily.in/when-antibodies-arent-enough-large-global-trial-of-experimental-e-coli-vaccine-discontinued-following-ineffective-results/) tested the nine-valent ExPEC9V vaccine in more than 17,000 adults aged 60 and older across 20 countries. Despite robust and sustained antibody responses, it recorded 11 invasive cases in the vaccine group against 13 in the placebo group.
