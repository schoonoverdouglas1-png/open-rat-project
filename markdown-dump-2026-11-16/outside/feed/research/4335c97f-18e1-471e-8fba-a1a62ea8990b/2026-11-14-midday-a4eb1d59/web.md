## Pneumococcal meningitis with thrombotic microangiopathy in a child
Serotyping in the [case report published November 5](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pediatrics/articles/10.3389/fped.2026.1870281/full) identified the meningitis isolate as 22F, a type not included in the PCV13 vaccine the boy had received. The authors say early recognition of pneumococcal hemolytic uremic syndrome is crucial because its treatment differs from other thrombotic microangiopathies.
