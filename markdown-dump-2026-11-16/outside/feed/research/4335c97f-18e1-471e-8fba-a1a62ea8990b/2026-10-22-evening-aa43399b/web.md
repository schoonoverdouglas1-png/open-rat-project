## SCY-247 clears Phase 1
SCYNEXIS announced on Oct 22 that its second-generation triterpenoid antifungal SCY-247 [cleared a Phase 1 study of the intravenous formulation](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/22/3386244/0/en/scynexis-completes-phase-1-study-of-intravenous-scy-247-a-novel-triterpenoid-antifungal-in-development-for-treatment-and-prevention-of-invasive-fungal-diseases.html), which was generally well tolerated with no drug-related serious adverse events. Phase 2 studies of both the oral and IV formulations are expected to begin in 2027, pending agreement with the FDA.
