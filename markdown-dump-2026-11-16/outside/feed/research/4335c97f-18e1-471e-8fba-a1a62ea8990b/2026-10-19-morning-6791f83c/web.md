## New family Funiliomycetaceae split from 145-year-old Dactylaria
[Multi-gene sequencing](https://scienmag.com/fungal-family-tree-rewritten-as-new-family-funiliomycetaceae-emerges-from-a-145-year-old-genus/) broke apart Dactylaria, a genus in use since 1880. Its one coherent lineage is now Funiliomycetaceae, with twelve species united in Funiliomyces.
