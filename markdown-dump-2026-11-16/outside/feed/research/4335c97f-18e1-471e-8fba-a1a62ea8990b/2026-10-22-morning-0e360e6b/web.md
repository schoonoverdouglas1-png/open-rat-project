## C. difficile is a two-kingdom ecosystem
Infected guts were enriched for [transcriptionally active *Candida albicans* and *Nakaseomyces glabratus*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-74319-4). The study sequenced both the metagenome and the metatranscriptome, so the fungi show up as live players in the infection rather than a static census.
