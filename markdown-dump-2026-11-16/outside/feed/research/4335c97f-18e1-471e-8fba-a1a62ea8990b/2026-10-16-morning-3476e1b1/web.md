## Google's Willow cuts quantum errors 40x with magic states
Google hit a record 99.99% logical fidelity for T-states on its Willow chip, in results published in Science this week ([Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/googles-willow-processor-cuts-quantum-errors-40x-with-magic-states/)). The trick works like a filter: a noisy state goes into a small patch of the chip, rounds of measurements check it for errors, and every flawed attempt gets thrown away.
