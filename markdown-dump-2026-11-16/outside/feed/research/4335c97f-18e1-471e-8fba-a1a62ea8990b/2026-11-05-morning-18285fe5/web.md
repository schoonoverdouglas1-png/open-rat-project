## GB-79, a new antifungal scaffold against drug-resistant Candida
[GB-79](https://pubs.acs.org/aidcbc/article/doi/10.1021/acsinfecdis.6c00901/5464750/GB-79-Leelamine-Isonitrile-Defines-a-New) outperformed fluconazole in lab tests against drug-resistant Candida isolates. In infected mice, it still reduced kidney fungal burden despite poor oral bioavailability.
