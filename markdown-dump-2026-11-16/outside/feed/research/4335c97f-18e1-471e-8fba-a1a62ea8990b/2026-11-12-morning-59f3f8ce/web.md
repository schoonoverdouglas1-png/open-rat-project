## Three new holiday trolley tours join the Chippewa River lineup
Chippewa River Trolley Co. has launched [three new holiday trolley tours](https://volumeone.org/articles/2026/11/12/408296-ding-ding-three-new-holiday-trolley-tours-join-the-fray-chippewa-river-trolley-co) for the season in the Chippewa Valley. The Christmas Tree Trolley runs two-and-a-half hours on the weekend after Thanksgiving and the first weekend of December.
