## Candida auris's 'Crazy-one' gene helps it dodge antifungals
The fastest-growing survivors carried a [spread of mutations in CRZ1](https://news.exeter.ac.uk/faculty-of-health-and-life-sciences/unexpected-discovery-of-new-mechanism-which-helps-deadly-fungus-candida-auris-evade-treatment/), the 'Crazy-one' gene that governs cell-wall synthesis.
