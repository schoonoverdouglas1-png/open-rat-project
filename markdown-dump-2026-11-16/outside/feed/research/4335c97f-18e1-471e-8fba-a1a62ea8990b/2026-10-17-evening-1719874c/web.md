## OVX836 misses 50% efficacy goal, advances as combination vaccine
The nucleoprotein-based shot posted 24% efficacy in 2,850 adults, missing statistical significance in its first large field trial. [Osivax](https://osivax.com/osivax-reports-phase-2b-results-for-ovx836-and-advances-combination-strategy-for-next-generation-seasonal-influenza-vaccines/) is now betting it works best paired with conventional seasonal shots, through deals with GC Biopharma and another global partner.
