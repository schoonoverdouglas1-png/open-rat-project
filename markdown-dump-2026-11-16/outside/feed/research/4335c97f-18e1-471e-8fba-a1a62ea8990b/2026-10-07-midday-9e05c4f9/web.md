## A rare Chinese tree's fate may rest on its fungal partners
Researchers [mapped the arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi](https://bioengineer.org/hidden-fungal-partners-may-decide-the-fate-of-a-rare-chinese-tree/) living in and around the roots of Heptacodium miconioides, the endangered seven-son flower, across five fragmented populations in eastern China. Most of those communities were assembled by chance rather than by environmental selection.
