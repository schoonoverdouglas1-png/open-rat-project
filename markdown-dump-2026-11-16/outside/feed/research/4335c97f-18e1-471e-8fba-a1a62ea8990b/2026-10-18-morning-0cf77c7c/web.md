## Cryptococcus walls remodel differently without chitosan
Michigan State and Duke teams used solid-state NMR and electron microscopy to compare wild-type Cryptococcus neoformans and Cryptococcus gattii against their avirulent chitosan-deficient mutants, the strains [studied as vaccine candidates](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-78940-9). The two species rebuilt differently: C. neoformans raised α-(1→3)-glucan and cut β-glucan, while C. gattii lost one α-(1→3)-glucan subtype and kept β-glucan steady.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-78940-9 (published 2026-10-18)
