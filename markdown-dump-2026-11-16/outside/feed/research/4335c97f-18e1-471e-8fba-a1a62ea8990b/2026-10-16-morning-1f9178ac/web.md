## WHO: tuberculosis funding cuts put millions at risk
Roughly $200–250 million a year in U.S. bilateral funding for tuberculosis is gone, and [the WHO says](https://www.theeducatedpatient.com/view/who-warns-tuberculosis-funding-cuts-put-millions-at-risk) programs in 18 high-burden countries are now at risk, with Africa hit hardest. The agency says drug supply chains and laboratory diagnostics are already collapsing, with thousands of health workers laid off.
