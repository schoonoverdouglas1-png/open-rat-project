## VAX-31 could cover 95% of adult invasive pneumococcal disease
In the pivotal OPUS-1 trial, [VAX-31 met all of its primary immunogenicity endpoints](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/phase-3-opus-program-evaluates-31-valent-pneumococcal-conjugate-vaccine-candidate-in-adults) against Prevnar 20 and Capvaxive. It uses site-specific conjugation technology intended to let more serotypes in while maintaining functional antibody responses.
