## Mysterious millipede identified nearly 90 years after first find
A millipede first collected in South Australia in 1936 has finally been identified as the new species [Colobothrix deckeri](https://scitechdaily.com/scientists-finally-identify-mysterious-creature-nearly-90-years-after-it-was-first-found/). Colobothrix means “stunted hair” for the short, blunt rod replacing the usual long bristle on mature males.
