## New antibiotic class discovered for first time in decades
[McMaster and University of Illinois Chicago researchers](https://www.agedcarefind.com.au/news/new-class-of-antibiotics-discovered-for-first-time-in-decades/33376) found lariocidin, a lasso peptide from backyard soil bacteria, effective against drug-resistant bacteria. It binds a ribosome site no known antibiotic touches, stopping the cell from making proteins.
