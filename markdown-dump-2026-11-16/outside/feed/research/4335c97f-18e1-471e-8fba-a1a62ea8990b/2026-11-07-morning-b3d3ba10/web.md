## Pneumococcal enzyme starts polyamine synthesis with agmatine
The Fort Lewis team [found the pneumococcal enzyme prefers agmatine over putrescine by about 3,200-fold](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-0817/15/11/1155), so it is a CAPADH rather than a CASDH. Disrupting the agmatine supply cuts capsular polysaccharide production, tying the corrected pathway to the capsule behind serotype virulence.
