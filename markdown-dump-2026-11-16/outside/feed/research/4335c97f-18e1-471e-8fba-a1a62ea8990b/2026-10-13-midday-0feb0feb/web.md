## New compound beats chlorhexidine on C. auris skin in mice
AVA-003, a stabilized hypochlorous-acid chemistry modeled on the immune system's own defenses, cut C. auris skin burden roughly 500-fold more than 2% chlorhexidine after a 3-day course in a mouse model ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261013694415/en/AvantGuard-Outperforms-Chlorhexidine-in-Candida-auris-Skin-Decolonization-Mouse-Model)). The independent Case Western study, published in Antimicrobial Agents and Chemotherapy, found it fungicidal against all four major C. auris clades.
