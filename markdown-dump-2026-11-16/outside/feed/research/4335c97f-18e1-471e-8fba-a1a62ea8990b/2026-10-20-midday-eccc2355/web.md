## WSU team finds switch to crank up antiviral defenses
Adding recombinant ISG15 made cells significantly more resistant to RSV in the WSU study ([WSU](https://news.wsu.edu/press-release/2026/10/20/wsu-researchers-discover-way-to-crank-up-antiviral-defenses/)). The pathway primes neighboring cells through the host rather than hitting the virus itself, so mutation has less to grab onto.
