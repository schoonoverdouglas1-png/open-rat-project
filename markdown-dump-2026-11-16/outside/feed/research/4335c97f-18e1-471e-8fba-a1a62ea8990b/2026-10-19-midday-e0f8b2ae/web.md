## Rezzayo wins FDA priority review for fungal-disease prevention
The FDA accepted CorMedix's application to use [Rezzayo](https://www.wcia.com/business/press-releases/globenewswire/9847698/cormedix-announces-fda-acceptance-for-priority-review-of-supplemental-nda-for-rezzayo-rezafungin-for-prophylaxis-of-invasive-fungal-diseases/) (rezafungin) to prevent invasive fungal disease, with a decision due in Q1 2027. The case rests on the Phase III ReSPECT trial, which tested once-weekly dosing against the standard preventive regimen in adults receiving allogeneic stem-cell transplants.
