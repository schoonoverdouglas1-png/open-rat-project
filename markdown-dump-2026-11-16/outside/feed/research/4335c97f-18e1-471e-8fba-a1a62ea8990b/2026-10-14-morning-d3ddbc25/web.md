## Molecular advances in chromoblastomycosis haven't reached the clinic
A [review published October 13 in PLOS Neglected Tropical Diseases](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pntd.0014801) finds that DNA sequencing has steadily expanded the roster of fungi behind chromoblastomycosis, a chronic skin infection of farmworkers in the tropics. It flags imiquimod, a topical immune-response modifier, as the most promising add-on to systemic antifungals, in a disease where no randomized trial underpins any treatment choice.
