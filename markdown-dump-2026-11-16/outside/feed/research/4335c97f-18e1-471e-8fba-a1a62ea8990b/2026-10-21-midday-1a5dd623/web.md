## Olorofim becomes first new-class antifungal to clear Phase 3
In the OASIS trial, oral olorofim [matched intravenous AmBisome on Day-42 all-cause mortality](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/drug-development/antifungal-new-class-f2g-shionogi-olorofim-trial-fda/104/web/2026/10), 23.8% versus 24.3%, in invasive aspergillosis patients for whom azole therapy was off the table. F2G will [resubmit its FDA new-drug application](https://international-biopharma.com/shionogi-and-f2g-report-data-from-global-phase-3-oasis-study-evaluating-investigational-oral-olorofim-versus-ambisome-followed-by-standard-of-care-in-invasive-aspergillosis-in-late-breaking-oral/) by the end of 2026.
