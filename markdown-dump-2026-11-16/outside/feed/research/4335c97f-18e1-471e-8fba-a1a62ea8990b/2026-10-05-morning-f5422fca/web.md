## Antifungal therapy reshapes gut microbiota in IBD
In 53 IBD patients with mild oral thrush, [fluconazole](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41575-026-01268-8) cut gut Candida overgrowth where swish-and-spit nystatin did not. The shifts held over eight weeks and tracked with lower risk of disease progression.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41575-026-01268-8 (published 2026-10-05)
