## New tapejarid pterosaur from the English Cretaceous
A partial skull roof first described in 1870 now stands as [Hintighernia edingerae](https://tetzoo.com/blog/2026/10/31/1tapejarid-pterosaur-from-cambridge-greensand). For the English animal, Hintighernia gives Tapejara's “lord of the path” a Proto-Brittonic form.
