## India pneumococcal map puts 14, 19F and 6B on top in children
The [meta-analysis of Indian studies from 2007 to 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12879-026-14389-x) pooled Streptococcus pneumoniae isolates across invasive disease, carriage and pneumonia. In adults with invasive disease, serotype 1 led at 11.69%.
