## The resistant ringworm spreading worldwide
A review in [Frontiers in Tropical Diseases](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/tropical-diseases/articles/10.3389/fitd.2026.1960706/full) traces how *Trichophyton indotineae*, carved out of the *T. mentagrophytes* complex as a distinct species in 2020 on genetic evidence alone, grew from a South Asian outbreak strain into a pathogen now reported across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and the Asia-Pacific. The review puts terbinafine resistance at 78.9% to 82.5% of isolates tested, driven by point mutations in the fungal squalene epoxidase gene.
