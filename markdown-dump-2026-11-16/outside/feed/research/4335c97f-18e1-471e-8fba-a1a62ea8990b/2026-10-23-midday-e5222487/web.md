## Starting HPV vaccine at 9–10 maximizes protection
By age 13, 61.6% of children who began HPV vaccination at ages 9–10 completed the series, versus 45.1% of those who began at 11–12. Early initiation carried [an adjusted hazard ratio of 2.85 for completion](https://www.emjreviews.com/microbiology-infectious-diseases/news/hpv-vaccine-initiation-at-ages-9-10-maximizes-protection-idweek-2026/) in the IDWeek 2026 trial.
