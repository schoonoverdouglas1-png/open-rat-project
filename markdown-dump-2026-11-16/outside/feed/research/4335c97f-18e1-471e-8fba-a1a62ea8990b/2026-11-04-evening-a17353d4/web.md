## First local chikungunya spread by tiger mosquitoes in Switzerland
The four Ticino patients [had not recently travelled](https://www.dailyalpine.com/43490/tiger-mosquitoes-transmit-chikungunya-locally-in-switzerland-for-first-time/) to a country where the virus circulates. Local transmission can occur when an infected traveller is bitten during the infectious period by a tiger mosquito, which can then pass the virus to others.
