## MenACWY-TT boosts responses against hypervirulent meningococcal strains
In infants given two doses at 2 and 4 months, [seroprotection rates favored MenACWY-TT](https://www.infectiousdiseaseadvisor.com/reports/menacwy-tt-vaccine-immunogenicity-hypervirulent-meningococcal-strains/) for both ST-1466 isolates. The comparison rested on laboratory antibody measures 30 days after the second dose, not on clinical outcomes against invasive disease.
