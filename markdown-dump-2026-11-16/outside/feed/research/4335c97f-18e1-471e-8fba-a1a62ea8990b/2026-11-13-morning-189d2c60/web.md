## Bexsero backed for one-dose MenB booster from age 10
Europe's CHMP has [recommended updating the Bexsero label](https://www.gsk.com/en-gb/media/press-releases/bexsero-4cmenb-receives-positive-chmp-opinion-for-one-dose-meningitis-b-booster-for-10plus-year-olds/) to include a single booster dose for people aged 10 and older vaccinated at least two years earlier. The recommendation rests on data showing immune memory from vaccination in infancy can be boosted with one dose up to 19 years later.
