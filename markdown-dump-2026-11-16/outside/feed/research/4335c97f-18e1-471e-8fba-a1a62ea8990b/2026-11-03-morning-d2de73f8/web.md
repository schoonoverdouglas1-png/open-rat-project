## Russia registers second meningococcal group B vaccine
On October 30, Russia's Health Ministry [registered Pfizer's recombinant vaccine Trumenba](https://gxpnews.net/en/2026/10/russias-health-ministry-registers-second-vaccine-against-meningococcal-group-b/) against serogroup B meningococcal disease. It contains two recombinant protein antigens from serogroup B meningococcus.
