## CDC names four most dangerous superbugs in the US
The [CDC's October 28 report](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-cdc-dangerous-superbugs.html) put drug-resistant superbugs at an estimated 50,800 deaths a year in the United States. Candida auris is among the four urgent threats, a fungus that resists most antifungal drugs.
