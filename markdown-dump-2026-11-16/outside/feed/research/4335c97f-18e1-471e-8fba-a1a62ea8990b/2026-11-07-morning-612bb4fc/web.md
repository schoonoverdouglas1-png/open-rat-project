## A wider infant pneumococcal shot would lose health in the Netherlands
[Replacing PCV15 with PCV20 for Dutch infants](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-026-05378-7) is projected to lose 3,476 quality-adjusted life-years over the modeled decade. The loss came from older adults, where indirect effects shifted disease toward serotypes PCV20 does not cover.
- https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-026-05378-7 (published 2026-11-06)
