## West Nile brain cases rise in parts of the US
[More than 880 people](https://medium.com/stacker-news/when-west-nile-virus-attacks-the-brain-severe-cases-rise-in-parts-of-the-us-8941d0a14b9e) across the United States have developed severe, neuroinvasive West Nile virus infection in 2026, tracking above the country's 10-year average. There is no treatment known to be effective, so patients receive supportive care.
