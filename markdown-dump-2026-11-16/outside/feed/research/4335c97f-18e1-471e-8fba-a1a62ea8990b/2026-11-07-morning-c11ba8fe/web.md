## Pfizer's 25-valent infant vaccine posts strong Phase 2 data
In results reported October 30, the investigational 25-valent pneumococcal vaccine [produced serotype 3 antibody levels about 15-fold higher than Prevnar 20](https://hubs.li/Q04Dq7Md0) after the fourth infant dose. Serotype 3's thick capsular membrane and mucoid properties have historically blunted vaccine-induced immunity, letting invasive disease persist despite widespread vaccination.
