## New trapdoor spider lives in tidal rock tubes off Mauritius
Researchers [poured seawater over the nests for around 20 seconds](https://wildlife-watchers.com/2026/11/02/trapdoor-spider-discovered-mauritius-islets/), then opened the doors and found no water inside. The spider's name combines the Latin mare (sea) and vallum (wall), giving “sea wall”.
