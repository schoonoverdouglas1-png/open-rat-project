## Serotype 3 led adult pneumococcal cases in Türkiye
A [two-year study from Türkiye](https://bioengineer.org/pneumococcal-resistance-runs-high-in-turkiye-with-serotype-3-leading-in-adults/) published October 29, 2026 found multidrug resistance widespread among pneumococcal isolates. Serotype 3 was the most frequent type identified among adults, at 21.6%.
