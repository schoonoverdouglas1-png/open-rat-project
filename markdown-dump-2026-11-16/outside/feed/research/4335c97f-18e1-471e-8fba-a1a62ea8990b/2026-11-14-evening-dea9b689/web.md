## Meningococcal carriage reached 93.75% in boarding students
Among 60 new entrants at a boarding martial arts school in Dengfeng City, [meningococcal carriage prevalence ranged from 30.00% to 93.75%](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1941998/full) over 13 follow-up visits in a study published October 22, 2026. Of the positive carriers, 35.59% carried at least two serogroups over time.
