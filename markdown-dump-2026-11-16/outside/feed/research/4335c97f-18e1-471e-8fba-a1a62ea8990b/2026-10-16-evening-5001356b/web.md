## Giant fungus found in China gets a new species, genus and family
Researchers from the Kunming Institute of Botany described Dianjunus rex from Yunnan forests, where its pale upright fruiting bodies reach 50 centimeters tall ([Knowridge](https://knowridge.com/2026/10/scientists-discover-a-giant-forest-fungus-with-an-ancient-family-history/)). Its DNA justified a species, a genus and a whole new family, Dianjunaceae, whose lineage a molecular clock traces to about 65 million years ago, roughly a million years after the extinction that ended the age of non-bird dinosaurs.
