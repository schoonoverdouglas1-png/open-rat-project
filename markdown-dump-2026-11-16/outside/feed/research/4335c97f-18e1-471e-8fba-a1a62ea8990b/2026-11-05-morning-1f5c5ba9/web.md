## Flu, pneumococcal and shingles shots showed no extra serious risk
The [study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1859470/full) compared paired flu, PCV20 and shingles vaccination with each shot alone using VAERS reports from adults aged 50 and older. Serious adverse event rates in the paired groups ran 3.7% to 5.5%, comparable to or lower than the single-shot groups.
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1859470/full (published 2026-11-05)
