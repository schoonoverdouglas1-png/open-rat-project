## OpenSSH sshole flaw fixed in 10.6p2
On musl Linux without PAM, [an overlong password can log in to password-less accounts](https://brandefense.io/security-news/openssh-sshole-cve-2026-123158/) through OpenSSH's sshd. OpenSSH 10.6p2, released November 5, 2026, fixes the flaw.
