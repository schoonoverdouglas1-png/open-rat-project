## MenX polysaccharide qualifies for multivalent meningococcal vaccines
In a study published October 8, 2026, MenX polysaccharide [qualified as an antigen](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1943863/full) for multivalent meningococcal conjugate vaccines. Its tetanus toxoid conjugate elicited potent complement-dependent serum bactericidal activity in vivo.
