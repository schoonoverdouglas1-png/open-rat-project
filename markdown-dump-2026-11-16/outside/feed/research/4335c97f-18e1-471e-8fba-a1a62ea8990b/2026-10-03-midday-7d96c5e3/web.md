## Scientists strip deadly fungus defenses, turning static drug lethal
Removing the septin gene aspB [turned caspofungin from a growth-staller into a killer](https://scienmag.com/scientists-strip-deadly-fungus-of-its-defenses-turning-a-static-drug-into-a-killer/) against Aspergillus fumigatus in lab and animal tests. Invasive aspergillosis still kills 30% to 90% of treated patients.
