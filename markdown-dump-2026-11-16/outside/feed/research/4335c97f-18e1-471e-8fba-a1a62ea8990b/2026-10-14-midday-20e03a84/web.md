## Fungus turns plant sugar into a bacterial recruitment bonus
A [study published in Microbiome](https://bioengineer.org/fungus-turns-plant-sugar-into-a-microbial-recruitment-tool-to-boost-roots/) traces the whole partnership to one transporter: Gongronella butleri strain w5 imports sucrose from tomato roots via GspSUT1, splits it into glucose and fructose, and leaks the hexoses into the soil, enriching auxin-producing Bacillus megaterium and B. zanthoxyli. When the researchers silenced GspSUT1, the hexose flow stopped, the bacterial partners faded, and the tomatoes' growth advantage collapsed.
