## Pan-drug-resistant Candidozyma auris in Kenya: second case on record
It came from a 28-year-old inpatient's hospital-acquired surgical-site infection in Kenya, documented in a [study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-70653-9) published September 29. Genome sequencing traced the resistance to mutations in the ERG11, CDR1, FKS1 and FUR1 genes.
