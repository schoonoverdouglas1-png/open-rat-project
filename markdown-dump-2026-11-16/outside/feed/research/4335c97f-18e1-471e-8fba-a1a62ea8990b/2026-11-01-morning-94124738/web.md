## Lyme vaccine protection came only after four doses
Pfizer and Valneva's [Lyme vaccine candidate](https://www.medicaldaily.com/lyme-vaccine-efficacy-four-doses-phase-three-trial-480588) was 73.2% effective counting from 28 days after the fourth dose, in a second tick season. The trial still narrowly missed its main statistical target.
