## Terbinafine kept potency in MOB-015 nail-fungus analysis
Among MOB-015-treated patients with evaluable end-of-study results, 74.3% reached mycological cure across the full range of baseline terbinafine susceptibility, according to the [new Journal of Fungi analysis](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-analyses-on-terbinafine-susceptibility-and-mob-015-published-in-journal-of-fungi-302898140.html). The work re-analyzed isolates from the completed North American Phase 3 study in collaboration with Prof. Mahmoud Ghannoum at Case Western Reserve.
