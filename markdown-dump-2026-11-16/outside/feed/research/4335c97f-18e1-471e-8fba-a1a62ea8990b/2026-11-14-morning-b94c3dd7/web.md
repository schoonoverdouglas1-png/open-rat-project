## Eau Claire home sales fell 8.4% this fall
Across Altoona, Eau Claire and Chippewa Falls, [new listings rose 11.1% this fall](https://www.homcentric.com/blog/eau-claire-housing-market-update-november-2026) from the same stretch last year. The median existing-home price through October was $324,900, up 5.2% from the same period in 2025.
