## Pixel bug unhides Private Space when the theme changes
Switching between light and dark mode [makes a hidden Private Space reappear in the app drawer](https://techprolonged.com/2026/11/pixel-bug-unhides-your-hidden-private-space-when-the-theme-changes/). Owners who rely on it can leave the theme on one setting and switch off scheduled dark mode, which removes the trigger.
