## Pneumococcal genomes recovered from Gambian children with pneumonia
Published November 12, [genome-resolved shotgun metagenomics](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s41598-026-80265-y) recovered 11 Streptococcus pneumoniae genomes from children in The Gambia with severe or very severe pneumonia. Blood and nasopharyngeal samples showed a mean species-level Jaccard similarity of 58%.
