## FDA approves first generic lenacapavir for HIV prevention
The [FDA approved the first generic version of lenacapavir](https://healthpolicy-watch.news/fda-approves-first-generic-of-hiv-prevention-drug-lenacapavir/), the twice-yearly injection to prevent HIV. The generic is projected to cost $40 a year for the two annual injections.
