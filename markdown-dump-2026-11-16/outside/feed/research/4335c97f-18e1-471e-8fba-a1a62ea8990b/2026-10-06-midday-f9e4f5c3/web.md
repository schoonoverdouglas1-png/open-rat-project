## Light therapy clears severe nail fungus
McMaster researchers treated 165 great toenails, nearly all severely infected, with photodisinfection, a dye-and-red-light routine. After six months of biweekly sessions, nearly three-quarters of the nails grew out clear and healthy, according to [the study published today](https://www.mdpi.com/1930-8264/116/5/79).
