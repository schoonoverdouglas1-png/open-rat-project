## Bacterial meningitis burden mapped in Central America
A [first subregional profile published today](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1941910/full) found Streptococcus pneumoniae predominated among Costa Rica's culture-confirmed invasive isolates. In Honduras, 75.3% of hospital discharges were coded as unspecified bacterial meningitis, a laboratory confirmation deficit the authors name directly.
