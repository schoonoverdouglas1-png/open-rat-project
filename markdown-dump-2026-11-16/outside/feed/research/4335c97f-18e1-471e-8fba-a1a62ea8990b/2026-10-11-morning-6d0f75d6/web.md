## Less biofilm, better hiding: a fungal trade-off
In work summarized by [PLOS Biology on September 29](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3004027), fluconazole-resistant outbreak isolates of *Candida parapsilosis* that make little biofilm masked their cell-wall β-glucan beneath extra mannan, and that masking left them less exposed to killing by innate immune cells. The same low-biofilm strains survived better in a mouse candidiasis model, especially in immune cell-rich organs, which undercuts the textbook view of biofilms as the key to fungal persistence.
