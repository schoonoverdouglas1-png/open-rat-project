## U.S. meningococcal cases hit 463 in 2025, second-most since 2013
[Serogroup Y drives much of the recent increase](https://www.cdc.gov/meningococcal/php/surveillance/index.html), which has climbed sharply since 2021 past pre-pandemic levels. Since 2019, CDC has also detected penicillin- and ciprofloxacin-resistant serogroup Y isolates in the United States.
