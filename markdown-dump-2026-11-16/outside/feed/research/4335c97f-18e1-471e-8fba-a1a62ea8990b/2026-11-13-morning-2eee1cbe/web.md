## Dependabot alerts will pause on inactive repositories
Starting December 8, 2026, GitHub will [turn off Dependabot alerts on most repositories with no activity in the past year](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-10-dependabot-alerts-will-pause-on-inactive-repositories/). Any push to the repository automatically turns alerts back on.
