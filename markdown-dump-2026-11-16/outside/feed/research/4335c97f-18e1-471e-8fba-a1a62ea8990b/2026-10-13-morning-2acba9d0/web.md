## Fungal sterol's double game shapes termite defenses
Thirteen Metarhizium isolates collected near termite foraging sites in five US states showed spore ergosterol rising with lethality to Reticulitermes flavipes in [The Science of Nature](https://scienmag.com/ergosterols-double-game-how-a-fungal-membrane-molecule-shapes-termite-immunity/). The Towson team reads it as a trade-off in which an ergosterol-rich membrane that mutes alarm shaking and allogrooming also leaves spores more exposed to the antifungal peptide termicin.
