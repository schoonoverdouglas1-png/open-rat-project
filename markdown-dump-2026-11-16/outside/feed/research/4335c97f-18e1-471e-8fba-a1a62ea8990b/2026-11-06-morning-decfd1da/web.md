## Heat cuts fungal biofilm on metal implants
On October 30, Solenic reported that [heating Candida on stainless steel rings to 60–70°C](https://orthotw.com/2026/10/solenic-medical-presents-idweek-2026-data-demonstrating-thermal-technology-s-ability-to-reduce-fungal-biofilm-on-metal-implants) cut viable fungal burden by multi-log amounts. In seven-day biofilms, 65°C heat plus antifungal therapy over three days reduced burden more than either treatment alone.
