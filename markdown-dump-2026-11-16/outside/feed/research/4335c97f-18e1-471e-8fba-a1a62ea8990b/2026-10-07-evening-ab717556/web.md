## Urine Aspergillus test's FDA-clearance studies published
Pearl Diagnostics announced the publication of the clinical studies behind FDA clearance of its urine-based MycoMEIA Aspergillus assay, in Clinical Infectious Diseases, per the [company's announcement](https://business.theantlersamerican.com/theantlersamerican/article/bizwire-2026-10-7-pearl-diagnostics-announces-publication-of-clinical-studies-supporting-fda-clearance-of-mycomeia-aspergillus-assay). The test caught 19 of 24 infections that serum galactomannan had missed, offering an alternative to diagnostic approaches that require invasive specimens.
