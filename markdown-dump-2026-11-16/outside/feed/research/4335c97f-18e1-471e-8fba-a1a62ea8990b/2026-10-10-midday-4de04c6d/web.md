## Rockrose extract kills ringworm fungi in lab tests
A polyphenol-rich extract from the Mediterranean rockrose *Cistus salviifolius* [killed several dermatophytes in laboratory tests](https://www.news-medical.net/news/20260928/Mediterranean-rockrose-extract-shows-strong-potential-against-skin-fungi.aspx), including *Trichophyton rubrum* and *Microsporum canis*, while doing nothing to *Candida albicans* at the concentrations tried. In lab-grown human skin infected with the fungi, the extract cut the fungal load and preserved the skin barrier without harming the skin's own cells, though it has not yet been tested in people.
