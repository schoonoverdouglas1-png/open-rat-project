## Echinocandins show potent lab activity against resistant ringworm fungi
A [study published today](https://www.mdpi.com/2309-608X/12/10/769) found anidulafungin and caspofungin potently active in the lab against terbinafine-resistant Trichophyton indotineae, screening 105 clinical dermatophyte isolates in all. Strictly in vitro, though: no clinical breakpoints were set, so for now this is a lead for resistant ringworm, not a treatment.
