## Court recommends class for 30 million in Amazon e-book suit
Magistrate Judge Gabriel Gorenstein issued the recommendation in an October 1 report, advancing the antitrust case alleging Amazon's agreements with the Big Five publishers curb lower-priced e-book sales elsewhere. Plaintiffs' review of 658 million transactions estimated customers paid over $740 million more than in a competitive market, according to [the report](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261002135291/en/Hagens-Berman-Court-Overseeing-Amazon-E-Books-Monopoly-Lawsuit-Recommends-Class-Certification-of-30-Million-Consumers).
