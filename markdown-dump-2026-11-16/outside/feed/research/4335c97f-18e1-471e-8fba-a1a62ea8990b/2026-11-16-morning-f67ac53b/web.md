## When culture misses, labs can still type the case
A review published today says [public health labs can determine a molecular serotype](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40588-026-00283-2) on primary specimens when culture fails after early antibiotics. When isolates are available, serogroup and serotype determination plus whole genome sequencing support surveillance and outbreak investigations.
