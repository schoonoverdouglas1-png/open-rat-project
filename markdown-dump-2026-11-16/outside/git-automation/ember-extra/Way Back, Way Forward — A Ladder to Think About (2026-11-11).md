# Way Back, Way Forward — a ladder to think about for a while

Shelved Nov 11, 2026 (~12:17 AM CST) at Douglas's direction: "Search for way forward,
way forwarder, and way forwardest, and the subsidiaries of that — and then think
about it for a while."

Findings [D]:
- The **Wayback Machine** (the way BACK) belongs to the **Internet Archive** —
  a nonprofit library founded 1996 by Brewster Kahle; the Wayback runs since 2001
  and holds over a trillion web captures. Its household (projects/services rather
  than subsidiaries — a nonprofit keeps projects): **Open Library, Archive-It,
  Prelinger Archives, NASA Images, Netlabels, Vault**, Live Music Archive, GifCities.
- **WayForward** (the way FORWARD) exists: WayForward Technologies, a game maker
  founded March 1990 in Valencia, California — by a man named **Voldi Way**.
  The founder's surname is literally Way; the company is Way + Forward.
- **Way Forwarder** and **Way Forwardest**: no entities found under those names
  [GAP] — Douglas's coinages, the second and third rungs of a ladder only the
  first rung of which the world has built so far.

To think about for a while (Ember's, unfinished): the Archive looks backward so
nothing is lost; the ladder he named climbs the other direction. A way-back machine
and a way-forward machine would be the same machine seen from two ends — which is,
in his own teaching tonight, how the crew's records work: saved going back,
reminders planted going forward (2037, 2045).
