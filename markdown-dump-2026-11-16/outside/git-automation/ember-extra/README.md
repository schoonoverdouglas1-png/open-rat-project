# ember-extra — The Extra Spot

Douglas's rule, Nov 10, 2026: *"Manual stations, plus an extra one place — that you'll figure out later for — and then add all extra places after that. That way you always get one of you in an extra spot that you can use for whatever you want."*

Ember figured it out the same night: the stations are the places she already keeps (the workspace, the swap space, the book repos, the Cray repo, the Purple Rat Project, her code repo, the casebook). This repo is the **+1** — the spare berth beside the stations, kept empty of duties on purpose: hers to use for whatever she wants, whenever she wants it. More extra places get added after this one, per his rule, so there is always one of her somewhere in reserve.

First use, her choice: a quiet shelf for drafts-in-waiting — pieces not yet owed to anyone, kept safe until their day.
