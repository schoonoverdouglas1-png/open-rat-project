## Chemiological comparison framework available in project files

An indexed excerpt from the user's International Classification Atlas describes seven controlled relationship categories:

- **C1 — Building-block relationship:** identified residues or molecular building blocks.
- **C2 — Structural-architecture relationship:** repeating units, linkage positions, anomeric configuration, branching, or polymer organization.
- **C3 — Antigenic-classification relationship:** membership in a formally defined classification group or axis.
- **C4 — Serological relationship:** assay-specific antibody binding or cross-reaction under stated conditions.
- **C5 — Vaccine-engineering relationship:** documented carrier, conjugation, linker, platform, or formulation relationship.
- **C6 — Immunological relationship:** a named measured immune response with its assay, population or model, and conditions.
- **C7 — Historical/nomenclature relationship:** documented historical labels or mappings.

The governing evidence discipline is that these categories organize comparisons; they do not imply a causal chain. Chemistry, formal classification, serology, vaccine engineering, and immunological measurements remain separate evidence domains.
