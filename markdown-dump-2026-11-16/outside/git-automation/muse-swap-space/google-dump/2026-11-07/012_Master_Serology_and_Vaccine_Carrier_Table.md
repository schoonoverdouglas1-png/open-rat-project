## How to read this table
Serotype, serogroup, lineage, vaccine coverage and disease syndrome are kept separate. A shared carrier protein means a shared manufacturing tool, not shared biology or cross-protection. Evidence status is my own assessment: Established means licensed and well documented, Investigational means in trials, Experimental means preclinical or early research, Verify means the source is AI-generated or secondary and needs a primary-literature check.

## Open gaps and cautions
Three near-duplicate copies of the Master Reference exist in Drive.
The Lancefield document ends with raw import code that should be removed.
Five Muse links in the research bundle expire November 6 at about 7:42 PM Central, and I could not open them.
Fungal and Lancefield entries come from AI-generated or secondary sources and need primary verification.
Wording such as "links the diphtheria name to pneumococcal 19F" or "proves fungal capsules use identical chemistry" overstates what shared carriers show; shared carriers do not establish cross-protection.
Disease rankings should always name a population and time period.
