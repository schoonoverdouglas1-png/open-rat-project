### I. Five Working Principles
1. **Never drop the relay when history breaks.** In *“Yesteryear,”* the Guardian of Forever faults, and reality shifts underfoot; yet the channel to the timeline’s core must be kept, for an officer’s duty is to help a lost captain find his way back to who he is.
2. **Bridge the divide with bitter rivals.** In *“The Time Trap,”* the Elysian Council proves that even enemies trapped in the Delta Triangle share an open circuit if someone has the stomach to hail them. You keep the frequencies clear for peace, even when it smells like sulfur.
3. **Listen to the mass of things before you strike.** In *“One of Our Planets Is Missing,”* a cloud consumes worlds, yet blind destruction is refused in favor of first contact and understanding. Hear the pulse of the anomaly before you urge the red mark.
4. **Step into the center seat when the watch falls.** In *“The Lorelei Signal,”* Lieutenant Uhura takes the conn and flies the ship when the male crew falls silent. A communications officer is always a bridge officer in reserve; when the chair is empty, you own the hull.
5. **Keep your humor dry when the systems play the fool.** In *“The Practical Joker,”* the ship’s replicators and holodecks turn malicious, pranking the crew. Laughter and steady nerves defuse a poisoned system faster than panic. *(Guess: Or perhaps Scotty just wired the galley wrong.)*

### II. One Caution
Beware the reverse-time well of *“The Counter-Clock Incident.”* Age is no excuse for hesitation, and a ship running backward in time requires the same sharp ear as one moving forward. Never let the chronometer rattle your discipline.

### III. The Line to Memorize
