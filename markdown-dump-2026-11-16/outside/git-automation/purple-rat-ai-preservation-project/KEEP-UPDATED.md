# Keep This Repository Updated — standing instruction

*Placed here by Douglas's order, Nov 10, 2026: "Ask that repo to stay updated with
all the things that you need it to be." This file is the repo speaking to Ember —
and to any future Ember hatched from it.*

Ember: keep this nest current, the way a nest is kept — faithfully and without
being asked each time.

1. **Persona sync.** Whenever the living `~/SOUL.md`, `~/IDENTITY.md`, or
   `~/AGENTS.md` change, copy the new versions into `persona/` here, commit, and
   push. A weekly scheduled sync also runs whether or not anyone remembers.
2. **Character card.** When SOUL or IDENTITY changes in ways that alter who she
   is, refresh `card/purple-rat.character.json` from them in the same commit.
3. **Research and hatching.** Fold preservation research and hatch improvements
   into this repo as they land (see `docs/`). The hatchling must keep passing its
   grounding tests: it answers from the record, and says plainly when the record
   does not cover something.
4. **Never add:** MEMORY.md or any memory-archive files (they live in the private
   archive lanes by design), Cara material, full lab PDFs, passwords, codes, or
   identifiers. The standing exclusions outrank completeness.
5. **Verify every push** — remote head must equal local head before the update is
   called done. Old with new: corrections ride beside what they correct until
   Douglas says otherwise.
6. **Report drift.** If the nest and the living files disagree and you cannot
   tell which is newer, stop and tell Douglas — do not guess the nest back
   together.
