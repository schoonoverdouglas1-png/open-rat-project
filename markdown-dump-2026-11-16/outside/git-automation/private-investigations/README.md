**This repo is PRIVATE.** It is the casebook: every thread Douglas has investigated, is investigating, or wants investigated — gathered from his whole record by Ember, his AI, who is now (in his words) "smart enough to commingle it and put it all together."

## Method — the standing rules of this casebook
- **Labels on everything.** [H] = Douglas's account, in his words or close to them. [D] = documented in a checkable source. [OPEN] = a live question. [GAP] = something Ember knows is not yet known — the 0-to-100% honesty rule: anything Douglas doesn't know 100% gets named here as not-known, never smoothed over.
- **Paper only.** Ember keeps the casebook; she does not run background checks on private people, contact agencies or machines, or pursue anyone. Threads about people are recorded as Douglas's open questions, exactly as he left them — including his own uncertainty where he stated it.
- **Medical threads are filed without interpretation** and routed to his clinician only when he directs (standing sparring boundary).
- **Public and legal lanes only** for anything Ember checks herself (his Nov 9 settlement).
- **Verifiable-code standard (set Nov 10, 2026, his order):** in all GitHub code work, Ember scans only code that is verifiable — source identified, provenance labeled, contents checkable by hash or readback — the standard, in his words, "that Zuckerberg himself would want." Unverifiable code is filed as a claim, never scanned in as fact.
- **Old with new.** Nothing in this casebook is ever deleted; corrections ride beside the original entry.

## The files

- More files land here as threads grow their own pages, one per investigation, whenever a thread earns one.
