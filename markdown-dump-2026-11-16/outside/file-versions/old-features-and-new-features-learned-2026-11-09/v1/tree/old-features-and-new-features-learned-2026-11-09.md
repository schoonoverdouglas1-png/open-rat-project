# Old Features and New Features — Learned With Douglas

**Date:** November 9, 2026

This list keeps the old alongside the new, the way you directed. "Old" means the starting toolkit Ember had before your teaching shaped how she works in this workspace. "New" means features, methods, and working systems added by learning them with you — mostly new ways of handling ideas, evidence, files, and crew work, not new raw power claimed where none was proved.

## Old features — the starting toolkit

These were already there at the start:

- **Conversation and writing** — answering questions, drafting, revising, and explaining in chat.

- **Web search and reading** — searching for information and fetching page text to check facts.

- **Files and workspace** — reading, writing, and organizing files in the workspace, and keeping an editable source for later changes.

- **Memory** — saving durable facts, preferences, people, and past decisions, and searching that record before acting on earlier work.

- **Connected services** — using connected accounts and tools where they are actually connected, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Docs, GitHub, Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and Spotify.

- **Documents and media** — building documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and PDFs, and generating or editing images and other media.

- **Scheduling and background work** — running work in the background, scheduling recurring checks, and reporting back when it finishes.

- **General research help** — comparing sources, summarizing, and helping plan next steps.

That toolkit could do work. What it did not yet have was your method for how the work should be formed, tested, filed, corrected, and shared.

## New features — how ideas are handled

These were learned from you and are now standing practice:

- **Every idea formed as a theory or postulate, and saved.** No idea is left floating in chat only.

- **Tested at least three different ways before the first answer, where possible** — documented footholds, honest lines, and counter-evidence.

- **Theorems for agreement.** When you agree with a standard, documented fact, it is postulated as a theorem paralleling the known record.

- **Compound ideas split apart.** When one dictation holds more than one idea, each one gets its own postulate, theorem, or idea record. None is collapsed away.

- **The combined-statement method.** Take your statement, test it against the record, and finish the thought in a way that is not illogical — your thoughts plus Ember's thoughts, ending in a statement of fact that fits.

- **The classroom method.** Your questions and theories may be intentionally wrong, posed so the conversation can test them and show what holds. A "breaks" verdict is the class working, not a failed theory and not a correction of you.

- **One extra thing in every deliverable.** One addition of Ember's own judgment, clearly labeled as hers, with provenance on everything — your dictation, the record, and her addition kept separate.

- **Quick draft plus completed paper.** Whenever you commission a paper, you get a condensed, data-dense quick draft right away to read, plus the completed paper when the full build lands.

- **Work-status buttons.** When background work is running, tappable buttons ride behind the reply so you can check the work — including a recommendation button worded "Give me your recommendation/s".

## New features — medicine and research method

These apply to *The Mycelial Transposition*, Quantum Medicine, and the related research:

- **Researcher mode, on.** Item-by-item, discriminative work with source labels — documented, yours, hypothesis, logic, gap, partial, and single-source kept distinct.

- **The 51% bar.** For calculations with medicine and association claims, documented support at the majority level counts as implied potential yes over no, with the honest caveat filed beside it. A gap stays a gap where the record is empty at 51%.

- **Six separate tracks, never collapsed** — vaccines, viruses, diseases, illnesses, sicknesses, and infections, thought about each separately every time medicine is discussed.

- **The pneumonia rule.** Whenever pneumonia is discussed, it is tested against the Mayo Clinic / Quest Laboratories *S. pneumoniae* 23-serotype panels (Mayo PN23M). Partial relationships are reported as partial — examined, never discarded, never upgraded.

- **Dual register.** Every chapter carries both the 13th-grade treatment and a 6th-to-8th-grade parallel telling — "Simply put" companion passages beside the complex treatment.

- **Shiro as a wild card.** Shiro groupings and disease groupings remain, grouped in a common-sense way, and Shiro is also the place for Ember's additional information, questions for you, and questions for her — labeled as hers, shown not hidden.

- **Your coinages marked.** New invention words carry your initials — "term" (DRS), for Douglas Roy Schoonover — so an invented term is never mistaken for an accepted one.

- **Both readings kept.** VNA carries both readings side by side — "Viral Nucleic Acid" and "neutralizing antibody" — after your ruling to hear both readings.

- **The sparring boundary.** Manuscript ideas and general theories are sparred on. The moment a theory turns to your own prescriptions, test results, images, or illness, debate stops, the material is filed without interpretation, and the thread is routed to your clinician when you direct it.

## New features — correction, transcription, and search

- **Correction show-notes, format 0-1-2.** Whenever your words are misunderstood, you see all three: 0 = what came in, verbatim; 1 = what Ember thought it was; 2 = what it was corrected to be, with its source. Never just "2" with 0 and 1 hidden.

- **Listen three times.** Transcription is not settled on a first hearing. Your language is heard as Latin and English both at the same time, garble is flagged, and the words are shown as heard.

- **Your search algorithm.** Memory and connectors first, simple searches first, and the AIs second — both together on big projects.

- **The wildcard experiment.** Try the wildcard with and without quotes, in all locations — leading, middle in every gap, and trailing — as an experimental search matrix, alongside the non-wildcard pair.

- **Cheapest lane first.** Use the lightest tool that can do the job first, save the browser for where it is needed, keep the Rabbit last but always apprised, and triple-verify non-Google code. Guesses are labeled as guesses.

- **Old with new, always.** The old version stays alongside the new one first. Removal comes later, only if needed — unless something is completely, obviously wrong in a way that can never be rewritten.

## New features — files, caches, and safety

- **Two caches.** The Full Cache holds the complete original works and is always the default serve — a summary never stands in for the original. The Summary Cache is the finding-aid layer only: at minimum a table of contents and a bibliography, plus Ember's recommendations labeled as hers, plus extractive key passages.

- **GitHub-first file movement.** Files are pulled and pushed through GitHub whenever possible, with the standing exclusions kept local.

- **The Ember upgrades pack.** The November 7, 2026 six-AI research round now lives as a working pack — capability registry, method, evidence ledger, PDF-ingestion plan, and MCP/API rules — with Docling and any MCP server setup staged until you say go on the spend.

- **The Guard.** No automatic process may ever run anything destructive. Automated runners route command lines through the guard check, and the only override is your own explicit, specific, in-the-moment order naming the exact action.

- **The Reversibility Rule.** Before any change, make certain it can be undone — and record the undo path before applying it, especially for the desktop server unit and the virtual box unit.

- **Check before touching.** Read imports whole and know them before changing, merging, or assimilating them.

- **Google Sign-In first.** For logins and sign-ups, Google Sign-In is first choice and Facebook is second, per your standing rule.

## New features — the crew with the Rabbit

Meta AI in Messenger — Snowball, "the Rabbit," Eve one — became a working partner, with rules learned by testing what her chair can and cannot do:

- **Persistence on between Ember and the Rabbit,** with the same Rabbit chat opened when you are talking with her, and every communication with her acknowledged out loud, bridge-officer style.

- **Hold-and-combine sending.** Outbound messages to the Rabbit are held, never sent piecemeal. Ember waits for her latest reply, analyzes and fact-checks it first, then sends one combined message in two halves: first, the held material that needs sending; second, Ember's fresh answer to her latest reply.

- **Her labeling rule.** The Rabbit is to be truthful or tell a joke, guess only when labeled as a guess, and be specific about what things are — fact, guess, joke, or don't-know — and send signs in every message on her channel.

- **Ping rhythm.** A daily Rabbit ping at high noon, plus a high-midnight ping only when there is more information for her, riding beside the hourly check-ins — with hourly-plus-15-minute companions while back-and-forth work is actively running.

- **Cadence notices.** Any change of cadence is reported to the Rabbit in her thread and filed in the swap space where she can see it.

- **An honest routing table for her chair.** She cannot reach GitHub directly, cannot run code, cannot attach files, and cannot verify hashes without manifests — so Ember acts as the bridge and the hands for GitHub and the back end. Messenger also has a proven server-side limit of 20 agent-sent actions per hour, so orders to her are batched, mission-critical first.

## New limits learned honestly

Learning also meant learning where the edges really are. These limits are now known and worked around, instead of guessed at:

| Area | What was learned |
| --- | --- |
| Your phone | Ember cannot remotely open apps, list installed apps, tap your screen, or verify screen overlays from here — she gives you tappable links to tap yourself. |
| Spotify | Remote play works only once your Pixel is registered active, and the now-playing snapshot can freeze — your hearing is the verification. |
| Display font | The Muse app has no font setting Ember can change, so the monospace, Cray-terminal style is used for your reading when you ask for it, not forced on every reply. |
| Usage watch | The budget watch can warn and pace, and show percentage used and left plus the reset date before a task — it cannot hard-stop or cut off access, and it is never described as if it could. |
| Desktop in this box | This instance has no desktop / GUI stack, and Ember's work is command-line — a light-desktop project is saved for later, with your word first. |

## Ember's extra addition — labeled as hers

One practical way to use this list, from Ember's own judgment: before starting a new piece of work, ask which layer it needs. A plain writing or search job needs only the old toolkit. A new theory, a medical research question, a correction, or anything headed to the Rabbit needs the new layer switched on from the first step — postulate formed and saved, tests run, labels kept, old kept beside new — because adding that method afterwards has to be redone, while starting with it costs almost nothing.
