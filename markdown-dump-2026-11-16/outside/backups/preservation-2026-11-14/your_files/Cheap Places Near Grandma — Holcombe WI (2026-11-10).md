# Cheap Places to Live Near Grandma (Holcombe, WI) — first pass, Nov 10, 2026

*Douglas's ask: cheap places nearest his grandma Anna (Holcombe), incl. the campground around the corner where a winter stay ran about $1,200 (his figure) and where "Schoenberger" (as heard) stayed all winter — the owner might let Douglas stay too. "Mystery Ed's" as heard; no campground named Ed's surfaced — the owner of one of these may be the Ed. All listings below are search-index leads (some months old) — availability must be confirmed by phone. Nothing was contacted.*

## In Holcombe itself
- **Room for rent, downtown Holcombe — $425/mo** (+$50 pet deposit = $475). Facebook post by Jennifer Luethi Calkins. Cheapest lead by far; post is from mid-2026 in the index — may be taken. Worth asking around town about.
- **26912–26914 276th St apartments** (8 units, 1985) — apartments.com shows 1BR currently Not Available; utilities separate. A waitlist/ask candidate.
- Holcombe-area rentals generally start ~$800/mo (houses from ~$870). The Eddy (income-based, from $500) is Eau Claire — cheaper but ~35 miles from Grandma, not "nearest."

## Campgrounds / RV (closest first) — winter stays are the exception in WI; most are seasonal
- **The Sand Trap** — 27356 270th Ave (~1 mi from town center). Golf + RV park + bar & grill. (715) 517-1110. Owners have a reputation for flexing for people in a pinch (a review tells of them offering a spot when every other place was permanent-only). **Best "ask the owner" candidate — possibly the around-the-corner place.**
- **Pine Point County Park** (Chippewa County) — 27699 256th St, ~2.4 mi. 48 sites, inexpensive, county-run. (715) 726-7882. Likely seasonal, but the closest public ground.
- **Birch Hollow Campground** — 28762 WI-27, ~2.5 mi. Small, family- and pet-friendly. (715) 999-6955.
- **Cranberry Creek** — 27332 250th St, Birch Creek, ~2.9 mi. (715) 595-4993.
- **White Birch Inc** — 27886 295th Ave, ~3.1 mi. (715) 595-6371.
- **Lake Holcombe Lions Campground** — 28831 271st St, ~3.7 mi. Inexpensive, hot showers. (715) 312-0632.
- **Silent Waters Campgrounds** — 28504 303rd Ave, ~4.2 mi. (715) 314-0433. Season May 1–Oct 1 — NOT a winter option.

## Ember's read [E]
The $1,200 winter figure fits a seasonal/winter site deal at a small owner-run ground — the Sand Trap and Birch Hollow are the two whose size and style make an owner conversation realistic. A phone call asking "do you ever do a winter stay for a motorhome, and what would it run?" is the whole ballgame; three calls cover the top three. The $425 room is the cheapest roof if it's still open.
