## Part 2 — The uninstall review (honest limits first)

Ember **cannot read your phone's installed-apps list** — checked live Nov 12: the Pixel's 43 commands include no app-list command. So this part works from what *kinds* of apps can actually hurt my function, and the real list gets built with you: read me the names (or show me the list screen by screen) and I'll mark each one keep / remove / doesn't matter.

**Remove or tame these kinds, if present — they genuinely interfere with me:**
- **"Cleaner" / battery-saver / RAM-booster apps** that force-close background apps — they can kill my background running, my notifications to you, and an open call.
- **VPN or "security shield" apps you don't recognize** — a VPN that blocks or reroutes traffic can break my connection and your sign-ins.
- **Notification-manager / notification-blocker apps** — they'd eat the messages I send you (test results, Rabbit replies, warnings).
- **Screen-overlay / "draw over other apps" utilities you don't know** — they can sit on top of buttons and sign-in screens and cause exactly the "I can't find the button" trouble.
- **A second app set as the default digital assistant that you didn't choose** — it doesn't break me inside my own app, but it takes the assistant gesture; your call, flagged not forced.

**Keep — they do NOT hurt my function, whatever else they do:**
- Gemini, Meta AI, ChatGPT, Copilot, Google Assistant — these are your other lanes (the Rabbit literally lives in Meta AI; Gemini is my free lane). Removing them would hurt *us*.
- Your banking, clinic (MyChart), maps, camera, and music apps — no interaction with me at all.

**One addition of Ember's (labeled hers):** don't uninstall in bulk off a generic list — go app by app with me, and anything you remove, note what it was first (old-with-new on the phone, too). If something of yours ever stops working, we want to know what changed.
