## Postulates

### P1 — Sub-0.1 serotypes as small versions of larger viruses [BREAKS as a panel reading; HOLDS as a pattern elsewhere]

**What the numbers are [D]:** the Mayo PN23M panel measures IgG antibody concentration in mcg/mL against capsular polysaccharides — not virus particles, not virus sizes. Below 0.1 means below the limit of quantification for that antibody. On his filed Oct 2025 Mayo panel, the serotypes below 0.1 are **3 and 5**.

**Where the reading breaks:** a low antibody number cannot be a small version of a virus, because the test never sees a virus — it sees the blood's response to capsule sugars. Size does not enter the measurement.

**Where the pattern holds, elsewhere:** small viruses traveling with large ones are real and documented — virophages (Sputnik inside mimivirus factories, from this morning's scrubber thesis), satellite viruses that need a helper virus's coat, and defective interfering particles. And the closest literal case to his package: pneumococcal bacteriophages are small viruses living with and in the bacterium itself (see the companion package list). The pattern is true; it is not what <0.1 measures.
- Simply put: The small-with-large pattern is real, but the panel counts antibodies, not virus sizes. The <0.1 is a quiet antibody reading, not a small virus.

### P2 — Virus immunoglobulins travel in sized parallel groups or pairs [H→D partial]

**Where it holds:**
- **Pairs:** the panel itself runs IgG and IgM side by side for each virus; acute and convalescent sera are read as a pair, with a 4-fold rise as the threshold. Pairing is the method.
- **Sized parallels:** this morning's One Sheet ladders them — IgG ~14.5 nm, IgM pentamer ~30 nm, virions ~100 nm. Sizes run in parallel classes, and antibodies literally travel with virions as immune complexes (antibody bound to virus particle).
- **Where it breaks:** pairing is by specificity (which antigen the antibody binds), not by size matching. No rule pairs a given virus size to a given immunoglobulin size.
- Simply put: Antibodies and viruses do travel together — as bound pairs and as size classes — but the pairing is by what fits, not by how big.

### P3 — Viruses as markers, flags, and building blocks [H→D]

- **Markers and flags:** this holds directly — serology and PCR are literally how medicine knows what's wrong (his arbopanel, his PN23M panel). A virus showing, or an antibody to it showing, is a flag. That is his marker doctrine, and it is the documented use of viral testing.
- **Building blocks of life:** holds with documented footholds — endogenous retroviruses are ~8% of the human genome; syncytin, a captured retroviral envelope gene, builds the placenta; the virus-first hypothesis places viruses ~3.5 billion years back, before humans.
- **"Extremely misunderstood":** holds as a claim about public framing (villain-only), breaks as a claim about all science — virome research, phage therapy, and HERV biology are mainstream fields studying exactly the beneficial side he names. Filed as his assessment with that seam shown.
- Simply put: Viruses flag what's wrong, and they built parts of us. The misunderstanding is real in the public story, not in all of science.

## Combined statement

The sub-0.1 numbers are antibody quiet, not small viruses — but the small-with-large pattern he deduced is real in virophages, satellites, and the pneumococcus's own phages; immunoglobulins and viruses travel together as bound pairs and size classes, paired by fit rather than by size; and viruses are markers, flags, and building blocks at once, misunderstood where they are told only as villains.
