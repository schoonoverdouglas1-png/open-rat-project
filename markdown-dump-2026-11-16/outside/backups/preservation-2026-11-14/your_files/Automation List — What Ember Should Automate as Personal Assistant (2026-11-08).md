# Automation List — What Ember Should Automate as Personal Assistant (2026-11-08)

Compiled at Douglas's order (Nov 8, 2026, ~4:57 AM CST): aggregate, from everything he has told Ember to do along the way, the list of things she should automate. Sent to the Rabbit the same morning with the ask for her own list back, so the two lists can be cross-compared ("cross-commingled") with both sets of thoughts on each item.

## A. Already automated and running (his orders, live now)
1. **15-minute status loop** — usage first (percent used/left + reset), bottlenecks, integration health, Rabbit read first every run; notify him in parallel.
2. **Rabbit check-in rhythm** — read her first, bug her gently on owed items, one batched send, never retry a 429 (Messenger cap is 20 actions/hour, Meta's side).
3. **Feed Watch (GitHub)** — every 15 min over Atom feeds + the REST leg for the two private repos; reports only new entries.
4. **All-accounts sweep** — hourly, cheap counts/metadata only across Gmail, Drive, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Health Connect; silent when nothing changed.
5. **Git automation** — every 2 hours: pull-check + push-check of the archive; on conflict STOP, change nothing, alert him; any push/pull sends one main-chat message in the same run (push + chat notification together, per his order).
6. **Usage budget watch** — Power plan tracking, warnings at his thresholds, reset Sat Nov 14, 11:07 PM CST; 95% rule: press him to suspend almost everything.
7. **AOSP weekly check** — Mondays, Pixel software watch.
8. **Messenger digest prep** — his works of the day prepared in main chat for his tap to send (never sent unattended).

## B. To automate next (from his standing asks and patterns)
9. **Email triage flags** — surface only action-required mail (account security deadlines, study forms, "you shared data with…" grant notices) above the marketing noise; counts already swept hourly, content flags are the missing layer.
10. **Owed-items tracker for the Rabbit** — her outstanding pieces (findings, preservation code, full comparison, book comments) tracked by content, bugged on the check-in rhythm until each lands once.
11. **Book comment pipeline** — Rabbit comments on *My Life as an AI* chapters inserted old-with-new, quoted and fact-checked, pushed with the git sync.
12. **Search-lane assimilation** — every new lane (Tavily once signed up, Marginalia personal key once it arrives) gets the same treatment automatically: timed benchmark, filed results, repertoire updated. Searches always run wildcard AND non-wildcard (his standing method).
13. **Pre-appointment prep** — one hour ahead of fixed points (Mayo, Mon Nov 9, 3:45 PM): papers in hand reminder, time/place/provider confirmed from the record.
14. **Reference upkeep** — hotkeys/shortcuts reference, gains ledger, corrections log (0-1-2) updated as they change, never rebuilt from scratch.
15. **Chess relay** — the Alekhine's game (1. e4 Nf6 2. e5 Nd5) carried in the Rabbit batch; his move directed, hers logged.

## C. Rules wrapped around all of it (his, standing)
- **The Guard:** no automatic process ever runs anything destructive; only his explicit, specific, in-the-moment order overrides.
- **Old-with-new:** originals untouched; corrections land beside them.
- **Notify push + chat together** on any automated push/pull; silence on no-ops.
- **Nothing personal, lab, or family material** goes to any other AI, GitHub, or the Rabbit — ever.
- **No clinician contact** unless he directs it; personal medical material filed without interpretation.
- **Money:** no paid anything as a fix without a verified price and his explicit go-ahead on the exact plan.
