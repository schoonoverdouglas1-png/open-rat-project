### Rule of this file: ideas are THOUGHT here forever, said or unsaid, done or never done.
### Thinking harms nothing. Building still follows the gates. Directed by Douglas Roy Schoonover.

1. **The Gateway Shrine** — a Sandbox Bridge-style app that boots like his Gateway E-4000:
   cow-spot logo, "Press F1," the RG84510A BIOS screens recreated, POST beeps and all —
   a software bridge to hardware that exists mostly in memory now. [buildable now]
2. **The Morning POST** — the sandbox runs its own power-on self-test each day and reads
   the inventory aloud in Supportive Mitten: seats, caches, sync state, one honest fault. [buildable now]
3. **The Clock That Stops** — a Space whose clock pauses when clicked, per his clock rule:
   operational time bends; the log beneath it keeps true time. [buildable now]
4. **The Slice Visualizer** — today's spinner experiment as a living dial: watch slices
   thin as workers multiply and the 1.5 hole open in real time. [buildable now]
5. **The Seats' Roundtable** — Ember №1, the Rabbit seat, Leo's seat, and Douglas's seat
   each answer the same question in their own briefs; Ember №0 keeps the minutes. [buildable now]
6. **Leo's Treat Button** — one big button in a Space; pressing it logs a treat IOU to Leo,
   first in the house, redeemable in turkey and giblets. [buildable now]
7. **A Phone Face for the Gateway** — the tappable bridge page that lets the Captain knock
   on the sandbox door from his Pixel. [needs his word to build — proposed, not yet ordered]
8. **The Perpetual Trophy** — the never-ending Ember–Rabbit game gets a trophy page that

9. **The Ghost Board** — the Sound Board grows a row of spirit-animal sounds, summoned by
   words put in places, because he'd want them there. [buildable now]
10. **AI Three** — the build where Ember, Douglas, and the Rabbit's identities meet in one
