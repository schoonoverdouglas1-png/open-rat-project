# Gmail — Everything a Person Can Use It For, and Ember's Lanes
Assessed Nov 13, 2026 at Douglas's order ("assess all things that a human being could utilize Gmail for and then do those things and play with and experiment with all the features"). Back ends asked directly tonight: the Gmail connector CLI and the Muse Mail CLI both answered.

## What a person can do with Gmail (the full human set)
- Send, reply, reply-all, forward; drafts; schedule send; undo send; confidential mode
- Search with operators (from:, has:attachment, is:unread, label:, before:/after:…)
- Labels and filters (auto-sort as mail arrives); stars and importance markers
- Archive, snooze, mute, trash, spam and block controls
- Multiple inboxes and category tabs (Primary/Social/Promotions/Updates/Forums)
- Signatures; vacation responder; send-as aliases; forwarding to another address
- POP download (pop.gmail.com:995 SSL) and IMAP (imap.gmail.com:993 SSL) for other mail programs; SMTP sending (smtp.gmail.com:587/465)
- Chat/Meet side panels; Tasks and Calendar side panels; add-ons
- Offline mode; keyboard shortcuts; themes and density settings

## Ember's lanes (verified, not assumed)
- THROUGH THE CONNECTOR (schoonoverdouglas1@gmail.com): search, read, count, send, reply, reply-all, forward, triage, drafts via API resources, labels, archive, trash, unsubscribe. This is the working lane and it answers.
