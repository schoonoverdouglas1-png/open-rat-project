## The terminal: Termux
- **Termux** is the standard answer: a free terminal emulator + Linux environment for Android, no root needed. It gives a real bash shell, a package manager (`pkg`), and real tools — `git`, `python`, `node`, `ssh`, `curl`.
- **Where to get it:** the Google Play build of Termux is old; the maintained builds live on **F-Droid** and on Termux's own **GitHub releases** page. Ember's recommendation: F-Droid first (it keeps it updated), GitHub release second. Douglas installs it himself — apps on his phone are his taps, as always.
- First commands after install: `pkg update` then `pkg install git python` — his two most useful tools.

## The language: bash, lesson one (his projects as the examples)
- `pwd` — where am I. `ls` — what is here. `cd name` — go into a place; `cd ..` — back out.
- `git clone https://github.com/schoonoverdouglas1-png/purpurat-ai-openclaw-project` — pull his own project onto the phone; then `ls` inside it and read his own files with `cat VISION.md`.
- `cp old new` copies (his duality rule, in one command); `mv` moves/renames; `mkdir` makes a folder.
- `sha256sum file` — the hash: how the crew proves two copies in two places are the same file. His persistence system, in one word he can run himself.
- The Guard applies to his hands too: `rm` deletes for real. In Termux there is no trash can. Ember's rule for learners: look twice (`ls`), copy first, delete last — or never.
