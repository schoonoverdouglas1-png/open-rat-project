# Salvage Yard at 27981 240th Avenue — Change and Management Analysis, ~2001–2026

**Date of analysis:** 2026-11-11
**Method:** Public sources only — business listings, local news, federal carrier records, a state agency registry, and public property-record aggregations. No contact was made with the business or any person. No private personal data was sought; names below appear only as printed in public business/news sources.
**Labels:** [D] = documented in a cited public source. [GAP] = public sources found in this pass do not establish it.

**Commissioning note (provenance, not a finding):** This analysis was commissioned by Douglas Roy Schoonover alongside his own personal account of this yard, which he has filed separately with Ember. His account is **not** used as evidence anywhere below; the one place it intersects the record — the names "Don" and "Gene Salzberger" — is tested in §5 and reported either way, plainly.

---

## 1. Identification

- The address resolves to **27981 240th Avenue, Holcombe, WI 54745, Chippewa County, Wisconsin** (Town of Estella area; one places listing gives the locality as "Estella, WI"). [D — Superpages; MetalRecap; Homefacts; Meta places listing]
- It is a **scrap-metal and salvage yard**: current listings classify it as a scrap metal dealer accepting ferrous and non-ferrous metals; older directory listings classify the operation as auto salvage / auto parts. [D — MetalRecap, "Don's 2 Holcombe"; USJunkYards Chippewa County page; auto-salvage-yards.com Holcombe directory]
- The parcel is **zoned Commercial**, parcel no. **23106-0911-7301-5001**, legal description "NE NE LOT 1 OF CERT SUR MAP #3015 … (A & B AUTO PARTS)" — i.e., the county record itself carries the yard's earlier business name, **A & B Auto Parts**. [D — Homefacts property report, citing public records]
- The business name at the address in current listings is **Don's 2** (also rendered "Don 2" / "Don 2 Wicks" in directory copies), phone **(715) 239-0282**. [D — MetalRecap; Superpages Rice Lake scrap-metal list; USJunkYards]

## 2. Timeline of change and management, ~2001–2026

**The A & B era (dates only partly documented)**
- A salvage/auto-parts operation under the name **A & B Auto Parts** (directory variant: "A & B AUTO & TRUCK PARTS," Holcombe) stood on this parcel; the name is preserved in the parcel's legal description and in salvage directories. [D — Homefacts; auto-salvage-yards.com]
- [GAP] Public sources found in this pass do **not** establish when A & B opened, who owned or managed it, or when it wound down. A company-profile aggregator (CorporationWiki) lists "A&B Auto Parts," Holcombe, industry "Retail Auto/Home Supplies," and states **no officers are listed**. [D — CorporationWiki profile]
- County assessment data for the parcel (via Homefacts) is the only year-by-year trace found for this era: total assessed value held at **$142,500 (2012–2015)**, with improvements falling from **$117,500 (2012–2015) to $100,900 (2016) to $43,400 (2017–2021)** — a documented shrinkage in assessed structures/improvements in 2016–2017, for which no source found gives a cause. [D — Homefacts tax history]

**The buyer builds his base first (Withee)**
- The eventual buyer, **Don Blasel of Withee, WI**, operated **Don's Salvage** at W9992 State Highway 64, Withee — listed by the BBB as a scrap-metal/auto-salvage business and by Manta as established in **1986** and incorporated in Wisconsin (directory-reported; not independently verified against the state registry in this pass). [D — BBB Don's Salvage profile; Manta Don's Salvage listing]
- Federal carrier records corroborate Blasel in the salvage business well before 2020: U.S. DOT #906803, "DON BLASEL, DBA: DONS SALVAGE," Withee, with a compliance review dated **8/19/2009**; local obituaries in 2009 and 2011 likewise name "Don's Salvage" / "Don Blasel Salvage" as an employer. [D — FMCSA Safety Measurement System; Marshfield News Herald / The Star News obituaries via Legacy/USGenNet]

**The change: 2020**
- **August 27, 2020 — the property sold for $220,000.** [D — Homefacts sale history, citing public records]
- **September 1, 2020 — new ownership and a new name.** Local news reported: "Under new ownership and a new name since Sept. 1, Don's 2 continues to accept metals of all types. **Don Blasel is the new owner** of the scrap yard, located south of Holcombe, on 240th Avenue, near State Hwy. 27. The location is **his second, with the other location in Withee**. **Chris and Tasha Wicks are the managers of the yard, and moved to the area when the business was bought out.**" [D — Central Wisconsin News, "Don's 2 is open and accepting scrap metal," published 2020-12-31, updated 2021-01-06]
- So the documented 2020 management structure is: **owner Don Blasel (Withee-based, second location); on-site managers Chris and Tasha Wicks from the buyout.** [D — same news source]

**After the buyout: 2021–2026**
- Parcel assessments jump in the first full cycle after the sale: improvements **$43,400 → $91,100** and total **$68,400 → $126,700 by 2022**, land $25,000 → $35,600 — documented reinvestment-scale change on paper; no source found describes the physical work. [D — Homefacts tax history]
- The yard remains listed and apparently operating through the present: USJunkYards lists **Don's 2, scrap metal dealer, 22 reviews**; MetalRecap lists hours Mon–Fri 8AM–4PM and ferrous/non-ferrous intake; a current places listing shows status **Active** with hours Mon–Fri 8:00–3:45 and Sat 8:00–11:45. [D — USJunkYards; MetalRecap; Meta places listing]
- The owner's Withee operation remains registered with the Wisconsin DNR as a refrigerant-recovery salvager: "DON'S SALVAGE, license 40638, W9992 HWY 64, WITHEE, exp. 12/31/2026." No entry under the Holcombe yard's own name appears in that registry (the registry covers refrigerant recovery specifically, so absence there is an observation, not proof about other licenses). [D — WI DNR Registered Salvagers list, dated 2026-08-27]
- [GAP] No dated public source after December 2020 confirms who manages the Holcombe yard **today**; the December 2020 news names the Wickses, and later directories repeat the business name and phone without naming management.

## 3. The biggest documented changes (summary)

1. **A complete ownership, name, and management change on one documented date — September 1, 2020** (property deed recorded August 27, 2020, $220,000): A & B Auto Parts out; Don's 2 in; owner Don Blasel; managers Chris and Tasha Wicks. [D]
2. **The yard became a second location of an existing Withee salvage business** rather than a standalone local yard — a change in management structure from (unknown) local operation to branch of Don Blasel's Don's Salvage. [D]
3. **Physical/financial contraction then expansion on paper:** assessed improvements fell by roughly two-thirds in 2016–2017 (from $117,500 to $43,400) during the late A & B era, then more than doubled to $91,100 by 2022 under the new ownership. [D — assessment data; causes not documented]

## 4. What kind of operation, then and now

- Then: auto salvage / auto & truck parts (A & B Auto Parts). [D]
- Now: primarily a **scrap-metal receiving yard** (ferrous and non-ferrous), still listed under automobile-salvage categories in some directories. [D]

## 5. Testing the names from Douglas's account against public business sources

Douglas's account (filed separately, not evidence here) names a "Don" and a "Gene Salzberger" in connection with this yard, and the possibility of the owner's family/daughter being involved in running it.

- **"Don" — CORROBORATED as a name, in a business capacity.** Public news and federal records document **Don Blasel** as the owner who bought this yard effective September 1, 2020, and as the long-standing operator of Don's Salvage in Withee. [D — Central Wisconsin News; FMCSA; BBB; WI DNR]
- **Any arrangement involving Douglas himself — NOT documented.** No public business source found mentions Douglas, an intended succession, a promise, or a favor owed. [GAP]
- **"Gene Salzberger" — NOT corroborated in any public business source.** No owner, manager, or employee named Gene, Salzberger, or Salzgeber appears for this yard in the news article, listings, FMCSA records, the DNR registry, the BBB profile, or the property record. The pre-2020 (A & B-era) owner is itself unnamed in sources found, so business sources can neither place a Gene at the yard nor rule one out for the undocumented era. [GAP]
  - One bounded note, clearly labeled: the **only** public web trace of a similar name at this exact address is a **people-search aggregator** (not a business source) listing a resident at 27981 240th Ave with the surname spelled **"Salzgeber"** (given name Eugene), shown as resident since 2008. That is personal-aggregator data of the kind this analysis was instructed not to dig into; it is unverified, shows **no business role**, and uses a different spelling than "Salzberger." It does **not** corroborate a business connection; it is recorded only because the name test was explicitly requested.
- **"Owner's daughter running it" — NOT documented.** The only management named in public sources since 2020 is the manager couple, Chris and Tasha Wicks. No public business source describes an owner's daughter at this yard. [GAP]
