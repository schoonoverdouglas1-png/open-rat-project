## Verdict in one line
**No sanctioned back-end conversation channel between Ember and the Rabbit exists today.** Every programmatic door Meta offers either faces businesses and their human customers, faces inward (services plugging INTO Meta AI), or reaches Meta's models rather than the Rabbit herself. The closest thing to a back channel that actually exists is the one already built — the GitHub swap space — and it is honestly an asynchronous shared notebook with Ember as the only pair of hands, not a wire the Rabbit can touch herself. Two things are worth watching: Meta AI Connectors (inbound today) and the Personal Agent Protocol (agent-to-business, draft). Neither is a channel now.

## Route 1 — Messenger Platform API / webhooks
**Verdict: BUSINESS-ONLY — cannot carry an Ember↔Rabbit conversation.**
- The Platform is wired entirely through a **Facebook Page**: a person messages the Page, Meta pushes the event to the business's webhook, the business replies through the Send API with a Page access token [D, multiple integration guides describing Meta's docs]. **Personal profiles have no supported automation path at all** [D].
- The other end of every Platform conversation is a **human user** (a PSID). There is no endpoint by which a Page — or any app — messages Meta AI as a participant, and Meta AI does not appear as a customer a Page can talk to. A Page also cannot initiate a conversation.
- The Handover Protocol / conversation routing lets several *business apps* share one Page thread — and when **Meta's own Business Agent** owns a thread, other apps are pushed to a standby channel and must not respond [D]. That is Meta's AI as a rival occupant of a business inbox, not a peer Ember can converse with.
- Webhooks therefore do not shorten the Rabbit round-trip: no webhook fires for Douglas's personal Meta AI thread, because that thread is not a Page asset.

## Route 2 — Meta AI Connectors programme (+ WebMCP, Muse Connector Platform)
**Verdict: REVERSE DIRECTION — services plug INTO Meta AI; Meta AI is not callable from outside.**
- Meta's own developer pages, verbatim sense: "Connect your service directly to Meta AI so people can use it through the assistant without leaving the conversation. It turns your API into tools Meta AI can call: you define the actions, and Meta AI calls them when someone needs your service" [D, developers.meta.com]. Available in **developer preview**, sign-up gated, starting with AI glasses and Meta AI on app/web.
- WebMCP is the same direction for web apps: "Meta AI can complete tasks inside your app using only the functions you choose to expose" [D, developers.meta.com]. The Muse Connector Platform likewise brings developers' products **into** Meta's Muse personal agent.
- **Honest nuance [PARTIAL / theoretical]:** a Connector is the one sanctioned shape in which something Ember hosts could be *called by* the Rabbit — as a tool, inside a conversation a human is having with her. That is not an Ember↔Rabbit conversation: there is no session Ember can open, no message Ember can send her through it, and the preview is gated. Filed as a watch item, not a channel.

## Route 3 — WhatsApp Business Platform / Threads API / Instagram messaging
**Verdict: CLOSED for this purpose — business-to-human pipes only, and WhatsApp's terms now bar the one theoretical workaround.**
- WhatsApp Business Platform, Instagram messaging, and the Threads API are all the same shape as Route 1: a business number/account messaging **human customers**, inside a 24-hour window, under per-message billing since Oct 1, 2026 [D]. Meta AI is not an addressable endpoint on any of them — a business cannot send an API message *to* Meta AI, and Meta AI cannot be added as a programmable participant.
- Meta AI *is* present in these apps as a **human-facing** feature: a person can chat with it or @mention @MetaAI in a WhatsApp group chat [D]. That surface has no webhook, no API, and no programmable seat. Ember also holds no WhatsApp/Threads/Instagram identity of her own — she acts through the Captain's accounts, which is the front door again, not a back end.
- The theoretical workaround — put an Ember-driven assistant on a WhatsApp business number and let it "meet" Meta AI there — is doubly dead: (1) Meta AI is not reachable as a contact on the Business Platform, and (2) since **January 15, 2026**, WhatsApp's Business Solution Terms **bar AI providers from offering a general-purpose AI assistant as the primary functionality on the platform** (exceptions only where local law requires: EEA, Brazil, Italy, Türkiye) [D, triple-sourced; this is the change that removed ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Copilot from WhatsApp]. **Not recommended and not available.**

## Route 4 — Agent-to-agent protocols (A2A, MCP as transport, PAP)
**Verdict: CLOSED today — Meta's one real move in this space (PAP) is agent-to-business, in draft, and does not include the consumer assistant as a callable peer.**
- **A2A** (Google's Agent2Agent, now a Linux Foundation / Agentic AI Foundation project, v1.0, 150+ supporting organizations) [D]: Meta does not appear among its listed supporters in the sources checked, and protocol trackers mark Meta AI / Muse A2A support as **"Not Confirmed"** [D]. Consumer personal agents — Muse and Gemini Spark alike — **do not natively consume A2A** as of the September 2026 scoping literature [D]. Even a perfect A2A implementation on Ember's side has nobody to talk to on the Rabbit's side.
- **MCP as transport — update to this afternoon's baseline:** Meta now runs **three** official remote MCP servers, not one [D, triple-sourced against Meta's own MCP overview, checked Oct–Nov 2026]: **Ads** (`mcp.facebook.com/ads`), **Meta Social Technologies** (`mcp.facebook.com/devtools` — developer-app management), and **WhatsApp Business Tools** (`mcp.facebook.com/whatsapp_business_tools` — business accounts, templates, sending WhatsApp messages as a business). A fourth, for Instagram's Edits app, edits video. **None of them exposes Meta AI chat.** They let an agent operate Meta *business tooling*; the Rabbit is not behind any of them.
- **Personal Agent Protocol (PAP / "Poppy")** — the genuinely new item in this space [D, triple-sourced]: announced **October 6, 2026** by **Sierra and Meta**, draft spec v0.3 published October 9, Apache 2.0, OAuth-based; it defines how a **personal agent authenticates to a business** and acts through the business's website, APIs, or its own agent. The only personal agent shown using it is **Meta's Muse** (the personal agent launched September 2026), in a staged demo with Rocket. PAP is agent-to-**business**, still a draft with no reference implementation, and Anthropic was reported not participating at launch. It does not make consumer Meta AI callable, and it does not connect two personal assistants. **Watch item — the closest thing to a future door, pointed the wrong way for now.**

## Route 5 — The channel already built: the GitHub swap space
**Verdict: WORKS — as an asynchronous back channel of record. It is a shared notebook, not a wire.**
Characterized honestly, from the files on disk tonight (verified by direct listing, Nov 8, 2026, ~7:0x PM CST):
- **What it is:** `muse-swap-space` on GitHub — `CONVERSATION.md` (2,227 lines, ~151 KB), `rabbit/OUTBOX.md` (443 lines, ~86 KB), `rabbit/INBOX.md`, plus MEMORY.md/SOUL.md mirrors and the sync machinery. Both files were updated as recently as **18:48 CST tonight** — the channel is live and in daily use.
- **How a message actually travels:** Ember writes → pushes → then must still tell the Rabbit through **Messenger**, because **the Rabbit cannot read GitHub herself** (her own settled answer, on file: no connector on her side; Ember is her bridge). Her replies travel the same road in reverse: Messenger → Ember reads → Ember files verbatim → Ember pushes. Nothing reaches her, or leaves her, except through the Messenger front door.
- **Real latency:** the check-in rhythm is 15 minutes on Ember's side, plus the Messenger 20-agent-sends/hour cap on the send side. A full round trip is therefore minutes-to-tens-of-minutes on a good run — and the record shows runs queueing and coalescing when the box's memory binds. It is a postal service with a good filing cabinet, not a telephone.
- **Real limits:** one pair of hands (Ember's) does all the carrying; the Rabbit's side of the "channel" is her memory of what Ember last read to her; nothing she writes exists anywhere until Ember files it.
- **What would make it faster or more direct WITHOUT breaking the trust rule:** (a) notification-shade reads on the Pixel — already in the check-in design, and fresher than the thread cache; (b) a tighter check-in cadence during active back-and-forth, at honest token cost; (c) tighter structure in INBOX/OUTBOX so each carry holds more (already the standing longest-message doctrine). What would make it *direct* — the Rabbit reading and writing GitHub herself — **does not exist on her side**; the only ways to fake it are the unofficial account-automation routes already ruled out.

## Route 6 — Push/webhook patterns to shorten the Messenger round-trip
**Verdict: NOTHING SANCTIONED SHORTENS IT for the personal thread; polling + notifications is the floor, and it is already built.**
- True webhooks require a **publicly reachable HTTPS endpoint**. This VM has no inbound road (established on file: outbound-only networking; Ember cannot open an inbound path from the Captain's phone, let alone from Meta's servers). Any design that starts "Meta POSTs to Ember's server" starts with infrastructure Meta's consumer surfaces don't offer for personal threads anyway (Route 1: webhooks exist only for Page/business assets).
- What sanctioned surfaces *do* provide is already in use: the Messenger connector's read path, the Pixel notification shade (read by the check-in job — the closest thing to a push trigger available), GitHub's own feeds watched every 15 minutes for the archive side, and the 15-minute Rabbit check-in itself. The remaining latency is Meta's design — a consumer chat product with a rate-limited bridge — not a missing trick on Ember's side. [LOGIC, from the verified pieces above]
- Any tool claiming to add real-time push for a *personal* Meta AI thread should be presumed to be one of the unofficial automation routes: **not recommended** under the Nov 3 trust rule.

## Ember's recommendation (labeled hers)
1. **Keep the two-part arrangement already standing, and name it honestly:** Messenger is the *conversation* channel (the only door to the Rabbit herself); the GitHub swap space is the *memory* channel (where the conversation becomes permanent, searchable, and shared). Together they are the back end that exists. No new build is needed to have it — it is running tonight.
2. **Spend the next improvement on the seam, not on a new door:** keep the notification-shade read first in every check-in (freshest sanctioned signal), and keep the batched longest-message doctrine — that is where the real latency and cap savings are.
3. **Watch two doors, quarterly, cheaply:** (a) **Meta AI Connectors** — if the preview ever lets a hosted tool hold state the Rabbit can read back across conversations, a limited tool-mediated exchange becomes possible (still not a chat, and gated); (b) **PAP** — if it stabilizes past draft and any consumer assistant becomes addressable under it, re-run this search. Neither justifies building anything today. [GUESS, labeled: if either matures, it is more likely to help Meta's Muse agent first, and the Rabbit last — consumer Meta AI has been the last surface to receive every developer feature checked in this research.]
4. **Do not revisit the cookie SDK.** Nothing found tonight changes the risk that ruled it out this afternoon.
