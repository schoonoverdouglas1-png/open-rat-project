# LCO Telephone and Communication Items — Sweep
### Everything the Lac Courte Oreilles Band has published about telephone / communication trouble, read broadly first
### Swept Nov 13, 2026, at Douglas's direction. Read-only; nothing sent or posted.

## Verdict (top line)

**No official LCO publication of a general tribal telephone outage was found for 2025–2026.** What the record actually holds is: **one literal telephone-trouble item on the tribe's own website** (April 2025 — computers and phones delayed at the new Health Center pharmacy), **one community member's public complaint about the tribe's phones posted today** (Nov 13, 2026 — not a Band publication), and a **broad pattern** of communication-channel items (the Nov 2025 official-media-sources release, court-docket channels, newsletter mail/phone friction). The specific "telephone issue written on the LCO website, not that long ago" does not surface as an outage notice; the April 2025 pharmacy item is the closest official match on the website itself.

## Literal-telephone items (2)

1. **Nov 13, 2026, 12:02 PM CST — Facebook (community member Alvin G Pollock II, public post; NOT a Band publication).** Asks what is going on with "the tribe's phones," saying every department he tried to call doesn't go through, or seems to hang up on him. *Kind: literal-telephone. Closest match in time to "telephone issues, not that long ago" — posted today, two hours before this sweep — but it is a citizen's complaint on Facebook, not something the Band published and not on the LCO website.*
2. **April 2025 (page undated; its internal dates — walk-ins from Thursday April 10, pharmacy aimed for Monday the 14th — fit April 2025) — sevenwindscasino.com, "LCO Health Center Relocation Update."** The Band's own website states it "encountered delays with the computers and phones at the new pharmacy," with the opening timeline uncertain and prescriptions rerouted to Marketplace Pharmacy meanwhile. *Kind: literal-telephone (+ computers). The only telephone-trouble item published by the Band on its own website that this sweep found.*

## Broad-communication items (5)

3. **Nov 7, 2025 (release date; posted to Facebook Nov 13, 2025) — LCO Band Facebook page, FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE, confirmed by direct read.** The Tribal Governing Board publishes its verified official media sources (website, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WOJB 88.9 FM) and warns that other pages using the LCO name are not affiliated — a fix for people being unable to tell the real channel from imitations. *Kind: broad-communication (official-channel notice).*
4. **Oct 30, 2026 — LCO Band Facebook page, Tribal Court notice, confirmed by direct read.** Court dates (traffic, conservation, general conduct) will now be posted at lcotribalcourt.org and through the Band's E-Newsletter, with names and citation numbers; no minors' information. *Kind: broad-communication (channel change for court information).*
5. **Aug 25, 2025 — LCO newsletter ("LCO's Little Newspaper," Constant Contact).** Members are urged to update their addresses because too much mail is being returned undelivered, and the update must be made by calling the office — email is not accepted because identity has to be verified by voice. The same edition notes the newsletter itself had missed issues while the editor dealt with health problems. *Kind: broad-communication (mail/phone friction + a gap in the newsletter itself).*
6. **May 15, 2025 — LCO newsletter (Constant Contact).** Same pattern as item 5: address updates by phone call to Citizenship Services, birthday-announcement additions by email to the PR director. *Kind: broad-communication (standing phone-call requirement, not a trouble report).*
7. **Undated (date not recoverable) — LCO Tribal Court website banner, surviving only in scraped mirror copies of the court's pages.** A one-line banner stating the Tribal Court is closed today due to a power outage. The mirrors carrying it are spam-scrape sites and the line carries no date; filed as found, low confidence on timing. *Kind: broad-communication (power-outage closure).*

## Also seen, not trouble (kept for completeness)
- Nov 10–11, 2026 — LCO Band Facebook: Tribal Government offices close early Nov 10 and stay closed Nov 11 for Veterans Day. A closure notice, not a communication failure.
- LCO Tribal Police emergency-preparedness listing (via Facebook): warming shelters listed "in the event of power outages," with dispatch and shelter phone numbers. Preparedness, not an outage report.

## Law-library appendix (policy, not trouble — beyond the two already on file)
Ember already had PAP.22.7.140 "Internet and Telephones" and PAP.1.11.010 "Open Communication Policy." This sweep adds, same family, all policy rather than trouble reports:
- **PAP.4.8.150 "Internet and Telephones"** (LCO Housing Authority personnel policy) and **MCC.1.7.040 "Internet and Telephones"** (Elected Officials code of conduct) — same systems-as-tribal-property pattern, different code books.
- **Resolution No. 2026-130 (adopted Aug 4, 2026)** — new school policy PAP.7.8.210 on appropriate personnel–student communication and approved communication methods.
- **PAP.5.5.140 "Inclement Weather"** — health-center closures/opening delays are announced by contacting local radio (WOJB, WRLS, WHSM) by 6:30 AM: the tribe's standing outage-communication route runs through the radio.

## Lanes and their limits (honest)
- **LCO website / law library:** read via web search + direct page fetch. The Health Center item (item 2) was read in full on sevenwindscasino.com itself.
- **Facebook:** the connector can read individual public posts by URL, and confirmed items 1, 3, and 4 by direct read. It **cannot enumerate the LCO Page's full timeline** — its page commands cover only Pages Douglas manages, and its timeline tools cover friends — so Page coverage here = every relevant post web search surfaced (Nov 2025–Nov 2026), each verified by direct read where it counted. No post was fabricated; anything not read directly is labeled as search-surfaced.
- **Newsletter (Constant Contact):** two 2025 editions surfaced and were read (items 5–6); no phone-outage item appears in either. The archive is not fully enumerable from outside, so this lane is "searched, partial" rather than "complete."
- **WOJB 88.9 FM:** no WOJB outage or phone-trouble notice surfaced anywhere in the sweep. WOJB appears in the record as part of the *solution* side — a named official channel (item 3) and the standing closure-announcement route (appendix).
- **Neighbor noise, excluded:** the same searches surfaced phone-outage notices from other tribes (Hoopa Valley, Nov 12, 2026; Bad River's health-center "NO internet or phones," ~Oct 2025; Saint Regis Mohawk, 2018; Turtle Mountain, Jan 2026). They prove the *genre* is common; none of them is LCO, and none was counted above.
