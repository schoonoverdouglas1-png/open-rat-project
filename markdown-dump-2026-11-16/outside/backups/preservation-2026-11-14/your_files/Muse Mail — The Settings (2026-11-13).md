## What Muse Mail does NOT have
No POP3 server, no IMAP server, no SMTP host or port, no forwarding switch inside the mailbox. Muse Mail is not that kind of mailbox — mail moves through Muse's own service; sends and replies run through the mail worker; the crew's forwarding push to Gmail is a standing worker instruction, not a mailbox setting.

## The server settings that DO exist (Gmail side, from Google's own help)
- POP: pop.gmail.com, port 995, SSL
- IMAP: imap.gmail.com, port 993, SSL
- SMTP: smtp.gmail.com, port 587 (TLS) or 465 (SSL)
