# The Mycelial Transposition
### A Complete Compendium of American Disease Names, Clinical Counterparts, and Viral Chains

## Introduction: The Architecture of the Chain
Every entry in this text runs through an unbroken historical and biological chain: American name → disease → symptomology → bacteria → fungi / yeast / mold → immunoglobulins → serotyping → virus (the end).

The American name preserves the cultural and sensory experience of illness—what the patient felt, where it struck, or how it behaved. The Latin counterpart fixes the modern biological reality. Together, they map how folk memory connects to microscopic etiology.

[D] = Documented fact
[H] = Working thesis / naming directive (Lou Gehrig’s disease framework)
[LOGIC] = Structural and cross-reference assembly
[GAP] = Unknown or empty record, kept open without invention
