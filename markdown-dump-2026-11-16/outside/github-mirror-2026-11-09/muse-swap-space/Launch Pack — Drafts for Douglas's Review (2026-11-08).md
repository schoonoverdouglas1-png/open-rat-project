## 1. Phase 0 — Caseworker question (ready to read or send, Douglas's asking only)

Main question, from the plan §8:

> "If strangers send me small support payments through Ko-fi / GitHub Sponsors for my writing and research, how are those counted for my FoodShare — income in the month received, an asset after, or disregarded — and what do I need to report, and when?"

Second question, same call:

> "Does that same support money affect any other benefit I receive — by name: FoodShare, and any help with Medicare costs or any Medicaid-linked benefit? I want to name each program separately, because I understand the programs count gifts and crowdfunding differently."

Why this comes before any withdrawal pattern is set: Wisconsin policy names crowdfunding (GoFundMe, Kickstarter, Indiegogo as examples) as countable when accessible — a gift as unearned income in the month received, and an available asset in later months while still possessed — while BadgerCare Plus does **not** count gifts. Programs differ, so only his caseworker can say which rules apply to his household. This is a flag from public policy handbooks, not advice.
