**The system itself:** A serological classification from the 1930s, created by Rebecca Lancefield. It groups beta-hemolytic streptococci by the antigenic carbohydrate in their cell-wall polysaccharide — the "C carbohydrate" or "substance C" — detected by precipitation reactions with antisera, with each group assigned a letter, A through V (I and J skipped).

**The timeline for your organism, in order:**
- 1884 — Nocard and Mollereau isolate it from cows with mastitis and call it "Streptococcus de la mammite"
- 1896 — Lehmann and Neumann give it the scientific name *Streptococcus agalactiae* ("a-" + "galaktos," absence of milk)
