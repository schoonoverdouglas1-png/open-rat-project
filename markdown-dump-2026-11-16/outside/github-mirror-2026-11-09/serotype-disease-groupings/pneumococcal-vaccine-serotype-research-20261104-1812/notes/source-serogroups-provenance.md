- Geno et al. 2015, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov:443/articles/PMC4475641/ — Danish/US table; serogroup 20 = 20A (US 20) + 20B; serogroup 22 = 22F + 22A; serogroup 23 = 23F (+23A,23B in text); '6E' discussed as genetic polymorphism, not established serotype.
- SeroBA v2.0 / SeroBAnk 2025, https://rucforsk.ruc.dk/ws/files/113450431/mgen001483.pdf — 107 known serotypes; nine new since 2020: 10D,15D,20C,24C,36A,36B,33E,33G,33H; SeroBA v2.0 identifies 102/107, NOT 24B,24C,24F,7D,6H. This both updates Sunfire and leaves 24B/24C/7D/6H as partially documented (existence asserted in exception list, not fully characterised in retrieved text).
- Serogroup 15: Pimenta et al. 2021, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8091843/ — originally four (15A,15B,15C,15F), 15D added; 15B vs 15C = O-acetylation via wciZ, interconverting; 15B/C commonly reported as pair.
- Serogroup 6: Park et al. via search + Burton et al. 2016, https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jcm.03194-15 — '6E' cps locus produces polysaccharide IDENTICAL to 6B; '6E' = genetic variant 6Bii, not a distinct serotype. 6F/6G/6H are hybrid/extra members in Sunfire but SeroBA cannot identify 6H — PARTIAL.
- Serogroup 20: 2025 review https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11905375/ + MDPI 2024 https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/12/9/974 — 20A = PPSV23 strain, rare; 20B common, extra glucose side chain (whaF intact); 20C = wciG-deficient variant of 20B, serologically indistinguishable by conventional antisera. PPSV23 '20' therefore = 20A in modern terms (documented in both sources).
- Cross-reactivity review MDPI 2024 — serogroups defined as antigenically related serotypes; focus pairs 6A/6B/6C/6D, 9A/9N/9L/9V, 15A/15B/15C/15F, 19A/19F, 20A/20B, 23A/23B/23F; PCV7 6B protected vs 6A but much less vs 6C; PCV13 6A+6B gave greater OPA vs 6C; PPSV14 cross-protection assumptions for 9N and 19F FAILED; cross-group example Dob1 antibody 6B cross-reacts 6A,6B,6C,19A, and mouse 35B→29 — limited clinical evidence across serogroups.
- Mitis group: Pimenta et al. 2019, https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2018.03199/full — cps5 in S. mitis, S. oralis, S. infantis (76-99% identity, Quellung + OPK for serotype 5); additional homologues for 12F,15A,18C,33F (PCR homology only in retrieved abstract). Earlier work: non-pneumococcal strains for 2,4,9A,13,23A (read.qxmd summary of 2021 paper) — PARTIAL, second paper not opened in full. Mitis group definition: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7669753/ — Mitis group incl S. pneumoniae, S. pseudopneumoniae, S. mitis, S. oralis, S. infantis, S. peroris, S. timonensis; S. pneumoniae + S. mitis from common precursor.
- Fungi: Cryptococcus serotypes A,B,C,D (+AD) are a SEPARATE system (GXM-based), https://clinicalpub.com/cryptococcosis-cryptococcus-neoformans-and-cryptococcus-gattii/ and https://www.antiinfectivemeds.com/articles/cryptococcus-neoformans/ — var. neoformans/grubii = A,D; gattii = B,C. Only documented pneumococcus–fungus link retrieved: cryptococcal polysaccharide 'known to share some antigenic determinants with certain pneumococcal polysaccharides' (chapter summary, https://www.academia.edu/29950718/Chapter_4_The_Capsule_of_the_Fungal_Pathogen_Cryptococcus_neoformans) — serotype(s) NOT named there, so GAP, not a per-serotype fungal relationship.
