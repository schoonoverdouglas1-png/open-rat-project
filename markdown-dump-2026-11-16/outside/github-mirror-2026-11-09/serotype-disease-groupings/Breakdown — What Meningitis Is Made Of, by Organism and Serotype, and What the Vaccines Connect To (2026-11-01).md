# Breakdown — What Meningitis Is Made Of, by Organism and Serotype, and What the Vaccines Connect To (2026-11-01)

*Manuscript reference work only — NEVER medical advice, no diagnosis, no application to any person.*

**Reading flag:** "stereotypes" read here as "serotypes / serogroups" — the organism-typing terms that fit "what meningitis is made out of." Meningitis itself is not made of social stereotypes; it is a syndrome (inflammation of the meninges, the membranes around brain and spinal cord) with many different causes. In his Architecture thread, "stereotypes" separately means cognitive/social stereotypes, kept as written there — that meaning does not fit this question, so it is not used here. Correct the reading if he meant the other sense.

**Tier tags:** [D] documented · [H] his · [GAP] unknown.

## 1. First correction: meningitis is not one thing made of one thing

Meningitis = the showing (syndrome). What "makes" a case of meningitis is whichever organism — or, rarely, non-infectious cause — reaches the meninges and inflames them. So the breakdown is by cause, and inside each bacterial cause, by capsule type (serogroup or serotype), because the capsule is what the vaccines connect to.

**Simply put.** Meningitis is like a fire alarm — it tells you something reached the membranes around the brain. The breakdown below tells you which germs can set it off, which coat each germ wears, and which vaccine is built from that coat.

## 2. Bacterial meningitis — the vaccine-connected causes

### A. *Neisseria meningitidis* (meningococcal meningitis) [D]
Gram-negative diplococcus. Six disease-associated serogroups, defined by capsular polysaccharide:

| Serogroup | Capsule | Vaccine connection |
|---|---|---|
| A | (α1→6) ManNAc-phosphate, partly O-acetylated | MenACWY conjugate (capsule sugar + carrier protein) |
| B | α2→8 polysialic acid, identical to human NCAM | MenB protein vaccine (fHbp, NadA, NHBA, PorA) — capsule NOT used, because it mimics human tissue |
| C | α2→9 polysialic acid, partly O-acetylated | MenACWY conjugate |
| W | Sialic acid + D-galactose | MenACWY conjugate |
| Y | Sialic acid + D-glucose | MenACWY conjugate |
| X | [GAP — MicrobeOnline lists X as disease-associated, gives no capsule chemistry] | Not in MenACWY/MenABCWY per Oct 30 original; X-specific detail [GAP] |

Combined: MenABCWY = MenACWY components + MenB protein components, covering A, B, C, W, Y (not X).

Scientific connection, stated plainly: each conjugate vaccine *is* the serogroup's capsule sugar, chemically tied to a carrier protein so the immune system makes memory IgG against that exact capsule. MenB is the exception that proves the rule: when the capsule cannot be used, the vaccine switches to proteins.

### B. *Streptococcus pneumoniae* (pneumococcal meningitis) [D]
Gram-positive diplococcus. Ten serotypes show as meningitis in his works (from §226 / Corrected Copy, Oct 29, 2026):

**1, 3, 4, 7F, 12F, 14, 15B, 17F, 18C, 23F**

Each serotype = its own capsular polysaccharide (individual repeat-unit structures [GAP] in his works; the principle — capsule defines the serotype — is documented).

Vaccine connections [D]:
- **PPSV23** — plain capsular polysaccharides, 23 serotypes, T-cell-independent, no memory.
- **PCV13 / PCV15 / PCV20 / PCV21** — capsular polysaccharides conjugated to a carrier protein (typically CRM197), T-cell-dependent, memory IgG.
- Notes from his tables: 23F is NOT in PCV21; 14 is NOT in PCV21; 3 and 7F are in all five products yet persist as vaccine-failure/persister serotypes — inclusion ≠ control.

Scientific connection: same as meningococcal conjugates — the vaccine *is* the capsule, conjugated. The difference is only which organism's capsules are in the vial, and how many (4–5 meningococcal groups vs 13–23 pneumococcal serotypes).

### C. *Haemophilus influenzae* [D, from MicrobeOnline cross-check]
Gram-negative coccobacillus. Encapsulated serotypes a–f; **Hib = type b only**. The Hib vaccine protects type b only — nontypeable strains lack a capsule and still cause disease, and are not covered by it. Invasive disease on the page includes meningitis and epiglottitis. Requires X (hemin) and V (NAD) factors in the lab.

Scientific connection: Hib vaccine = type b capsular polysaccharide conjugated to protein — same conjugate logic, third organism.

### D. Other bacterial causes, no capsule-vaccine connection in his works [D, general]
- Group B *Streptococcus* (*Streptococcus agalactiae*) — neonatal meningitis; no vaccine in his works.
- *Listeria monocytogenes* — neonatal and immunocompromised meningitis; no vaccine.
- *Escherichia coli* and other Gram-negatives — neonatal meningitis; no vaccine.
- These are listed for completeness: a breakdown that stopped at the three vaccine-covered organisms would falsely imply meningitis is only those three. It is not.

## 3. Viral meningitis — no capsule, no bacterial vaccine connection [D, general]
Most commonly enteroviruses; also herpes simplex virus, varicella-zoster virus, arboviruses (including the California serogroup viruses in his work — La Crosse virus and Jamestown Canyon virus cause encephalitis, a brain-tissue inflammation, which is related to but distinct from meningitis, the membrane inflammation), mumps virus, and others.

Scientific connection to vaccines: **none of the bacterial capsule vaccines above connect to viral meningitis.** Viral meningitis is prevented, where prevention exists, by vaccines against the specific virus (e.g. varicella, mumps as part of MMR) — not by MenACWY, MenB, PCV, or Hib. Conflating the two is the most common category error in this area: a meningococcal vaccine does not prevent viral meningitis, and a viral infection does not carry a bacterial capsule.

## 4. Fungal meningitis — capsule chemistry again, but a different kingdom [D, general]
- *Cryptococcus neoformans / C. gattii* — capsular polysaccharide glucuronoxylomannan (GXM); the major fungal meningitis cause, especially in immunocompromise.
- *Coccidioides* spp. — coccidioidal meningitis, a complication of Valley fever in his works.
- Scientific connection to vaccines: **no routine fungal meningitis vaccine in his works.** Antifungal treatment, not vaccination, is the documented response — and the fungal capsule (GXM) is chemically distinct from bacterial capsules, so bacterial conjugate logic does not transfer to it.

## 5. Non-infectious meningitis [D, general]
Chemical irritation, certain drugs, autoimmune inflammation, and malignancy can inflame the meninges without any organism. No vaccine connection at all. Listed so the breakdown does not imply every meningitis is infectious.

## 6. Summary table — what each vaccine is scientifically connected to

| Vaccine | Connected to (capsule/protein) | Prevents meningitis from | Does NOT prevent meningitis from |
|---|---|---|---|
| MenACWY | A, C, W, Y capsular polysaccharides, conjugated | Meningococcal A, C, W, Y | MenB, MenX, pneumococcal, Hib, viral, fungal |
| MenB | B surface proteins (fHbp, NadA, NHBA, PorA) | Meningococcal B | A, C, W, Y, X, pneumococcal, Hib, viral, fungal |
| MenABCWY | Both of the above, combined | Meningococcal A, B, C, W, Y | MenX, pneumococcal, Hib, viral, fungal |
| PCV13/15/20/21 | Pneumococcal capsular polysaccharides, conjugated | Pneumococcal serotypes in that product | Meningococcal, Hib, viral, fungal |
| PPSV23 | Pneumococcal capsular polysaccharides, plain | Pneumococcal serotypes in that product (no memory) | Meningococcal, Hib, viral, fungal |
| Hib | Type b capsular polysaccharide, conjugated | *H. influenzae* type b | Other *H. influenzae* types, nontypeable strains, all others |

**The one-line version:** a vaccine is connected to a meningitis case only through the exact capsule (or protein) it is built from. Change the capsule, and you change the vaccine — and no capsule on one organism equals any capsule on another.
