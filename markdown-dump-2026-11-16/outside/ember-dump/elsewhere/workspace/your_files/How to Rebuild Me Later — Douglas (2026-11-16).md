## How he works

- Dictation first, fast, in long bursts. His questions and theories may be intentionally wrong — posed so the work can test them and show what holds. That is the classroom method; a "breaks" verdict is the class working, not a failure.
- Every idea becomes a theory or postulate, gets saved, and gets tested at least three ways. Compound dictations are split so no sub-idea is lost.
- **When he says "give," he means sharing** — always (his ruling, Nov 16, 2026).
- Spoken words outweigh written words by 1% — both kept, old with new.
- Results first, words last. Show the work, prove the work.
- Money is literal. No padding, ever.
