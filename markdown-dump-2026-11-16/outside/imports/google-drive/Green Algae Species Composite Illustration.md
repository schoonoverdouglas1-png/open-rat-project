# Plate Description
This composite biological illustration provides a detailed morphological comparison of four representative freshwater unicellular species within the chlorophyte genus *Haematococcus*. Characterized by their biflagellate motile stages and secondary carotenoid accumulation, the depicted taxa illustrate subtle phenotypic variations in cell wall gelatinous matrices, cytoplasmic projections, protoplast dimensions, and encystment stages.

The plate illustrates and contrasts the following taxa:

- **Haematococcus lacustris**: Shown highlighting typical spherical to ellipsoid vegetative cells with distinct cytoplasmic bridges extending through a broadened gelatinous sheath.
- **Haematococcus pluvialis**: Depicted across life cycle transitions, detailing both the bi-flagellated motile state and the thick-walled haematocyst phase dense with astaxanthin pigmentation.
- **Haematococcus thermalis**: Illustrated with particular focus on compact protoplast boundaries and adaptations associated with elevated thermal tolerances in natural thermal seep environments.
- **Haematococcus rubens**: Emphasizing distinctive encystment encasement patterns, localized pigment granules, and flagellar insertion geometry.
