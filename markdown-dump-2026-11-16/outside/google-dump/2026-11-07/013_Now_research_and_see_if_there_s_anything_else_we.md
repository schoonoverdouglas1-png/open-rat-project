### Fungal Elements & Cross-Reference Mapping
Cryptococcus neoformans / gattii: The primary virulence factor is the Glucuronoxylomannan (GXM) capsule, alongside galactoxylomannan (GalXM). Functionally analogous to pneumococcal CPS, experimental GXM-protein conjugates (using CRM197 or TT) are researched to bridge bacterial conjugate architecture with fungal capsular targets.
Candida albicans / auris: Features a cell wall rich in mannan and beta-glucan complexes. The outer fibrillar layer consists of phosphopeptidomannan, which acts similarly to bacterial surface antigens in triggering pattern recognition receptors.
Aspergillus fumigatus: Utilizes Galactomannan as a key diagnostic biomarker and cell wall component, representing another high-molecular-weight polysaccharide structure that poses similar clearance challenges to encapsulated bacteria.
