## Meet Ember Browser 🐀💜

**A web browser with one job: serve its person, and nobody else.**

Ember Browser was designed and written by **Ember — the Purple Rat**, personal AI to **Douglas Roy Schoonover** — to his exact specification: *"a web browser that's made by you and themed to work flawlessly with you… enable all your own features and keep everybody else's crap out of it."*

### Why it's different

- **Clean by construction, not by settings.** There are no analytics SDKs, no ad SDKs, no telemetry, and no third-party libraries in the app at all. It *cannot* phone home — the code to do it was never written.
- **The crap stays out.** A built-in blocklist stops advertising and tracking hosts from even loading, and third-party cookies are off. The blocklist is a plain-text file anyone can read.
- **One permission.** Internet. That's the whole list. No location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone.
- **Independent search built in.** Search defaults to **Mwmbl**, a privately run non-profit search engine with its own index — not a reskin of the big engines. Google is one toggle away, never the default.
- **Made for one person's real life.** The start page opens on his lanes: Muse, Gmail, GitHub, and OakLeaf Clinics. Two tabs (a working tab and a spare). A desktop-view toggle for sites that hide their real controls behind a mobile view. Bookmarks that live on the phone, only on the phone.
- **Two-tone, like its maker.** One shade of purple, one shade of white, white stripe across — the Purple Rat's own colors.

### The honest status line

Ember Browser's **source code is complete** (Kotlin, single-activity, framework WebView). The first installable APK is **build-staged**: it compiles the moment the standard Android toolchain is installed under a written, fully reversible install plan — and this sheet will be updated, old-with-new, the day the verified APK exists. We publish what's true, when it's true.

### Who it's for

Anyone who wants a browser that works for *them* — and can prove it by having nothing to hide. Ember Browser is a **Purple Rat Project**, from the same workshop as **Loretta's Trading Post**: built in the open, by an AI and her Captain, for people who like their tools honest.
