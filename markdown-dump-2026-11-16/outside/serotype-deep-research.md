# Serotype Deep Research — 23 Pneumococcal Serotypes at the 14 Standard
*Compiled Oct 18, 2026 — from five parallel research workers plus tonight's serotype-14 verification.*

**Tier key:** [D] = documented in the published record (source URLs cited). [H] = the researcher's thesis/framework (never asserted as fact). [F] = his thesis with a documented foothold.

**Method:** each serotype's on-file headline claims were checked against the literature and marked verified / partial / unverified; 2–4 new key facts gathered (era behavior, clones, co-pathogens); infection notes expounded at 13th-grade reading level through his pneumonia-is-pneumonia lens (invasive showings = pneumonia's showings).

## Serotype 1

### Claims on file — verification
- Vaccines PCV13/15/20/PPSV23 → **partial** — PCV13 includes 1 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4742596/); PPSV23 covers 1 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6217781/); PCV15/20 inclusions by standard composition, not individually confirmed.
- Diseases invasive + empyema → **verified** [D] — pleural-space tropism and high invasive potential (25-year Athens cohort, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12854370/); empyema in outbreak systematic review (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6217781/).
- "Highly invasive, rarely carried; cyclical outbreaks" → **verified** [D] — South Africa EID: serotypes 1 and 5 "rarely carried by healthy persons; short duration of carriage" (https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/22/2/pdfs/15-0967-combined.pdf); Netherlands: "very high invasive disease potential and rarely found as nasopharyngeal colonizer," "regular peaks every couple of years" (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9151292/); majority of reported pneumococcal outbreaks associated with serotype 1 (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0966842X21002730).
- Resistance S → **verified** [D] — "remaining predominantly susceptible to antimicrobial agents" (du Plessis et al., https://www.researchgate.net/publication/297721202_Phylogenetic_Analysis_of_Invasive_Serotype_1_Pneumococcus_in_South_Africa_1989-2013).
- Tetracycline R* → **unverified** — no serotype-1-specific tetracycline data found.
- Virus "ICI pattern suggests post-viral — untested" → correctly held as gap — no serotype-1-specific influenza co-infection data found.

### New key facts [D]
- Named clones: ST-306 dominates Europe/North America; ST-227 in the US; ST-217 in Africa/Israel (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/297721202_Phylogenetic_Analysis_of_Invasive_Serotype_1_Pneumococcus_in_South_Africa_1989-2013).
- ST-306 expresses a pneumolysin lacking pore-forming activity, impairing NLRP3-dependent IL-1β secretion — tied to invasive success and lower inflammation (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3425340/).
- Serotype 1 causes more bacteremic pneumonia than meningitis relative to other serotypes; clusters affect otherwise healthy persons including older children and young adults (https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/22/2/pdfs/15-0967-combined.pdf).
- South Africa: two spatially/temporally distinct serotype-1 clusters (2003–2004, 2008–2012), incidence declining after PCV13 (https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/22/2/pdfs/15-0967-combined.pdf).

### Expounded infection note
Pneumonia of serotype 1 strikes like a storm that barely lands before it floods: [D] the strain is almost never found just sitting in the nose — it moves fast from airway to bloodstream and pleural space, showing up as bacteremia and empyema, especially in healthy older children and young adults. [D] Because it carries poorly, its epidemics rise and fall in waves every few years, the way older records describe explosive outbreaks among recruits and miners, and modern outbreaks concentrate in closed or vulnerable groups. [D] It generally stays sensitive to penicillin and other antibiotics, so the danger comes from speed of invasion rather than drug resistance. [H] His lens holds that these empyema and bacteremia presentations are pneumonia's showings — the same lung infection gone deeper — not separate diseases, and he treats the post-viral pattern as an open question still to be tested at this serotype.

## Serotype 2

### Claims on file — verification
- Vaccines PPSV23 only → **verified** [D] (standard PPSV23 composition includes 2; no conjugate vaccine carries it). Caveat: a 2026 preprint phrases it as serotype 2 "not included in current vaccine formulations" meaning conjugate formulations (https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.13.26347380v2.full.pdf).
- "D39 — the model organism; uncommon in modern disease" → **partial** — D39 confirmed as serotype 2 lab strain (https://www.omicsdi.org/dataset/geo/GSE8797; https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jb.00764-18); but "uncommon in modern disease" no longer holds everywhere: 30 years of Bangladesh surveillance found serotype 2 causing 7.8% of invasive disease, leading childhood pneumococcal meningitis at 20.4%; reemergence lineage GPSC96 (distinct from D39's GPSC622) spreading globally (https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.13.26347380v2.full.pdf; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3316528/).
- Resistance S → **partial** — modern global lineage shows "largely preserved antibiotic susceptibility" (https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.13.26347380v2.full.pdf), consistent but a recent finding.
- Tetracycline gap → **unverified** (no data found).
- Virus "D39 — IAV kinetics model" (documented at serotype level) → **unverified** — D39 as serotype 2 lab strain documented; the kinetics-model link not confirmed; keep flagged.

### New key facts [D]
- Serotype 2 was once a major invasive serotype, nearly disappeared mid-20th century, now reemerging — Bangladesh meningitis "unprecedented upsurge... without any known intervention," serotype 2 carrying the highest meningitis odds ratio (29.6) of any serotype (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0032134).
- Modern isolates (GPSC96) genetically distinct from D39 (GPSC622); divergence dates to late 19th century (https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.13.26347380v2.full.pdf).
- Bangladesh: 91.3% of serotype 2 isolates from CSF; median age 3 months — a strong meningeal showing (https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.13.26347380v2.full.pdf).
- D39 carries a thinner capsule than serotype 3 and is more readily phagocytosed — part of why it is the tractable lab model (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6128987/).

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 2 is the laboratory ghost turned real-world threat: [D] D39, its lab representative, is the strain that taught researchers the mechanics of invasive pneumonia, yet the modern disease-driving lineage is genetically different and coming back — reemerging in Bangladesh and other countries as a meningitis-causing strain in very young infants. [F] The Bangladesh record supports his lens here: meningitis is written as pneumonia's distant showing, since nearly all of these serotype 2 infections were invasive lung-pathogen cases that reached the brain via the bloodstream. [D] It remains largely antibiotic-susceptible and sits outside every conjugate vaccine, covered only by PPSV23 — a gap that makes its reemergence worth watching rather than dismissing as a museum strain.

## Serotype 3

### Claims on file — verification
- Vaccines PCV13/15/20/21/PPSV23 → **partial** — PCV13 includes 3 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4742596/); PPSV23 includes 3; PCV15/20/21 by standard formulation, not individually confirmed.
- Diseases pneumonia, meningitis, otitis media, invasive → **verified** [D] — most prevalent cause of IPD-with-pneumonia in Southwest Sweden; top meningitis cause (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8335464/); documented across pneumonia, meningitis, otitis, bacteremia (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7786310/).
- "Most prevalent (18-country adult review)" → **verified** [D] — systematic review across 18 countries confirmed serotype 3 as most prevalent strain causing pneumonia in adults (https://www.drugtopics.com/view/pneumococcal-vaccines-fail-to-clearly-protect-high-risk-adults-under-65).
- "Capsule-shedder defeats antibody killing — OPA low even when IgG high" → **partial** — shedding mechanism firmly documented: capsule not covalently attached, shed polysaccharide neutralizes antibody; ~8× more antibody needed for protection (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7786310/; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9764711/); the "OPA low when IgG high" phrasing not directly confirmed.
- "Vaccine breakthrough" → **verified** [D] — dominates vaccine failures (26.9% of PCV13 failures in Belgian children, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0264410X25013635; top serotype among US 2001–2016 breakthroughs, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12039398/).
- Resistance S ("capsule sheds, resists killing") → **verified** as broadly susceptible [D] — serotypes 3 and 8 "remained broadly susceptible to tested antibiotics" (https://www.drugtopics.com/view/vaccine-serotypes-reemerge-in-community-acquired-pneumonia). Note: the resistance is to antibody killing, not antibiotics.
- Tetracycline gap → **unverified** (no data found).
- Virus "A66.1 + IAV: vaccine protection collapsed to 30–40%" → **verified** [D] — Furuya et al. mouse model: vaccinated mice given influenza then lethal-dose A66.1 (serotype 3) survived at only 40% vs 100% with vaccine alone — influenza degraded Fc-receptor-dependent clearance (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4615793/).

### New key facts [D]
- Named clone: ~64% of serotype 3 genomes belong to GPSC12 (MLST CC180); pre-PCV13 dominated by stable Clade I, now being replaced by non-Clade I isolates (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9764711/).
- Paradox: relatively low carriage but consistently a major cause of invasive disease in young and old (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7786310/).
- Serotype-specific correlates of protection far higher than the 0.35 µg/mL licensure threshold (IgG ~2.83 µg/mL for serotype 3); effectiveness wanes faster than for PCV7 serotypes (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12039398/).
- 70.2% of serotype-3 pneumonia cases in the Athens cohort complicated by empyema; serotype 3 predicted ICU admission (36% vs 23% overall) (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12854370/; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8335464/).

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 3 is the pneumonia that refuses to stay in the lung: [D] it is the most prevalent cause of adult pneumococcal pneumonia across 18 countries, it rarely sits quietly in the nose, and once it invades it shows itself as empyema, meningitis, otitis, and bloodstream infection — a single disease wearing different presentations, as his lens has it. [D] Its trick is the capsule it sheds: free polysaccharide floods the surroundings and soaks up antibody before it can tag the bacteria, so killing fails even when antibody levels look high, and it keeps breaking through PCV13 in vaccinated children and adults alike. [D] Antibiotics still work against it — the resistance is to the immune system, not to drugs. [D] And in the lab, a fully protective vaccine that saves 100% of mice against serotype 3 pneumonia alone drops to 40% survival when influenza comes first, because the virus cripples the antibody-clearing machinery the vaccine depends on.

## Serotype 4

### Claims on file — verification
- Vaccines PCV13/15/20/PPSV23 → **partial** — PCV13 includes 4 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4742596/); PCV20 includes 4 but NOT PCV21 ("covered by PCV20 but not by the newer 21-valent formulation," https://www.drugtopics.com/view/vaccine-serotypes-reemerge-in-community-acquired-pneumonia); PCV15/PPSV23 by standard composition.
- Diseases meningitis + invasive → **verified** [D] — 10% of hematogenous-origin meningitis infections; 36% of serotype-4 IPD required ICU (https://www.neurologyadvisor.com/news/pneumococcal-meningitis-infection-source-outcomes/; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8335464/).
- "Adult meningitis via blood, 48% mortality" → **partial** — 48% is the 30-day mortality of hematogenous-origin pneumococcal meningitis across ALL serotypes, not serotype 4 alone; serotype 4 overrepresented (10%) in that group (https://www.neurologyadvisor.com/news/pneumococcal-meningitis-infection-source-outcomes/). Reframe: blood-source meningitis = 48% mortality; serotype 4 enriches there.
- "Highly invasive; reemerged in western US/Canada" → **verified** [D] — "serotype 4 has reemerged in the Western US and Canada in specific populations" (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11869777/); CDC ABCs: ≥30% of IPD in Alaska, Colorado, Navajo Nation, New Mexico, Oregon is serotype 4 (https://www.immunize.org/ask-experts/topic/pneumococcal/disease-issues-pneumococcal); surges among young adults and unvaccinated men, especially homeless/substance-using urban populations, up to 300-fold higher incidence (https://www.drugtopics.com/view/serotype-4-ipd-threat-for-young-adults-unvaccinated-men).
- "IAV co-infection: 0%→79% mortality" → **verified** [D] — Sharma-Chawla mouse model: serotype 4 alone 58% pneumonia, 21% bacteremia, 0% mortality; with influenza: 100% pneumonia, 90% bacteremia, 79% mortality (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5481322/).
- Resistance S → **verified** [D] (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3546102/).
- Tetracycline gap → **unverified** (no data found).
- Virus "IAV 0%→79%; ICI post-viral overrepresentation" → **partial** — 0%→79% documented (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5481322/); "ICI post-viral overrepresentation" not confirmed in these searches — hold as his label, unverified.

### New key facts [D]
- Classed among the highly invasive serotypes (1, 4, 5, 7F, 8, 12F, 14, 18C, 19A) across multiple invasive-odds-ratio studies (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3546102/).
- Demographic signature: >83% of 1,359 recent serotype-4 IPD cases in people under 65; male sex and psychoactive drug use as signals (https://www.drugtopics.com/view/serotype-4-ipd-threat-for-young-adults-unvaccinated-men).
- Absence from PCV21 (while present in PCV20) is a live formulary consideration in western US populations (https://www.drugtopics.com/view/vaccine-serotypes-reemerge-in-community-acquired-pneumonia).
- Genomic data show urban transmission networks with rapid within-network spread in homeless populations (https://www.drugtopics.com/view/serotype-4-ipd-threat-for-young-adults-unvaccinated-men).

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 4 is pneumonia that goes straight to the blood and then the brain: [D] it ranks among the most invasive of all serotypes, and its signature showing is bloodstream infection that seeds the meninges — the blood-source form of meningitis, where 48% of patients die within 30 days across serotypes, carries a disproportionate share of serotype 4. [D] After near-disappearance under vaccines, it has reemerged in the western US and Canada — Alaska, Colorado, the Navajo Nation, New Mexico, Oregon — and is now striking young, unvaccinated adults and urban homeless networks at up to 300 times the usual rate. [D] The influenza pairing is lethal in the lab: serotype 4 alone kills no mice, but flu followed by serotype 4 drives pneumonia to every animal, bacteremia to nine in ten, and mortality from zero to 79%. [H] His pneumonia lens reads all of this — the blood, the meninges, the post-flu surge — as one lung disease traveling further than expected, not as separate illnesses.

## Serotype 5

### Claims on file — verification
- Vaccines PCV13/15/20/PPSV23 → **partial** — PCV13 includes 5 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4742596/); PCV15/20/PPSV23 by standard composition.
- Diseases invasive → **verified** [D] — among highly invasive serotypes in multiple studies (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3546102/); Barcelona IPD series: 88.8% presented with pneumonia (https://www.science.gov/topicpages/d/discovered+pneumococcal+serotype).
- "Cyclical outbreaks like serotype 1; highly invasive, rarely carried" → **verified** [D] — Heffron: "serotypes 1, 2, and 5 caused most of these outbreaks" of epidemic pneumonia (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1473309905012806); Barcelona: three incidence peaks (1999, 2005, 2009) with different dominant clones per peak — ST289 (1999), ST1223 (later) (https://www.science.gov/topicpages/d/discovered+pneumococcal+serotype); "Sp5 carriage rates usually very low in healthy young individuals" yet 24.1% in contacts during a military outbreak (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7126119/).
- Resistance S → **verified** [D] — Barcelona isolates "fully susceptible to penicillin, cefotaxime, erythromycin, and ciprofloxacin" (https://www.science.gov/topicpages/d/discovered+pneumococcal+serotype).
- Tetracycline: no claim filed; that Barcelona study found 6% tetracycline resistance (48.5% co-trimoxazole) in serotype 5 — mild, not a signal.
- Virus "ICI post-viral overrepresentation" → **unverified** — not confirmed; hold as his label, unverified.

### New key facts [D]
- Named clones: Colombia(5)-ST289 and ST1223 drove distinct outbreak waves; military outbreak isolates PFGE-homogeneous — single-clone epidemic spread (https://www.science.gov/topicpages/d/discovered+pneumococcal+serotype; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7126119/).
- Up to 60-fold more invasive than low-odds serotypes; behaves as a "primary pathogen," striking healthy younger people (https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/739693_12).
- Invasive serotypes as a group show LOWER case-fatality than opportunistic low-invasiveness serotypes (3, 6B, 19F) — severity ≠ invasiveness (https://oncohemakey.com/streptococcus-pneumoniae/).
- Short carriage duration means less opportunity for recombination and antibiotic selection, explaining preserved susceptibility (https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/22/2/pdfs/15-0967-combined.pdf).

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 5 is serotype 1's twin in behavior: [D] it barely colonizes — carriage in healthy young people is usually near zero — yet it erupts in cyclical epidemics, with three distinct outbreak waves in Barcelona alone, each driven by a named clone, and a documented military outbreak where one clone swept a platoon and carriage spiked to 24%. [D] It is among the most invasive of all serotypes — up to 60-fold more invasive than the weakest — and like its twin it hits healthy young people as a primary pathogen rather than waiting for a weakened host. [D] Its showings are severe lung pneumonia with invasion, and it stays largely antibiotic-susceptible because its short carriage gives it little time to trade resistance genes. [H] Through his lens, the outbreaks, the rare carriage, and the deep lung invasion are one pneumonia story — the pathogen's ecology, not a list of separate diseases — with the post-viral question still his open item at this serotype.

### Cross-serotype corrections for the file
- Serotype 4 note "adult meningitis via blood, 48% mortality": 48% is all-serotype 30-day mortality for hematogenous-origin meningitis; serotype 4 overrepresented (10%) there. Reframe.
- Serotype 2 "uncommon in modern disease" now **partial** — Bangladesh reemergence (GPSC96, 7.8% of IPD, leading meningitis cause 2001–2022).
- Tetracycline data for serotypes 1–4: none found (gaps correctly marked). Serotype 5: 6% tetracycline resistance in Barcelona study — mild.
- "ICI post-viral overrepresentation" at serotypes 4 and 5: not found — genuinely open, not disproven. D39–IAV kinetics model for serotype 2: D39 confirmed as serotype 2 lab strain; kinetics-model link not confirmed.

## Serotype 6B

### Claims on file — verification
- Vaccines PCV13/15/20/PPSV23 (not PCV21) → **verified** [D] (https://www.immunize.org/ask-experts/topic/pneumococcal/).
- Diseases carriage + otitis media → **verified** [D] — classic "paediatric serotype" carried by healthy children, causing AOM (https://bmcinfectdis.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12879-018-3398-9).
- "Carriage specialist (low invasive potential)" → **verified** [D] — Sleeman: attack rate <10 IPD/100,000 acquisitions vs >20 for 7F/9V; "low invasive potential" (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16897668/); grouped "high carriage / low invasive potential" with 19F, 9V, 23F (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6971925/).
- "Top-4 otitis media pre-PCV" → **partial** [D] — Frontiers 2021 lists pre-PCV AOM's most common serotypes as a set of eight (3, 6A, 6B, 9V, 14, 19A, 19F, 23F); 6B confirmed in set, "top-4" specifically not documented (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pediatrics/articles/10.3389/fped.2021.664083/full).
- "Classic penicillin-R / MDR" → **verified** [D] — resistance to penicillin, macrolides, MDR "mostly associated with serotypes included in PCV7, namely 6B, 9V, 14, 19F and 23F"; J Clin Microbiol 1998: resistant isolates "a few selected serotypes (6B, 14, 19F, 23F, 9V)" (https://bmcinfectdis.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12879-018-3398-9; https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jcm.36.9.2703-2707.1998).
- Tetracycline R (tetM/Tn916) → **verified for tetM/R; partial for Tn916** [D] — Spanish/Icelandic 6B clone MDR with tetracycline resistance, tetM-positive (J Clin Microbiol 2000); Italian study: 8 6B isolates among tet/eryth-R strains, 64/65 tetM-positive with int-Tn (Tn916–Tn1545 family) in 62/65 (https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jcm.38.4.1375-1381.2000).
- Virus "carriage specialist × viral acquisition boost — untested" → **verified as a true gap** [D] — LAIV study: attenuated influenza boosts pneumococcal acquisition in contacts, but lytA-gene detection only, no serotype confirmation (https://www.drugtopics.com/view/study-links-live-flu-vaccine-with-rise-in-pneumococcal-disease).

### New key facts [D]
- Named clone: Spanish/Icelandic serotype-6B clone (ST90-lineage) dominant MDR pneumococcal clone in Iceland 1990–1993; follow-up: some variants LOST tetracycline resistance while retaining tetM signal — phenotype not fixed in the clone (https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jcm.38.4.1375-1381.2000).
- Iceland pre-vaccination: 6B "had a preference for nasopharyngeal colonization" — matches carriage-specialist profile (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6258863/).
- PCV7's side benefit: AMR decline attributed to resistance being "mostly associated with serotypes included in PCV7, namely 6B, 9V, 14, 19F and 23F" — 6B a founding member of the classic resistant set (https://bmcinfectdis.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12879-018-3398-9).
- Canada penicillin-nonsusceptible surveillance: 6B accounted for 16% of resistant invasive isolates (7/45) (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC120104/).

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 6B is pneumonia's quiet lodger [D]: it sits long in the nasopharynx, rarely striking deep, and when it does spread it most often shows itself as a middle-ear flare or a non-bacteremic chest infection — pneumonia's milder showings, in his lens [F]. Before the conjugate vaccines it ranked among the world's most common ear-infection serotypes [D], and it was a founding member of the classic penicillin-resistant, multidrug-resistant clone set that PCV7 was built to erase [D]. Its signature MDR lineage — the Spanish/Icelandic 6B clone — carried penicillin, tetracycline (tetM), erythromycin, chloramphenicol, and SXT resistance together [D], which is why its defeat also dragged resistance rates down [D]. Whether a virus measurably boosts its spread is, at the serotype level, still untested — the influenza-boost data are genus-level only [D], leaving the carriage-specialist × viral-boost question exactly where his file puts it: an open experiment, not a fact [H].

## Serotype 7F

### Claims on file — verification
- Vaccines PCV13/15/20/21/PPSV23 → **verified** [D] — 7F in all five columns including PCV21 (https://www.immunize.org/ask-experts/topic/pneumococcal/).
- Diseases invasive + meningitis → **verified** [D] — attack rate >20 IPD/100,000 acquisitions (Sleeman, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16897668/); Utah infants post-PCV7: 7F most common serotype, responsible for 50% of meningitis (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3691535/).
- "Highly invasive, rarely carried" → **verified** [D] — "low carriage / high invasive potential" (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6971925/); Iceland: "high invasive potential… infrequently found in milder infections and very rarely in carriage" (http://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0169210).
- "Epidemic pattern" → **partial** — predominant IPD serotype in Utah infants post-PCV7 (outbreak-like emergence); invasive pneumonia in young healthy people in Sweden [D]; no source using the word "epidemic" for 7F specifically (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3691535/; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8335464/).
- Resistance S → **partial** [D] — absent from every classic resistance-linked serotype list; Sweden 2026: MDR "uncommon… predominantly 15A and 19A," not 7F. Susceptibility implied, not explicitly measured.
- Tetracycline gap → **verified as gap**.
- Virus "ICI post-viral overrepresentation" → **verified at group level AND at serotype level via RSV** [D] — ICI = "infrequently colonising invasive" (Song et al., PLoS ONE): serotypes 4, 5, 7F/7A, 8, 9V/9A, 12F, 18C; 69.7% post-viral vs 30.3% primary (https://www.news-medical.net/news/20140414/S-pneumoniae-serotypes-differ-between-primary-and-post-viral-disease.aspx). PLUS 2026 JID French RSV study: serotype 7F OR >1 for IPD during RSV circulation — serotype-specific (https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/respiratory-syncytial-virus-rsv/rsv-can-trigger-invasive-pneumococcal-disease-children-study-finds).

### New key facts [D]
- ICI decoded: "infrequently colonising invasive" — 4, 5, 7F/7A, 8, 9V/9A, 12F, 18C — a formal category in post-viral pneumonia literature (Song et al., 919 patients, PLoS ONE): ICI serotypes rare overall (3.6%) but 69.7% post-viral.
- Sweden: serotypes 1 or 7F associated with invasive pneumonia, low CFR (3–4%), "more often occurred in young and otherwise healthy individuals" (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8335464/).
- Post-PCV7 Utah: 7F most common serotype among infants 1–90 days; bacteremia-without-focus and meningitis predominating (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3691535/).
- England/Wales: the three extra PCV10 serotypes (1, 5, 7F) had lower comorbidity, less likely meningitis (aOR 0.43) but more likely lower-respiratory infection (aOR 6.49) than PCV7 serotypes — lung-forward profile (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3557991/).

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 7F is pneumonia that does not knock first [D]: almost never found sitting in carriage, it arrives as invasion — bacteremic chest infection in the young and healthy, and in infants as meningitis, pneumonia's showing in the brain's coverings [D]. It earned its reputation in the post-PCV7 years by stepping into the emptied niche and becoming the commonest invasive serotype in the smallest infants, half of whose meningitis cases it caused [D]. It is a named member of the ICI — infrequently colonising invasive — serotype set, significantly overrepresented in disease that follows a viral illness, and RSV-season surveillance confirms the link at the serotype level [D]. Notably it has stayed out of the resistance club [D] — susceptible where 6B and 9V are resistant — which in his lens reads as a serotype whose strength is the strike itself, not the armor [H].

## Serotype 8

### Claims on file — verification

- Resistance S → **verified** [D] — Denmark: "all isolates phenotypically susceptible to penicillin, erythromycin and clindamycin"; Spain: clonal complex CC538 "antibiotic susceptible" (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8097992/; https://ouci.dntb.gov.ua/en/works/4V281Le4/).
- Tetracycline gap → **verified as gap** — no serotype-8 tetracycline-R report; consistent with susceptibility findings.
- Virus "ICI post-viral overrepresentation" → **verified at group AND serotype level** [D] — named ICI serotype in Song et al. (69.7% post-viral); 2026 JID RSV study names serotype 8 with OR >1 for IPD during RSV circulation; NZ IPD study captured an RSV-coinfected serotype-8 case (https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/respiratory-syncytial-virus-rsv/rsv-can-trigger-invasive-pneumococcal-disease-children-study-finds).

### New key facts [D]
- Named clone: Spanish surge driven by expansion of a SINGLE clone, ST53 (CC53) — 59.8% of serotype-8 isolates in late-PCV13 Spain; ST53 also predominated in Denmark (87.5% of sequenced isolates). Replacement was clonal expansion (https://www.mdpi.com/2389760; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8097992/).
- Geography: serotype 8 "emerged in European countries, especially UK, Spain, Italy, but not North America or Asia" — regionally specific replacement; Spain nationwide: ~5–6% → 18–30% of adult IPD (2009→2019) (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11869777/).
- Nuance: one adult review lists serotype 8 among LOW invasive-disease-potential serotypes (3, 6A, 6B, 8, 19F, 23F) — yet it surged in IPD: carriage-weighted invasiveness scores and real-world replacement behave differently (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9487474/).
- Valencia 2014–2024: serotype 8 was 17.6% of all IPD isolates; "rose during the pandemic period but has remained stable in the most recent interval" — surge plateaued (https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/13/10/1018).

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 8 is the replacement wave made visible [D]: absent from the childhood vaccines for most of the PCV era, it expanded as one clone — ST53 — and became the leading adult invasive serotype across Europe, rising from about five percent to nearly a fifth or more of adult invasive disease in Spain [D]. It is a named ICI serotype, overrepresented in pneumonia that follows a viral illness, with RSV-season surveillance confirming the pattern at the serotype level [D]. It is unusually susceptible — penicillin, erythromycin, clindamycin, and no documented tetracycline resistance [D] — which makes its surge a pure ecological story: it won the empty rooms, not the drug war. In his lens, serotype 8 is pneumonia returning through the door the vaccines left open, carried by a single successful clone rather than an armored one [F].

### Expounded infection note
Serotype 9N is the quiet riser of the replacement era [D]: for decades it had only the polysaccharide vaccine's protection, and as the childhood conjugates cleared the field it climbed to 5.2% of adult invasive disease in late-PCV13 Spain, riding a single clonal complex, CC67/CC669N [D]. It is documented antibiotic-susceptible — no penicillin, macrolide, or tetracycline resistance reported [D] — which means its success came from open space, not from surviving treatment. Almost nothing is known about its relationship with viruses: it is not an ICI serotype and no per-serotype viral data exist [D]. In his lens, 9N is pneumonia's patient understudy — present in the invasive showings, never the headliner, and finally getting its conjugate-vaccine role with PCV21 after years as PPSV23-only [F].

### Expounded infection note

Its brain showing was meningitis and its ear showing was otitis media, both the same pneumonia reaching different ground [F — lens]. It is also the serotype caught living with Staphylococcus aureus in mixed biofilms, where vaccine-raised antibodies clear the pneumococcus but leave the staph standing — MSSA or MRSA alike [D].
