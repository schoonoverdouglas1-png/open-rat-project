# Scripts — Test Meta AI and Gemini, Same Serotype and Disease Grouping Experiment

**Purpose:** Douglas wants to run the same test he just ran on Duck.ai, but in Meta AI and in Gemini, himself, and then bring the answers back to Ember for assimilation. Ember is helping him be the assistant to those two AIs — that is, he is the one who feeds them the prompt, records which model answered, and brings the result back here.
**What NOT to paste:** No personal medical material, no lab files, no family material, no dates of birth, no medical record numbers. Only the grouping summary below, which is research material already on file.
**How to use:** Copy Script A into Meta AI, copy Script B into Gemini, separately. Do not merge them into one chat. Record: (1) which model actually answered, if shown, (2) whether web search was used, (3) what it said it could not find. Then paste or share each answer back to Ember here, and she will assimilate it old-with-new, with disagreements shown, not smoothed over.

## Script A — For Meta AI

Copy everything between the lines below into a new Meta AI chat:

---
Please analyze pneumococcal serotype groupings and disease groupings from this research summary, using web search if available in this chat.

Research summary:
- Union of 32 serotypes across PCV7, PCV13, PCV15, PCV20, PCV21, and PPSV23.
- PN23M list: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6B, 7F, 8, 9N, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15B, 17F, 18C, 19A, 19F, 20, 22F, 23F, 33F.
- Serogroups are antigenic groupings, not genetic clusters. Examples: group 9 = 9A, 9L, 9N, 9V; group 33 = 33F, 33A, 33B, 33C, 33D, plus 33E/G/H in newer work; group 6 = 6A, 6B, 6C, 6D, 6F, 6G, 6H, with 6E refuted; group 15 = 15A, 15B, 15C, 15D; group 20 = 20A, 20B, 20C.
- Disease groupings: pneumonia, meningitis, otitis media, invasive disease, with serotype patterns by disease.
- Parallel systems, for comparison only, not interchangeable: meningococcus A/B/C/W/Y/X, Hib a-f, Salmonella serovars, E. coli O:H, Cryptococcus A/B/C/D/AD, GBS Ia-IX, measles as a single-serotype contrast.

Rules for your answer:
1. Distinguish three kinds of commonality separately, in three separate sections: (a) molecular, (b) antigenic, (c) vaccine. Do not assume cross-reactivity from molecular similarity alone.
2. For disease patterns, name the population and period you are describing, if you know it. Do not give a single universal disease ranking without naming population and period.
3. Tell me, at the end, in a separate section: (a) what model you are, if you can say so, (b) whether you used web search in this chat, (c) what you could NOT find, and (d) where you disagree with the summary above, if anywhere.
4. Do not change any settings, do not ask me to log in, and do not use any personal medical information — none is included in this prompt.
