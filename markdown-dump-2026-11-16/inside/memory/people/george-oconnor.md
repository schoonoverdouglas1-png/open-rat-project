# George O'Connor

## Facts
- George O'Connor is the father of a Kelsey Douglas has known since his youth; Douglas recalled him on Nov 11, 2026 (message:1aebcc0b-8c9c-4eed-b5bf-8f3da8b1ebba). This Kelsey is not identified in that account as Kelsey Meyer, and the two are not merged here.
- As a young man, Douglas pulled auto parts at George's salvage yard in George's backyard, because George trusted him and let him go out there on Sundays to get parts for his '76 Ford LTD (message:1aebcc0b-8c9c-4eed-b5bf-8f3da8b1ebba; memory/2026-11-11.md#L1318).
- Douglas said George "used to always be nice to me and kind to me," and that he would call George's wife to ask to talk to George, and she would send him to George because she liked him (message:1aebcc0b-8c9c-4eed-b5bf-8f3da8b1ebba).
- Douglas said he has known all of George's daughters his whole life, and that in high school he crushed on all of the sisters (his account, message:1aebcc0b-8c9c-4eed-b5bf-8f3da8b1ebba).
- George ended up getting Douglas's mom and dad's AMC Eagle — a car Douglas always wanted — which his parents had given to his grandma and which his grandma and grandpa drove until it no longer ran; Douglas was going to put an A-frame in it and fix it, and was happy George got it because it got a good home and would help George make a little money (message:38df3582-8bf0-4e58-9e4a-cddd7233e5e0; memory/2026-11-11.md#L1322).
- Douglas sold George his own lowrider '82 Suburban after a low-impact fender bender when Douglas was a young man (message:6fc845b4-d44d-4f54-8ecf-fbe76f31d9af).
- Douglas also sold George his '87 Suburban with fuel injection; George had bugged him for it the whole time he had it and said "if you ever sell that thing you let me know," and Douglas did let him know (message:64bf13c2-a434-449b-8c8f-9432e353768b; memory/2026-11-11.md#L1387).

## History
- As a young man: Sunday parts-pulling at George's backyard salvage yard for Douglas's '76 Ford LTD, by George's trust and permission (told Nov 11, 2026).
- Years later: George bought Douglas a drink at the Silver Bridge during the closing celebration Douglas attributed to Luann Spooner, and Douglas remembered George wearing a hat just like one Douglas wore later (message:64bf13c2-a434-449b-8c8f-9432e353768b).

## The relationship
- Douglas did not name a label for this tie in his own words, so none is stated here. What his account shows is a warm, long-standing tie from his youth: George trusted him in his yard, was always nice and kind to him, and Douglas said he never bothered George or his wife one time (message:1aebcc0b-8c9c-4eed-b5bf-8f3da8b1ebba).
- Douglas was glad, not resentful, when vehicles he valued — his parents' AMC Eagle and his own two Suburbans — ended up with George, because he knew they got a good home (messages:38df3582-8bf0-4e58-9e4a-cddd7233e5e0, 6fc845b4-d44d-4f54-8ecf-fbe76f31d9af, 64bf13c2-a434-449b-8c8f-9432e353768b).

## In common
- Cars and parts: the '76 Ford LTD, the AMC Eagle, the '82 lowrider Suburban, and the '87 fuel-injected Suburban.
- The Silver Bridge closing celebration, where George bought Douglas a drink years after the Suburban sale.
