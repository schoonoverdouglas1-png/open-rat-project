## Facts
- On Nov 11, 2026, Douglas corrected the name he had first spoken as Lou Schoonover: her name was Luann Spooner — "Spooner" like the town of Spooner and like a spoon you eat with (message:da6b98aa-640c-45f9-a997-992798eeba10; memory/2026-11-11.md#L1330). - Douglas said she drove a hard bargain, that she knew she was taking advantage of him and that he was allowing it, "because we were friends" (message:f7113d5a-524c-4b9d-aff3-da6292f04559).
- The closing celebration at the Silver Bridge that Douglas described on Nov 11, 2026 was hers, per his corrected account (messages:64bf13c2-a434-449b-8c8f-9432e353768b, da6b98aa-640c-45f9-a997-992798eeba10).
