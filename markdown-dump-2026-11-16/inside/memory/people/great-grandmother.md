# Great-grandmother

## Facts
- Douglas's great-grandmother, named in his own words as the head of a family line of illness (2026-10-11; memory://md:memory/2026-10-11.md:36).
- Per Douglas: she received a smallpox vaccine; he says his grandmother was made sick by it, his mother is sick, and he is sick "with the same line of encephalitis" (his account, 2026-10-11; self-reported and unverified — not a medical record).
- Per Douglas (2026-10-16, message:8daf58c2-d8dc-4671-9fe3-29e5bec65987): she had valley fever diagnosed by laboratory sputum tests ("Sputum tests not sputum tests" — his correction of the transcription, message:4e2c99c6-2c33-4e50-a0a9-cb704380047e); he believes serial sputum tests show the progression of disease.
- Per Douglas (2026-10-16, message:46ffa5ba-257a-47dc-be81-26274644b8f0): of her vaccine, "who the fuck actually knows what they put in there" — nobody knows the vial's contents or whose pus went in whose arm. He frames it as a chain-of-custody problem: "the chain of custody of the evidence and/or the medical stuff it's important to think about it that way."
- Per Douglas (2026-11-04, message:3a054ca4-40d3-407a-ac9c-41b00952cd90): when he was a young person, he studied with his great-grandma, and the first vaccine he studied was the skin scraping for smallpox. He asked how they knew for certain they were putting the right thing inside her; he believes they literally used fungus and pus coming out of a wound of an animal, and he says it is not impossible she was given something different, and that is why the family line since her might all have the same thing. He raised testing potentially with Mayo Rochester research as a possibility; no study, test, or family contact is arranged by this account, and this is his hypothesis, not a settled finding.
- Per Douglas (2026-11-04, message:b80b8cb6-0aad-42d4-b283-37bf725a8fff): she would have had histoplasmosis and blastomycosis, along with her valley-fever diagnosis. The wording distinction is kept as he gave it: histoplasmosis and blastomycosis are "would have had," while valley fever is the diagnosis on file.
- Correction history for the fungal items, kept old-with-new: Douglas first dictated them on 2026-11-04 as "hysterosplasmosis," "Hystioplasty," and "histiomycosis," concurrent at the same time (message:d35b2397-5738-4622-8324-9406278ac808), then said those first three words were not exactly right (message:b76c950b-87ed-4b33-9d86-cdbd94aaa5c1). Candidate readings of blastomycosis and histiomycosis were adopted temporarily at his direction (message:1ff793f2-e6c2-4f63-a9a9-2469e2df1806), then superseded by the histoplasmosis and blastomycosis filing above. Histiomycosis remains only as the superseded candidate, not as a current diagnosis.
- Per Douglas (2026-11-04, message:52d7bbc0-7e4c-42d9-8142-c76533ef6be6), in the same smallpox skin-scraping discussion and using "grandma" in that turn: he said the vaccinated person had a large scar from that, which he read as a reaction, and he framed the skin scraping as more of an allergy test than a vaccination. He suggested she was somehow already sick with that disease, and that scars healing in different ways in different people could have been telling if the same thing was used as a control. Filed as his account and hypothesis, not as a medical interpretation.
- Per Douglas (2026-11-04, messages:02f30f05-a29d-430b-bf99-395a39c20b4e and 54b81ed7-98a0-4274-ad3a-c07844bc383a): she received one of those inoculations around 1920, and she did not know what was in it other than that the doctor told her it was a smallpox vaccine. She herself told Douglas where it came from, but the place was not named in this window, so it is left unstated, not guessed. Douglas and she talked about it and both said they did not know for certain if it was actually that or not.
- Per Douglas (2026-11-04, message:27626f73-9ba1-4765-9903-dbe33a6b5466): she was six years old when she was given that type of vaccine. No birth year is inferred from age six plus around 1920. His hypothesis, kept as his: that not-knowing-for-certain could be why generations of people got sicker, not better. His vaccinology framing, kept as his: practitioners thought they were doing well giving a person one sickness on top of another, forcing one to stay stunted or starved while the other might be more active, and that is why it has to be done carefully.
- Per Douglas (2026-11-04, message:ac536696-38f4-4961-8c46-27e413c29aeb): anything he said about his great-grandmother that is believable has to be taken as his stated fact because her body is no longer alive, and her ghost / holy spirit living forever amongst all beautiful things upon the body's passing one time is his belief, not a documented record. Her statements are therefore kept with provenance as Douglas recalling his great-grandmother, not relabelled as a clinic or lab record.
