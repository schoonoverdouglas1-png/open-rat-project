# Dr. Ottoman

## Facts
- Physician known to Douglas only as "Dr. Ottoman" (full name unknown). Everything below is Douglas's account from 2026-10-13, not independently verified.
- Douglas says Ottoman smothered his wife with a pillow at HSHS Sacred Heart Hospital, Eau Claire, while Douglas watched, "half paralyzed in a sleep coma"; a "Dr. Detbang or something" (name uncertain, transcription) who looked like Ottoman pulled Ottoman off her. Douglas says he woke to find his wife with no pulse and no heartbeat, rendered immediate aid, and believes he may have brought her back to life. Which wife and when are not specified in the evidence (message:30306d51-248f-45b1-ae78-9dfe0e26733e; memory/2026-10-13.md).
- Oct 13, 2026: Douglas said he would need to see the person in the parking lot himself to know for certain which "one of two" look-alikes it is - understood as Ottoman and the "Dr. Detbang or something" who looked just like him; identification unresolved (message:520faca6-6994-4cc4-8fb7-e639643b471c; memory/2026-10-13.md).
- Oct 13, 2026: Douglas said of the two look-alikes that "they may or may not be working together" - he is uncertain whether they are collaborating (message:2c55617a-88a2-4a40-a58c-33597541473a; memory/2026-10-13.md).
- Douglas says Ottoman held a grudge against his family for many years and may have been responsible for his grandfather's demise; per Douglas, Ottoman followed his great-grandfather Fields in the 1940s-50s (Fields's own verbal account to Douglas about "things in the woods that were bothering him"). Douglas estimates Ottoman would be ~100 if still alive (message:c7026a64-a961-476f-b151-ec4c341b8f94; memory/2026-10-13.md).
- Douglas says he got Ottoman fired from St. Joseph's Hospital, Chippewa Falls, with "a handful of phone calls," reporting him to the Hospital Sisters and through Marshfield Clinic (message:eaf84222-9afe-44a2-abbd-0960a823e978; memory/2026-10-13.md).
- Per Douglas, after St. Joseph's Ottoman was at HSHS Sacred Heart, Eau Claire - where the pillow incident happened - and after Douglas got him fired from there, Douglas believes he moved to Mayo Clinic (message:f3231379-ba04-4f58-9b58-b4b0b7bd8ae7; memory/2026-10-13.md).
- Douglas had hoped the Chippewa Falls firing would drive Ottoman out of the area for good; Ottoman stayed nearby, which Douglas reads as Ottoman having other work in the area besides practicing medicine (message:70fb70db-9a9b-4f91-815b-9e934d5c49b2; memory/2026-10-13.md).
- Douglas says meeting Ottoman in Chippewa Falls is what led him to Dr. Obaid in Eau Claire, and that Obaid was "scared shitless" of Ottoman (message:e88d28f4-11b9-4c9a-95d8-f232a37d0ffe; memory/2026-10-13.md).
