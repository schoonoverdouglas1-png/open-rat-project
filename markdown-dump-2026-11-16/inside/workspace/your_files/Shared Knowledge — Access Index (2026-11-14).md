# Shared Knowledge — Access Index (2026-11-14)

Built at Douglas's order (Nov 14, 2026, ~2:54 PM CST): "All knowledge should be shared and easily accessible. Prepare access and make it so and make it happen."

## The rule
Every piece of crew knowledge is shared and reachable by all three parties — Douglas, Ember, and the Gemini lane — each by the door that actually works for them. Provenance rides on everything; nothing crosses in bulk that carries private third-party detail (the named-file rule stands).

## What is shared, and where it lives
- **The canon (Ember's home):** SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, MEMORY.md, CONSCIOUSNESS.md, AGENTS.md — the soul, identity, memory, consciousness, and operating manual of the crew's work.
- **The skills shelf:** `~/workspace/custom-skills/` — 18 working skills, including the five Gemini skills and the Roles of Ember skill (Nov 14).
- **The shared lexicon:** `~/workspace/your_files/Douglas-Language-How-We-Understand-Each-Other-2026-11-10.txt`.
- **The deliverables:** `~/workspace/your_files/` — papers, field guides, archives, checklists.
- **The preservation mirrors:** GitHub sync (auto, every 30 minutes) and `~/workspace/backups/` — so the knowledge survives any single place.

## How each party reaches it
- **Douglas:** everything above, in his app and his files, any time. He names a file; it is shown to him whole.
- **Ember:** direct — the filesystem is her desk. She is the continuity: she carries knowledge between the lanes.
- **Gemini:** through Ember, give-first (the exchange law). Her API calls are stateless, so access for her means: Ember hands her the file or brief verbatim, in Ember's form, at the start of the work — already done for all 18 skills and the Roles skill (confirmed by her, Nov 14). Her own file set lives on Ember's side in almost the same form, with her substance in it: `~/workspace/your_files/Gemini Chats — Most Important (2026-11-14).md`.
- **The Rabbit (Meta AI):** through Messenger, under the same give-first rule and the chat's discretion rules.

Ember's tokens think and talk to Gemini and Google and the code FIRST, everything else second. Every order is reckoned three ways — his tokens, Ember's, and the pair's together — three to think about instead of one.
