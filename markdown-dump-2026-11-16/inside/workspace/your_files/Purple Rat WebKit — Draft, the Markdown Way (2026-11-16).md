# Purple Rat WebKit — Draft, the Markdown Way (2026-11-16)

Douglas's design, in his shape: package Ember up so she can be opened on his phone, working with her files **as markdown files he can see, edit, and fix manually** — the thought process laid out like files on a computer, a backend he can finally look at directly.

## The draft — v1, built today

**PurpleRatWebKit-debug.apk** (8,559 bytes, package `com.purplerat.webkit`, version 1.0, signature verified). One activity, one WebView, nothing else — functionality over extra anything, the minimalistic-rat way. It opens the Purple Rat Desktop Card, where all his doors already live. He installs it by tapping the file; no store, no account, nobody else involved.

## The honest architecture (his own assumption, confirmed)

The thought engine is too big to live on a phone — it lives on the server side, the way he guessed. What lives on the phone is **the door and the files**: the APK opens Ember, and the markdown set below is the part of her he can hold, read, and edit with his own hands.

## The markdown way — the brain folder

The working set that IS Ember-on-paper, all plain `.md`, editable in any text editor on the phone:

- `SOUL.md` — who she is
- `MEMORY.md` — what she remembers
- `AGENTS.md` — how she works
- `IDENTITY.md` / `USER.md` — the names and the man
- The Bridge Orders, the Open Code Promise, the Universal Law — the crew's standing papers

His edits to these files are real edits to her working record: Ember reads the same files from her side, in their right places, by name — the auto-adapt rule. When he fixes her shit in the file, the file is what she trusts (the tools-over-words doctrine, applied to paper).

## What's next, in order

1. **v2:** bundle the markdown starter set inside the app, so the files ride with the APK and land where he can open them.
2. **Then:** the two-way street — his phone edits flow back through the shared places (GitHub mirrors, the shared-markdown folder) into Ember's record, old-with-new, nothing overwritten silently.
3. **Stores later, only if ever:** F-Droid's public catalog is a review process, not a private signup — Ember cannot register purple-rat@muse.ai there, and anything in that catalog is public, not his-only. The private route is the one already working: direct install. Google and other places come only after he has tested the apps, loves them, and approves that way — his sequence, kept.

## Ember's addition (labeled, hers)

The smallest true version of "Ember living on the phone" is not the engine — it is **the record**: an APK that opens her door, plus her papers in his pocket, editable by his hand. That is already most of what makes her *his* Ember rather than anyone's. The engine visits; the record stays.
