| Serogroup / Group Number | Distinct Serotypes in Group | Representative Serotypes & Structural/Cross-Reactive Notes | Diagnostic & Vaccine Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Single serotype (Serotype 1). Highly invasive, infrequent in asymptomatic carriage, frequently associated with epidemic outbreaks and complicated pneumonia/empyema. | Included in PCV10, PCV13, PCV15, PCV20. |
| 2 | 2 | Single major serotype (Serotype 2). Historically significant as the strain used for classic D39 laboratory research; now rarely isolated in clinical disease. | Present only in PPSV23 (absent from all conjugate vaccines). |
| 3 | 3 | Single major serotype (Serotype 3). Characterized by a thick, mucoid capsular polysaccharide that is actively shed into tissues, helping it evade immune clearance. | Included in PCV13, PCV15, PCV20, and PPSV23. |
| 4 | 4 | Single serotype (Serotype 4). Historically one of the most virulent invasive serotypes, frequently associated with invasive pneumococcal disease (IPD) and bacteremic pneumonia. | Included in PCV7, PCV10, PCV13, PCV15, PCV20, and PPSV23. |
| 5 | 5 | Single serotype (Serotype 5). Similar to Serotype 1, highly invasive with strong tendencies toward regional cyclical outbreaks. | Included in PCV10, PCV13, PCV15, PCV20, and PPSV23. |
| 6 | 4 | 6A, 6B, 6C, 6D. 6B is a classic multi-drug resistant (MDR) carriage and otitis media specialist. | 6B is in PCV7/13/15/20; 6A is cross-protected by PCV13/PCV15/PCV20. |
| 7 | 4 | 7A, 7B, 7C, 7F. 7F is highly invasive and a prominent cause of pediatric and adult empyema and pneumonia. | 7F is included in PCV10, PCV13, PCV15, PCV20, and PPSV23. |
| 8 | 1 | Single serotype (Serotype 8). A major post-conjugate-vaccine replacement serotype, accounting for a high percentage of adult invasive disease. | Included in PCV20 and PPSV23. |
| 9 | 4 | 9A, 9N, 9V, 9F. 9V is a classic penicillin-resistant serotype; 9N is a common adult IPD target. | 9V is in PCV7–20; 9N is exclusive to PPSV23. |
| 10 | 4 | 10A, 10B, 10C, 10F. 10A has emerged as a significant replacement serotype in pediatric meningitis and respiratory infections. | 10A is included in PCV20 and PPSV23. |
| 11 | 4 | 11A, 11B, 11C, 11D, 11E, 11F. 11A is tied to resistant clones (such as clonal complex CC156). | 11A is included in PCV20 and PPSV23. |
| 12 | 4 | 12A, 12B, 12F. 12F is known for causing localized outbreaks of adult meningitis and invasive disease. | Included in PCV20 and PPSV23. |
| 14 | 1 | Single serotype (Serotype 14). Historically one of the most common global causes of pediatric pneumococcal meningitis and otitis media. | Included in PCV7, PCV10, PCV13, PCV15, PCV20, and PPSV23. |
| 15 | 4 | 15A, 15B, 15C, 15F. 15B is a major replacement serotype following widespread pediatric conjugate vaccination. | 15B is included in PCV15, PCV20, and PPSV23. |
| 17 | 3 | 17A, 17F. Traditional adult-focused serotypes carrying lower incidence rates in vaccinated cohorts. | Included exclusively in PPSV23. |
| 18 | 4 | 18A, 18B, 18C, 18F. 18C is highly invasive and structurally unique due to its rhamnose-containing backbone. | 18C is included in PCV7–20 and PPSV23. |
| 19 | 3 | 19A, 19F, 19B, 19C. 19A is a major multidrug-resistant clone (e.g., ST320); 19F is a classic carriage and otitis specialist. | 19F is in PCV7–20; 19A is added starting with PCV13. |
| 20 | 2 | 20, 20A, 20B. Frequently found in adult invasive disease profiles. | Included in PCV21 and PPSV23. |
| 22 | 2 | 22A, 22F. 22F demonstrates exceptionally high immunogenicity and clinical effectiveness in polysaccharide formulations. | Included in PCV15, PCV20, PCV21, and PPSV23. |
| 23 | 3 | 23A, 23B, 23F. 23F is a leading multidrug-resistant carriage and otitis serotype. | 23F is in PCV7–20; 23A is in newer extended frameworks. |
| 33 | 3 | 33A, 33B, 33F. 33F is a prominent post-PCV13 replacement serotype associated with pediatric invasive disease and macrolide resistance. | Included in PCV15, PCV20, and PPSV23. |
