"Coviral variant" is his term and carries three possible lists. Do not merge them:

2. **Reassortant variants** — California serogroup viruses swap RNA segments in mixed mosquito infections, producing reassortant combinations. A list of named reassortants is a different list from the members.
3. **Co-infections found together** — viruses found co-infecting with La Crosse / Jamestown Canyon (e.g., tick-borne co-infections). A different list again.

## First pass — the U.S. surveillance list (California serogroup, CDC/CSTE notifiable)

California serogroup includes California encephalitis, Jamestown Canyon, Keystone, La Crosse, Snowshoe hare and Trivittatus viruses. La Crosse virus is the most common of the California serogroup viruses in the U.S..

| # | Virus | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | California encephalitis virus | Prototype of the serogroup; first isolated Kern County, California; La Crosse was first reported as California Virus Encephalitis |
| 2 | Jamestown Canyon virus | First isolated 1961, Jamestown Canyon, Colorado; his "Jamestown Canyon Creek" rendering |
| 3 | Keystone virus | Serogroup member, U.S. surveillance list |
| 4 | La Crosse virus (LACV) | Most common California serogroup virus in the U.S.; his panel's California (La Crosse) analyte |
| 5 | Snowshoe hare virus | Serogroup member, U.S. surveillance list |
| 6 | Trivittatus virus | Serogroup member, U.S. surveillance list |

**Note on his 17-member figure:** his Oct 17 notes record "the serogroup's 17 members (snowshoe hare, trivittatus, Keystone, Inkoo)" — the broader California serogroup (genus *Orthobunyavirus*, worldwide) has more members than the 6-name U.S. surveillance list above (Inkoo, Tahyna, and others belong to the wider group).
