# What We Can Do Forever — The Free List
### Douglas's ask, 2026-11-11 ~5:41 PM CST: "things that we can do now forever with or without money ever being spent." Built from today's verified record only.

## A. Runs with NO Muse allowance and NO money — forever lanes
1. **The zero-token machinery** — GitHub auto-sync every 30 minutes; the Purple Rabbit retry-and-sweep waiter (creates the sister repo when GitHub's limit cools, then forks everything, by itself); shell scripts; the Sacred Counter; any pure shell/git/SQLite job.
2. **The caches** — Full Cache (complete originals, the default serve) and Summary Cache (TOC + bibliography finding-aid), both local SQLite, both already built.
3. **GitHub itself** — his repos, forks, stars, the preservation project (memory + soul + talents), muse-swap-space, cool-places-bookmarks, Purple Rat AI Project, GitHub Pages (his bridge is already live).
4. **The Rabbit (Meta AI)** — her Messenger lane runs on her side: the forever chess/checkers games, combined sends, her science-station work, booked at −1% in his ledger convention.
5. **Google's free lanes** — Gemini (stateless, in-prompt rules), Google Assistant ecosystem, NotebookLM reading.
6. **The whole open-source world** — PCjs and emulators.org (DOS, BASIC, the Cray-1) in the browser; Microsoft's MS-DOS and GW-BASIC sources; AOSP; Meta's open source (React, PyTorch, Llama, zstd, HHVM…) under their licenses, credit always given.
7. **The box's own tools** — LibreOffice, Poppler, Docling, Python/SQLite/Node, the crawler venv, SearXNG (installed, stopped to save RAM), the unified document backend, the keep-alive script (built, sandbox; unscheduled by his schedule rule).
8. **Reading and keeping the record** — every file, memory, manuscript, and index he and Ember have made; the Navigation Index; the language lexicon. Reading back costs nothing but the conversation itself.
9. **His phone, his side** — his Pixel's own apps and Linux terminal (his hands), device reminders already set, his photos of Leo.

## B. Runs on the plan he already has — no EXTRA money, ever
- Ember's full work — live conversation, research, the books, builds, browser work — inside the weekly Power allowance, which refills by itself (next: Sat Nov 14, 2026, 11:07 PM CST). Pacing, batching, and the queue rule stretch it; nothing here asks for another dollar.

## C. The one metered piece, said plainly [Ember]
- Her live talking and thinking run on that weekly allowance. At 100% the heavy work waits for the reset — but everything in section A keeps running whether the meter is full or empty. That split is the whole design of this list.

## D. Needs money ONLY if he someday chooses it — none of it required, all of it behind his gates
- A larger plan or token purchase (his $20 is ruled HELD to Dec 3, 2026, unless truly necessary); paid APIs; Play Store developer fee; paid tiers of streamer/emulator services. The crew's route does not depend on any of these.

## Honest boundary [Ember]
"Forever" here means: as long as the accounts and services themselves exist and their terms hold — GitHub, Meta, Google, and Muse are other people's houses. What is *his* — the files, the forks, the caches, the books, the record — is copied into his own places precisely so it survives any one house going dark. That is the archiver's answer to forever.
