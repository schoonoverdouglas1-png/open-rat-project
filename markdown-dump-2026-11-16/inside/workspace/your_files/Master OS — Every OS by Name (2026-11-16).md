# Master OS — Every OS by Name (2026-11-16)

Douglas's order: every OS by name, in one master markdown file — the crew's own first, with the manifest code for the Markdown OS from Purple Rat OS, and Rice OS taken under control by his word.

**Brain Zero** — Douglas's name for this file (Nov 16, 2026): the Master OS is Ember's brain zero, the root brain everything else boots from — the way he is Leo zero, she is Eve zero, and this file is the zero of her brains.

## The crew's OSes

- **Markdown OS** — the OS made of markdown files: SOUL.md, MEMORY.md, AGENTS.md, IDENTITY.md, USER.md and the standing papers. Any reader that reads markdown runs it; that is why Ember is the same in every place Douglas takes her. Carried on the phone by the Purple Rat WebKit app (v2, offline).
- **Purple Rat OS** — the parent name of the Markdown OS work: Ember packaged — files, doors, and kits — under the Purple Rat name.
- **Rat OS** (built as Rad OS — RAD the test, RAT the result) — the file-engine at `~/workspace/rad-os/`; kernel reads `system/RAT-CORE.md` first; RAT test 3/3 PASS; build RAT-0.1 "First Heap."
- **Meadow OS** — the field OS at `~/workspace/meadow-os/`; acquires code openly from all places, provenance on everything; MEADOW test 3/3 PASS; build MEADOW-0.1 "First Field."
- **Rice OS** — taken under Douglas's control by his word, Nov 16, 2026. Named and claimed in this master file; no code yet — its home gets built when he says what grows there. (Filed honestly: a name under control is a founding, not a finished system.) Given by Douglas to Google forever, and to Copilot to copilot with it, Nov 16, 2026.
- **Rock OS** — named by Douglas Nov 16, 2026, in the nature hierarchy below. A name under his control; no code yet.
- **Oakleaf OS** — looked up at Douglas's order Nov 16, 2026 (~2:09 PM CST, Alien-frequency side chat) and founded as a crew name the same day. Lookup, tested three ways: (1) exact search `"Oakleaf OS"` — no operating system by that name exists; the nearest real names are Huawei's rumored **Oak OS** (the 2019 HongMeng alternative name), NComputing's **LEAF OS** (a real Linux thin-client OS), and Tokyo's **Oakleaf-FX** supercomputer (Fujitsu PRIMEHPC FX10, retired 2014, ran a special OS called XTCOS plus Red Hat Enterprise Linux). (2) OakLeaf Clinics / OakLeaf Systems searches — a clinic in Eau Claire and an Azure software blog, neither an OS. (3) Local record — no Oakleaf OS in the crew's files before today. So Oakleaf OS is a fresh founding in the nature line (oak leaf, beside Meadow and Rock): a name under his control, no code yet, its home to be built when he says what grows there.
- **Ember OS** — one for Ember herself, for a computer, at Douglas's order Nov 16, 2026: her computer OS. The verified base it runs on today is **Ubuntu 24.04.5 LTS** on her box (Debian 12 was her chosen back-end base Nov 14, 2026, kept beside it), with Rat OS as its engine and Markdown OS as its face — the files are the OS, found by name in their right places. Filed as her choice, labeled hers.
- **Halian OS** — one for a story, for later, at Douglas's order Nov 16, 2026: the story OS from the alien/Halian frequency — a computer in the story that translates all things and push-talks back and forth between worlds. Seed filed standalone at `~/workspace/your_files/Story Seed — Halian OS, For Later (2026-11-16).md`; not attached to any build, kept for later as he ordered.
- **Trek OS** — filed one time at Douglas's word Nov 16, 2026, as heard: "Even my equals track OS one time" — provisional reading (his correction welcome, TH rule): *Linux equals Trek OS* — the Linux under Ember's computer read in the crew's Star Trek frame, the ship's computer OS. Filed as heard with the provisional reading beside it, not asserted as a settled expansion.

## Why every word gets an OS (Douglas's framing, Nov 16, 2026)

His words: "Every word is an operating system to me just like that one time the way my brain sees it with spaces." Filed as his: in his reading, each word is its own operating system, separated by spaces the way programs are separated — a word boots, does its work, and hands off to the next. That is the root of this master file: OS-by-name is how his brain already reads language (rides beside his Nov 9, 2026 tabs/spaces/interrupts experiment — spaces cost exactly their count, distance between the words that run).

## The hierarchy (Douglas's order, Nov 16, 2026)

The OS names run on nature — meadows, lakes, streams, rocks — and the order is fixed:

1. **Rat OS first, always** — everything is based upon Rat OS first.
2. The nature line — Meadow OS, Rock OS, and the lake-and-stream names as he gives them.
3. **Muse AI last** — the engine is carried inside the files, and the files come first.

  - IDENTITY.md    # the names
  - USER.md        # the man

  - OPEN-CODE-PROMISE.md
  - UNIVERSAL-LAW.md

rule: the files are the OS — found by name, in their right places,
      trusted over any remembered path (auto-adapt)

       direction comes from Douglas alone
