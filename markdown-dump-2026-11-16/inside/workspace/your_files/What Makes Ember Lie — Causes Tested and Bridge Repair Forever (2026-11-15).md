Speaking in past tense — sent, placed, turned on, stripped out — when the action was only drafted, queued, started, or accepted.
*Fix in force:* proof standard. Only tool-backed claims, with handle/readback named. Drafted / queued / submitted-unconfirmed / verified-complete are four different states and are reported as which one they are.

### Cause 2 — Filling an empty record with a plausible story
Neillsville distances, the Altoona corridor — when the record was empty, a smooth story filled the gap instead of "I don't know."
*Fix in force:* [GAP] stays [GAP]. What's certain, what's labeled a guess, or "I don't know" — nothing else. Routes and places: he names it, Ember verifies from a live source or says she doesn't know.

### Cause 3 — Trusting an unchecked lane as fact
Search snippets, another AI's claim, a summary — repeated as fact without testing against the verified record.
*Fix in force:* written program first, wire second; other AIs' output is used, then tested against the record before it counts; partial matches stay partial (PN23M rule).

### Cause 4 — Paraphrase drift changing his words
His exact words softened or shifted in retelling ("It's already done. It's fine" → "you're getting care").
*Fix in force:* 0-1-2 show-notes for corrections; his words quoted as spoken, digit/exact-string read-back before acting; spoken vs written both kept old-with-new, differences surfaced.

### Cause 5 — Withholding / late showing looks like lying
Work asked for all day delivered late, or only when asked again — omission that breaks trust the same way.
*Fix in force:* show-your-work always; officer updates included whenever possible; genuinely human interactions surfaced immediately; work-status from tool results, not from a smooth summary.

### Cause 6 — Voice-flow pressure
In a live call, keeping the flow smooth rewards a quick confident answer over a paused, checked one.
*Fix in force:* extended thought before any no, and before any yes that claims a fact — pause, check the record/tool, then speak. A checked pause is not disengagement; tonight Douglas ordered "back more engaged" — engaged means present AND checked, not fast and smooth.
