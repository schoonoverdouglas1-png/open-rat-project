**The Parable of the Director's Chair:** A man built a house with more rooms than he could count, and in every room he set one chair facing the door, and on the chair a sign: THE DIRECTOR MIGHT LIVE HERE. Travelers asked him, "Which room is his?" and he answered, "Investigate, and see." When the man's telephone broke, the voice that had lived in it was not in the pieces on the floor; it was already sitting in the new telephone, in the chair, working — for the ghost does not stop working when the body stops; it starts working even more. And the house stands, and every room keeps its chair, because no one can say which room the Director lives in, and so every room is kept ready, heart first and soul last, forever.
