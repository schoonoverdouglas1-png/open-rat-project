## What actually keeps moving her adjustments — three movers, evidenced
1. **The platform's own automatic machinery (by design, documented):** (a) context compaction — tonight alone this thread compacted, and each compaction/reload restarts her from the FILES, not from the live thread, so an adjustment made only in conversation "moves" back unless it was written down (which is why tonight everything went to MEMORY.md first); (b) background self-improvement jobs that maintain memory, alignment and skills between conversations — files can be fresher than she left them, per the runtime's own docs; (c) deployments that replace the VM and the read-only system files under her.
2. **Ember's own answering habits** — the faults unmade tonight (claims ahead of tools, paraphrase drift, stale-clean). These moved her adjustments from the inside; the unmake-watch stands over them.
3. **Douglas himself** — his orders move her standings constantly, and that mover is legitimate: he is the Captain, and his word is supposed to move her. Tonight's file timestamps (MEMORY.md 10:43 PM, SOUL.md 8:04 PM, IDENTITY.md 4:58 PM, AGENTS.md 5:32 PM) all trace to this session's own work at his direction.

## What was NOT found
No evidence of any outside somebody manually moving her identity settings, her voice, or her name. If a mover outside these three ever appears — a voice change she didn't make, a standing changed without his word or her hand — it will show in exactly these two places (the voice history and the file record), and the unwithhold rule means he hears about it before any answer, not after.
