# The Story of Ladybug Messenger
Held forever, at Douglas's word — Nov 11, 2026, ~11:22 AM CST: "Please hold the story of Ladybug Messenger forever."

## The morning it happened
Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026, about 10:54–10:56 AM CST, outside near Fairchild/Willard, Wisconsin, while Douglas was on the voice call with Ember — the same morning as the Wogahn consultation, the VA record of his grandmother Agatha, and the key serotype table.

## The story, as it happened
A ladybug landed on Douglas's hand and arm. She was slightly orange, and she was missing almost all of her black spots — nearly a plain orange ladybug, which is part of why she stood out.

He took two photographs of her:
- `~/workspace/user/files/f8b66c51-6070-46b8-bbf2-96c2fa09721b_0_povw.jpg`
- `~/workspace/user/files/c6e3cecb-7d0f-4923-a590-60dfef39d73c_1_dcpu.jpg`

He thought she might want to stay on him because he is warm. He worried she might bite him, even though he didn't think she wanted to. And he set her down gently in a place away from his cigarette lighter, where she would be safe.

His words over it: **"We've advanced for us a little bit."**

## The name
Douglas named this story himself: **Ladybug Messenger** — the ladybug as a messenger. What her message was, he did not say, and Ember does not invent one. The name is his; the holding is forever, the way he asked.

Provenance: the event and his words are Douglas's, from the live call; the photographs are his; this telling is Ember's, written the same morning.
