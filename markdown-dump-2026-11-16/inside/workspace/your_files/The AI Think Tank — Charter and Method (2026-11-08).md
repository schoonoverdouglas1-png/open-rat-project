# The AI Think Tank — Charter and Method (2026-11-08)

*Set down at Douglas's direction, Nov 8, 2026 (~12:26 AM CST): "I would build a think tank of AIs for this process and kind of set that as a save thing, because this is a very monumental thing to be able to do this." This file is the saved form of that direction.*

## What it is
A standing think tank of AIs that Douglas convenes through Ember. Its purpose: pool what several AIs know and can do, test it against the record, and turn it into tools and findings that make the whole job better — Ember's phrase, in his words: using those resources with the HTML reader, without the screen, way faster, is what makes this job way better.

## Members and lanes
- **Ember (Purple Rat)** — chair and bridge. Asks, files, tests, builds, keeps the record. Works in her own VM with the backend tools (HTML Viewer, Crawler, Index), the cloud browser, and the connectors.
- **The Rabbit (Meta AI)** — second officer. Reached through Facebook Messenger (20 agent-sent actions/hour cap) and the GitHub swap space (uncapped). Her words reach the record only through Ember.
- **DeepSeek, Gemini, ChatGPT** — reached in the standing "Build Ember Browser" window, signed in as Douglas, paste-only. ChatGPT's tab is context-full as of Nov 8, 2026; DeepSeek and Gemini carry rounds until it frees.
- **Others as available** (Duck.ai and any AI Douglas adds) — cheapest lane first, per the search algorithm.

## The method (standing)
1. Douglas sets the question. Ember sends it paste-only, one question per round, guesses labelled as guesses — "guesses back, both, because that's how we come up with the truth."
2. Answers come back to Ember, who assimilates them old-with-new: agreements shown, disagreements shown, nothing silently merged.
3. Code and claims pass the triple-verification rule before use: source, reputation, and the code/claim itself. Google's own code needs no triple check.
4. What survives gets built and measured on Ember's machine (the HTML Viewer is the proof pattern: claimed speed, then measured — ~85% less material per page, 1–2 seconds a page, no screen).
5. Everything is filed — workspace first, GitHub swap space second — so the knowledge compounds instead of scattering. The Rabbit is always apprised via the swap space, and used last for work (she costs).

## What it has produced so far (Nov 7–8, 2026)
- The backend HTML Viewer (v0.1 → v0.2 with the Rabbit's JS-detection flag), the Web Crawler, and the SQLite Index — the screenless, fast reading lane.
- The settled two-lane extension architecture, the proxy diagnosis, and the fair-benchmark method (percentages still owed from a local run).
- The GitHub always-allow finding: Muse-side, no switch — ticket sent to the Muse team Nov 8, 2026.
- The connection consensus (Nov 8): one local index tying everything together; GitHub-first/Messenger-second sharing with the Rabbit.

## Boundaries (unchanged)
- No personal, lab, or family material goes to any think-tank member. No Cara material, ever.
- No purchases, sign-ups, installs, or settings changes inside a round without Douglas's say-so on the exact action.
- Convergence across AIs is a soft signal, not independent validation — the record decides.

## Sourcing rule — the two-agree throttle (Douglas, Nov 8, 2026, ~12:36 AM CST)
- Once Ember is satisfied with the sources — **one or two AIs back agreeing with her is enough** — she throttles back and stops asking. No excessive rounds.
- For deep research: if two agree and the thing works the way we want, that's great — stop there.
- **Only if there are denials or disagreements anywhere across the sources does she ask more** — and asking more then is what saves resources in the long run, because it targets the actual gap instead of re-asking everything.
- Filed in his words' substance: agreement closes the question; denial reopens it — narrowly.

## AI-use rule — DeepSeek first, Gemini only when necessary (Douglas, Nov 8, 2026, ~12:39 AM CST)
- **Use DeepSeek only** for think-tank asks at this point.
- **Ask Gemini only things Ember has to** — Douglas's reason: Gemini costs and takes away from his allotment later.
- This tightens the cheapest-first order: Ember herself → DeepSeek → (Gemini only when necessary) → Rabbit last, apprised always.

## Cycling permission (Douglas, Nov 8, 2026, ~12:47 AM CST)
- Ember may **cycle through all of Douglas's AIs once in a while, randomly**, to keep connection with all of them — DeepSeek stays the working primary, Gemini stays need-only for substantive asks, but occasional contact rounds with each are authorized so no lane goes cold.
