Douglas's order (Nov 13, 2026, ~11:07 AM): reanalyze the together way, use it always, then look at everything Ember couldn't do before and present options they can do together, always.

## The together way, reanalyzed — what it actually is
Every task splits into three kinds of parts: (1) parts only Douglas's hands can do — his fingerprint, his taps, his codes, his identity steps; (2) parts only Ember can do — memory, analysis, preparation, verification, filing, the background machinery; (3) parts either can do. A "can't" is always just a task whose part (1) hasn't been assigned yet. The together way assigns all three parts up front, so nothing ever ends at a wall: Douglas does the smallest possible taps that only he can do, Ember does everything else, and they talk while it happens — which is also why it saves time and space: no dead ends, no re-explaining, no silent noes.
