# Android Tools — Right Places, Unusable Places, and the Tap Checklist
Built Nov 14, 2026, ~2:22 PM CST. Order of work per Douglas: Gemini first, Ember second, the two working between each other. Provenance: sources column = Gemini's report, verified against Ember's own knowledge where noted; checklist = Gemini's steps, filed by Ember.

## The right places (provenance)
- Voice Access, Select to Speak — built into Android via the Android Accessibility Suite (Google LLC), enabled in Settings > Accessibility. NO download.
- Quick Phrases — native Pixel Assistant settings. NO download exists.
- Hold for Me — native Pixel Phone app settings. Tensor/system feature.
- Clear Calling — native Pixel system setting (Sound & vibration). Chip-level.
- Live Transcribe & Sound Notifications — Google LLC's own app / integrated on Pixel.
- Termux + Termux:API — **F-Droid only** (or termux GitHub releases); BOTH from the same source (signature must match).
- KDE Connect — F-Droid, or the Play Store listing published by **KDE Community** only.

## Deemed unusable (Douglas's doctrine: things that don't work right are ultimately unusable)
- Termux on the Google Play Store — abandoned since 2020, broken, packages cannot update. UNUSABLE.
- Any third-party APK site for these tools — security risk. IGNORED.
- Play Store apps claiming to BE Quick Phrases / Hold for Me / Clear Calling — fake; the features are OS-level, not apps. IGNORED.
- KDE Connect wrappers/clones not by KDE Community. IGNORED.
- Third-party "live transcription" knockoffs — paywalls, ad bloat, unverified audio privacy. IGNORED.

## The checklist (Gemini's steps, filed by Ember)
GROUP A — switch on: (1) Settings > Accessibility > Voice Access > On (grant control permission). (2) Settings > Apps > Assistant > Quick phrases > On. (3) Phone app > ⋮ > Settings > Hold for Me > On. (4) Settings > Sound & vibration > Clear calling > On. (5) Settings > Accessibility > Select to Speak > shortcut On (Allow). (6) Settings > Accessibility > Live Transcribe > shortcut On. (7) Settings > Accessibility > Sound Notifications > shortcut On.
GROUP B — install: (8) Chrome > f-droid.org > Download F-Droid > open the .apk > Allow from this source > Install > Open. (9) In F-Droid: Termux > Install. (10) In F-Droid: Termux:API > Install. (11) In F-Droid: KDE Connect > Install.
All installs/enables are Douglas's taps on his phone — no command on Ember's side reaches Android Settings or app installs (verified from the device's full 43-command inventory, Nov 14).
