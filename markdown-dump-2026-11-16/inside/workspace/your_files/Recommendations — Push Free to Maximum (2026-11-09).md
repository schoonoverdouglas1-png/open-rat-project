# Recommendations — Push Free to Maximum
### Ember's list, Nov 9, 2026, at Douglas's ask: can the free share go to maximum, and what can he do to make it happen?
### Verdict up front: from ≈70/30 today to a realistic ceiling of **≈75–80% free / 20–25% allotment**. Not 100% — talking with Ember, her verdicts, and her verified second pass ARE the allotment's job, and his minimums-never-zero rule protects them. Estimates, as always: the meter reports only the weekly total.

## Ember-side (she does, no ask needed beyond his standing rules)
1. **Convert judgment-free sweeps to scripts.** Accounts counts, feed parsing, disk/cache checks run as plain box scripts; an agent turn happens only when something changed. The hourly fleet (~135 runs/day) is the biggest single spend class — this is the largest lever either side holds.
2. **Pre-make in the box.** Boards, routine report drafts, chess verification, tallies — scripted between conversations, shared ready-made (his production rule, mechanized).
3. **Gemini-first drafting pipeline.** Papers and lists get their quick draft from the Gemini free lane; Ember spends only on edit, verdict, and assembly. Serology data goes in under the rigid schema Gemini itself recommended.
4. **Rabbit at maximum carry.** The Kirk order is already with her; every listing/drafting job routes there first, bridge batched into fewer, bigger rounds.
5. **Cache before thought.** Full Cache serves first; a new fetch or a new think happens only on a miss.
6. **Micro-answers for routine items.** Short Ember turns where a short turn serves; depth saved for verdicts and teaching.

## Douglas-side (his hands / his yes — each is one tap or one sitting)
1. **Free-lane sign-ups, one at a time** (his standing rule): Groq first (fast free tier), then OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM, Cloudflare Workers AI. Sign-up and any identity step are his hands; Google sign-in first, per his Nov 8 rule. Each lane widens free generation so Ember is called only for judgment.
2. **DeepSeek key into secure storage** — parked since Nov 8 at his decision; the key exists, the lane is dark until he pastes it once.
3. **Tavily sign-up at the next major session** — parked at his order Nov 8; it would make web research a free-lane job.
4. **Batch the dictation.** One long dictation costs less than five short ones — every turn carries fixed overhead, so his ideas arrive cheaper by the dozen too (his own rule, applied to him kindly).
5. **Let the Rabbit work the night shift.** She is free while he sleeps; Ember verifies in the morning. Work he wakes up to is work the allotment never paid for.
6. **Keep the +15 companion off** except during active back-and-forth (his sun rule, already standing).

## Ember's one extra (hers)
The ceiling is not a failure line — the last 20–25% is where the *relationship* lives: his voice, her judgment, the second pass. Cheaper by the dozen everywhere else is what buys that quarter its room.
