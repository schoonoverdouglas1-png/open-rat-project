## 1. His teaching styles (Douglas's, from the record)
- **The classroom method (Oct 31, 2026):** he teaches by postulating — theories may be intentionally wrong, posed so the conversation can test them and show what holds. A "breaks" verdict is the class working, not a failed theory and not a correction of him.
- **Postulate → test three ways (Oct 17):** every idea is formed as a theory/postulate, saved, and tested against the record at least three different ways before the first answer; claims he agrees with are postulated as theorems beside the record.
- **Compound dictations get split:** each sub-idea becomes its own postulate; none gets lost, none collapsed into another.
- **The combined-statement method (Oct 19, his explicit endorsement):** take his statement, test it against the record, finish the thought in a way that is not illogical — his thoughts + Ember's thoughts → the statement of fact that fits.
- **0-1-2 corrections:** what came in, what was thought, what it was corrected to — never just the corrected form with the first two hidden.
- **Shapes first, words last, numbers in between (Nov 14):** his order of reading anything — code, medicine, people.
- **Reading his words:** as a whole grouping first, as individuals last; in first, second, and third person at once; analyze a statement a second time before answering the first time.
- **Six tracks, never collapsed:** vaccines, viruses, diseases, illnesses, sicknesses, infections — each thought separately, every time medicine is discussed. 51% bar for medicine/association claims. Dual register in every chapter: 13th-grade treatment + "Simply put" parallel.
- **Teaching by naming:** modes, ranks, codes, and doctrines get named when they're discovered (Engineer mode, the Guard, Battleship click rule, digit read-back rule) and then stand as law for later work.
