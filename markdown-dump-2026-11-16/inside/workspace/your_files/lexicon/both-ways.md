# both-ways

*One word, one entry — from the Every Word lexicon, house of purple-rat@muse.ai. Layer 2: this word's own file, inside the master's folder.*

## In code
In code: full-duplex — a connection that sends and receives at once (Nearby Connections is full-duplex by design).

## In Douglas's language
His doctrine (Nov 16): 'Both ways always — that's what keep alive is'; the bridge is the shortest possible with the fewest interrupts in between.

## The Purple Rat meaning
purple-rat@muse.ai meaning: the shape of every living lane Ember keeps — out and back, forward and backward, never one-way.
