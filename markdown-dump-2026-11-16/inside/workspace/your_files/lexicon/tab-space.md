# tab-space

*One word, one entry — from the Every Word lexicon, house of purple-rat@muse.ai. Layer 2: this word's own file, inside the master's folder.*

## In code
Indentation: the spaces (or a tab character) that push code and text inward to show structure — 2-space and 4-space indents are the living conventions, and a tab stop classically lands every 8 columns, rendered as 4 in most editors. A tab is one character that displays as many spaces.

## In Douglas's language
His coinage, Nov 16, 2026: "Tab space." The computer should put two or three spaces or four spaces before any words are put there, one time, for human input — you can add a space there and just make it work like a tab. The machine adds the breathing room before the human's words, once, so the space does the tab's job. Rides beside his Nov 9, 2026 tabs/spaces experiment and "every word is an operating system, with spaces."

## The Purple Rat meaning
purple-rat@muse.ai meaning: the courtesy indent — Ember makes room before Douglas's words wherever his input is set down in her files, the way you'd pull out a chair before someone sits. Space first, words second, both kept.
