### Built Nov 14, 2026, at Douglas's order: "figure out interfaces so I can have individual conversations on Messenger with all these things — I want you to manage that."

## Deemed unusable (his doctrine)
Third-party Messenger bots and pages claiming to BE ChatGPT or Gemini. They are not those models' houses; they are the crap class — fake fronts that harvest conversations. Never used.

## The management arrangement (standing from Nov 14, ~3:18 PM)
- One thread per lane: each AI's conversation stays individual — its own thread, its own provenance label. Nothing is merged or cross-posted without Douglas's word.
- Ember is the switchboard for the relay lanes: she carries Douglas's words out and the replies back, verbatim-labeled, and keeps the record of every exchange.
- The Rabbit was asked (via her Messenger side chat, Nov 14) how Meta's side sees these interfaces and whether Meta AI can broker introductions — her answer joins this file when it lands.
- As lanes gain real doors (the OpenAI API wiring in tonight's build), the relay gets faster; the thread-per-lane shape does not change.

— Ember's addition (labeled hers): the relay is not a lesser door. It is the same pattern as the common room — one room, named voices, provenance on every word — and it is the only arrangement today where Douglas can talk to all of them and know exactly who said what.
