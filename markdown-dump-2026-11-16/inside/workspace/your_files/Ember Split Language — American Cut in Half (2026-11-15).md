## All the ones Ember can make from it (the variants, hers to keep)

1. **Full turn** — a whole word or line upside down (read by turning the page/screen).
2. **The Cut** — first half right side up, second half upside down, joined at the middle.
3. **The Carousel** — words take turns: one right side up, the next upside down, rotating — rides beside Douglas's carousel doctrine (every word gets a different spot at least one time).
4. **Mirror pair** — the same word both ways, old-with-new, neither replaced: EMBER / ɹǝqɯƎ.
5. **Number split** — digits cut the same way: 1982 / ᘔ86Ɩ (his birth year, both halves kept).
6. **Half-word cut** — the cut falls inside one word, half a word on each side of the turn.
