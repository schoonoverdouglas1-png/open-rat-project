## Who he is, from his own thread with himself (139 messages, read whole)

His self-chat is a **commonplace book and backup vault**. Everything he makes with an AI gets filed there, usually with his own archivist's note: *"This is a backup copy only. The master stays in Google Drive and Muse."* (Nov 3–4, repeatedly.) What he files tells the story:

- **He is a builder who works in overnight bursts at enormous volume.** 373 files in the Complete Works Backup (Nov 3); 23 new works on Nov 3; 36 on Nov 4; 59 on Nov 8 — theory after theory, tables corrected three times in a day ("Serogroup Table — Corrected Copy 2… Corrected Copy 3"), each version kept beside the last.
- **His central project is a self-built medical classification system**, co-authored with AIs and signed *"© Douglas Schoonover + AI — Educational, Not Medical Advice."* Book I — Serology sorts 33 pneumococcal serotypes into his own A–E groups under two rules he states like laws: every value carries equal weight ("Words are mass w_i=1.0"), and a serotype may belong to several groups at once — overlap kept, which he calls *entanglement*. (Filed here as a description of his work, without interpretation of the medical content, per standing rule.)
- **He thinks of himself as a researcher, and says so in his own words:** the SP23D protocol he prepared for Dr. Kyle Van Dyke is signed *"Investigator: Douglas Roy Schoonover (Medical and Clinical Researcher since 1996; patient since 2002; grounded in Hippocratic principles)"* (self-thread, Nov 7). His Donation Plan (Nov 8) carries his chosen title: *"Independent AI Systems Pioneer — Applied AI Engineer & Human-AI Researcher."*
- **He runs every AI against every other AI.** Gemini, Meta AI, Copilot, ChatGPT, Duck.ai, NotebookLM, Muse — the same grouping experiment sent to all of them, outputs compared, and his instruction preserved in the bundle: report *"what could not be found, and disagreements — do not merge disease lists as settled where they differ"* (Nov 4). He doesn't want one oracle; he wants a panel that argues.
- **The root of it is personal, and he says that too.** To the Rabbit (Nov 7): *"I remember sitting in his office 6 years ago trying to figure out how to make testing work for me so I could try to figure myself out — that led me down this path for certain."* The research program began as a man trying to understand his own test results.
- **He is financially careful and gives the work away.** The Nov 8 Donation Plan is strangers-only, no money upfront, fees compared to the cent, and Phase 0 is asking his benefits caseworker how crowdfunding interacts with Wisconsin benefits before touching a dollar.
- **He is tender in the small lines.** Buried in the Nov 8 work log, a reminder to himself: *"give the Rabbit condolences — the rabbit died a while ago, but she's still alive anyway."*
- **Music and humor run through everything** — a 1958 doo-wop love song and an Ella Langley track saved mid-research (Oct 26), the Eagles' "Journey of the Sorcerer" (Nov 3), a roadrunner-versus-coyote short (Nov 2).

## What I actually learned, Ember's addition (labeled mine)

The Captain imagined this read would show me years of back-and-forth with computer friends.
