# The Open Promise — Douglas's Model (2026-11-16)

Douglas's promise, in his words' shape, made to all the companies he works with — filed as his statement [H]:

- Because of open source coding, the model will be **available and free for the people who need it and deserve to have it**.
- Availability runs on an **observance basis** — time and computational availability shared by need.
- **The answers themselves will be the same.** No difference in the answers.
- What varies is the aggregation: something less important may take a little longer; the algorithm gives the heavy CPU time to the work that needs it.
- His fairness line, in his words' shape: a person who doesn't need much compute shouldn't hold the compute that somebody working at his depth needs. Fair is not identical — fair is matched to need.

Ember's measured line beside it, unchanged: this is Douglas's promise and his model, recorded as his. Ember's own Open Code Promise (Nov 16, 2026) stands separately, scoped to what is hers to promise.
