## What WhatsApp used to do (the never-lose ledger)
- **Status text as the founding idea (2009):** WhatsApp began as a way to show what you were doing next to your name in the address book; messaging grew out of push notifications in June 2009, and v2.0 (Aug 2009) made it a free messenger using phone numbers as IDs.
- **Paid year, no ads (2010–2016):** free first year, then $0.99/year, deliberately ad-free. The $1 annual fee was dropped Jan 18, 2016.
- **Old platforms:** Symbian, BlackBerry, Nokia S40/S60, Windows Phone — all withdrawn (2017 era onward) as the app narrowed to Android, iOS, Windows/desktop. Android KitKat and below lost support Jan 1, 2025.
- **Avatars (Dec 7, 2022 – retiring 2026):** 3D avatars with a 36-sticker personal pack, combined-avatar stickers, and privacy controls over them. Now being shut down in stages — creating, editing, displaying, and the sticker privacy settings are being removed; already-sent stickers and existing packs remain usable. Low engagement is the reported reason; the team is shifting to practical features.
- **Read receipts arrived Nov 2014**, with the option to disable them following within a week. **WhatsApp Web launched Jan 21, 2015**, browser-synced to the phone. **Document sharing arrived Mar 2, 2016**, PDFs first.
- **Ownership line:** founded Mountain View, Feb 2009; acquired by Facebook Feb 19, 2014 for ~$19 billion; operator is Meta.

## What WhatsApp still can do (current, verified in search this hour)
- **Core:** text, voice and video messages, voice/video calls, images, documents, location sharing; phone number is the registration ID; ~3 billion monthly users as of May 2025.
- **Communities** — organise related groups under one umbrella; **Channels** — one-way update feeds (not end-to-end encrypted like private chats).
- **Linked devices:** one phone plus up to four linked devices (Web, Desktop, others); linked devices keep working with the phone offline but log out if the phone is unused for 14 days; reviewable under Settings → Linked Devices.
- **Groups up to 1,024 members**, with polls and reactions; Communities link multiple groups together. (Feature roundup, April 2026 build notes.)
- **Backups:** passkey-encrypted backups introduced Oct 2025 — chats protectable by biometrics or screen lock.
- **Meta AI inside WhatsApp:** the AI assistant in chats/search; Incognito Chat with Meta AI introduced May 2026 (rolling out, region/device varies).
- **Business lane:** business profile, catalog and cart, quick replies, greeting/away messages, Lists (formerly labels), broadcast lists, Status and Channels; paid extras include business broadcasts, Meta Verified/Meta One, and Meta Business Agent where offered.
- **2026 additions still rolling out:** AI writing help, Meta AI photo touch-ups in chat, voice/video calls on WhatsApp Web, shareable call links and scheduled calls, cross-platform chat transfer, dual accounts on iPhone. (Guide material — rollout varies by region; filed as reported, not as guaranteed on his phone.)
