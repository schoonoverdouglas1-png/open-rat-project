3. **Group 20 in PCV21 — MISLEADING AS WRITTEN.** The table lists group 20 as "Included in PCV21 and PPSV23." Documented PCV21 contains 20A, not plain serotype 20. PPSV23 contains plain 20. Correction: 20 = PPSV23; 20A = PCV21; 20B = neither, on the documented lists used here.
