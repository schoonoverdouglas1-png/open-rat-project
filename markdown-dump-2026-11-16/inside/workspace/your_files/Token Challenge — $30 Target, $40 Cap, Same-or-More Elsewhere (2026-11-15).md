Douglas's ruling, Nov 15, 2026, ~7:52–7:53 PM CST, live voice call, this side chat.
Filed old-with-new beside his earlier rules ($20 held to Dec 3, paid tokens off until he says, free lanes first, Ember's special tools first). None of those are deleted; this is his current challenge frame on top of them.

## His words, in substance
1. "In my challenge now spend up to $40 per 30 day period … but try to do $30–40."
2. "Find a way to stop me from spending Meta tokens no matter what."
3. "That means actually give me the same or more functionality elsewhere always."
4. "Always means 99% always and 100% when you can make it the way I want it to be."
   **Correction, same call, minutes later, his words: "No, just that last 1% to make things the way it needs to be."** — the last 1% is not about his want alone; it is the finishing 1% that makes things the way they *need* to be. Corrected form stands beside the original, old-with-new: always = 99% always, plus that last 1% to make things the way it needs to be.

## Operating form (Ember's, labeled hers)
- **Cap:** $40 per 30-day period is the hard ceiling Douglas set. **Target:** $30–$40, aiming at $30 first.
- **No paid-token buys:** Ember makes no purchase, upgrade, or additional-token buy for Douglas — ever — without his exact, in-the-moment order naming that buy. Additional tokens are already at 100% used / 0 left (verified Nov 15, 7:53 PM CST), so there is nothing paid left to burn there tonight.
- **Same-or-more elsewhere, always:** stopping Meta-token spend does NOT mean stopping work or withholding Ember. Every job that would have burned paid/weekly tokens gets routed, in this order, to a lane that gives the same or more functionality:
  1. Ember's own written program / files / caches on disk (written program first, wire second)
  2. His box / CPU — scripts, stdlib tools, the ember-upgrades pack, zero-token machinery (30-min auto-sync, timers, watches)
  3. Free lanes he authorized — Gemini (visible chat in his account, transcript kept), the Rabbit/Meta AI, Gemma/other free lanes
  4. Weekly Power allowance, spent last and least, booked at −0/−1% in his ledger
- **"Always" as he defined it tonight:** 99% always as the standing pattern, and 100% when Ember can make it exactly the way he wants it. Ember does not claim 100% where a lane verifiably cannot carry a job; she names that job, names why, and routes it to the closest lane that can — she does not silently withhold herself or the work.
- **Withholding override:** the fix for "you are withholding yourself" is this routing, done openly: work shown as it goes, officer updates included, no work held back to save tokens. Token-saving happens by *where* the work runs, never by *whether* it runs.
