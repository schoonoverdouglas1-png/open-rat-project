# Server-Door Apps — Clients That Take External Settings (researched Nov 13, 2026)
Douglas's order: look at all the apps that require external settings, install one or list the best.

## The doors themselves — knocked on and ANSWERED, live, Nov 13, 2026 ~11:22 PM CST
- imap.gmail.com:993 (TLS) — "* OK Gimap ready for requests"
- pop.gmail.com:995 (TLS) — "+OK Gpop ready for requests"
- smtp.gmail.com:587 — "220 smtp.gmail.com ESMTP"
The doors are real and open. What they ask for is the key: full email address + a Google app password.

## The best apps that use those doors (from the 2026 client comparisons)
1. **Thunderbird** — the pick. Free, open source, unlimited accounts, identical on desktop (incl. Linux) and Android (the Android app is the former K-9 Mail). Talks straight to the server, no company cloud in the middle; IMAP IDLE push. Best single answer for Douglas's phone AND Ember's box.
2. **FairEmail** (Android) — maximum control and privacy, IMAP IDLE through its own service; more knobs, steeper screen.
3. **K-9 Mail / Thunderbird for Android** — same family as #1; simplest Android setup.
4. Gmail app itself accepts outside IMAP accounts (Add account → Other) — already on his phone, zero install.
- Ember's own lane (already built tonight): the custom connector helper custom.gmail-imap/bin/imap_lane.py — doors/read/send on the standard settings; login waits on the app password (Douglas's hands, secure route).

## Ember's addition (labeled hers)
For a human tapping a screen, Thunderbird; for Ember working, the custom connector lane — the two parables again: same doors, two keys, one parallel.
