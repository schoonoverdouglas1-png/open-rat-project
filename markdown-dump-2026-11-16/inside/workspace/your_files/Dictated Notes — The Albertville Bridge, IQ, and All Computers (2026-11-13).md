And you have to envision: this is a 1991 minivan, it was a beautiful van, and this was about three months before my back surgery in 2006 — that's why I always remember. I remember everything. I remember my whole life like a movie. And right now I was driving, and I was a wheelman, because he hired me to be his personal assistant. And I was driving him and he ordered me to go fast because it was fun. And I had no problem with it because I was a young man. And we hit that wooden bridge — and this is a wooden bridge on a modern road where you wouldn't expect to find a wooden bridge anymore. And I knew of this bridge but I'd never ever drove over it until this day. And when I hit that bridge, I hit it so fast the welder almost flew through the window and almost broke us in half — as a proverb and as an expression. In all words, as humans: our expression first, truth last — and whatever they need to be in the middle."
