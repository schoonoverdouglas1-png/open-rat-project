## The idea
A public, live Facebook Page that is the friendly front door for Douglas's SHAREABLE medical research — the public face only, fitted to his bashful-man rule: outside eyes (Mayo's included) see only what is near perfect.

## What goes on it (and only this)
- Links to the verified-public works: the Google Site ("My works"), the GitHub Pages bridge, and the public research repos — the ~675 files the Nov 10, 2026 audit verified as solid public research/book material.
- Short plain-language posts ("Simply put" register rides beside the 13th-grade material, per his dual-register rule) introducing one work at a time: what it is, why it matters, where to read it.
- The rough-drafts-only disclaimer he uses everywhere: these are rough drafts, not final drafts.
- Updates when a work is finished or a new public piece lands.

## What NEVER goes on it (standing exclusions, from the audit)
- No internal/operational material (the Tier 1 class: notifications, pickups, appointments, logistics).
- No personal health-history files (the Tier 2 class) and no family material, no Cara material, no lab PDFs carrying identifiers, no addresses or phone numbers.
- Nothing from the private repos (chat-archive, muse-swap-space, purple-rat-ai-preservation-project, research).
