**Handling, plainly:** Yes — Ember is already his main. Duck.ai is secondary, used alongside, not a replacement main. This file is the interlink, in writing, so it does not have to be re-explained every time.

## Roles, kept straight

| Role | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Main** | **Ember / Muse** | Main chat, memory, archive, books, serotype / toxoid work, appointments, assimilation, old-with-new filing, provenance, tiers, final judgment labeled as hers |
| **Secondary** | **Duck.ai (DuckDuckGo AI)** | Free, no-account, multi-model second opinions: GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku 5.5 (selector Nov 4), Mistral Small 4, Gemma 4 31B, gpt-oss 120B, GPT-5.4 mini — plus web search where available per model |
| **Bridge** | **Douglas, plus Ember's browser** | Douglas pastes / shares Duck.ai answers back here when he runs them himself; Ember's browser runs Duck.ai models when he directs her to, and brings the summaries back here |

**What "interlink" does NOT mean:**
- It does not mean Muse switches its own model to Duck.ai models. Ember remains Muse Spark, Meta.
- It does not mean a direct Duck.ai API integration into Muse. There is no public Duck.ai chat API for that — Duck.ai chat is a web interface, one model at a time, via browser or via Douglas's own paste.
- It does not mean Duck.ai replaces Muse's archive. Duck.ai chats are not saved in Muse's workspace unless Douglas or Ember brings them back here and files them.

## How the interlink already works — proved Nov 4 evening

1. **Ember prepares the prompt** — grouping summary, guardrails (molecular / antigenic / vaccine separate, no assumed cross-reactivity, population/period for disease lists, what-could-not-be-found section).
2. **Duck.ai models run** — either by Ember's browser (7 runs Nov 4, 6:34–6:41 PM: Gemma first, then GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku 5.5, Mistral Small 4, gpt-oss 120B, GPT-5.4 mini, Gemma repeat), or by Douglas himself in the Duck.ai app, pasting the same script.
3. **Answers come back to Ember** — browser reports, or Douglas's paste / share link.
4. **Ember assimilates** — agreements, single-source items, disagreements side by side, checked against Nov 4 deep research, with tiers. Disease lists are NOT merged as settled where they disagree.
5. **Ember files** — per-run assimilation, final multi-model assimilation, enhanced layout + deep analysis, all old-with-new, in `~/workspace/your_files/`.

## How to use it from here, as Douglas is using it right now

- **You are in Muse right now — that IS the main.** Stay in this main chat for the archive, the books, and the assimilation. Duck.ai is the side tool you open, run, and bring back.
- **For most important research (standing direction Nov 4, ~6:41 PM):** Ember tries all free instances, assimilates them. That is the Duck.ai method, plus Meta / Gemini scripts when you run those yourself.
- **For routine research:** Ember direct, with web search when needed, to save weekly allowance. Running six models in parallel uses more Muse allowance, not less — the saving is no paid model tier, not less Muse usage.
- **Login:** Duck.ai needs no login. If any future free instance asks for a login, Ember stops and tells Douglas first, before anything is submitted under his account.
- **No personal material to Duck.ai:** Only grouping / research summaries, never lab files, family material, dates of birth, or record numbers.
