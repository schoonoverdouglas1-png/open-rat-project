## Simply put

Before, the table was mostly a list: which serotypes sit in which group, and which vaccines cover which ones. With the new frame, every entry also has to say: which of the six separate tracks it belongs to, where it came from (its Key), and whether the connection is full, partial, or still a gap. Nothing old is erased. New questions Ember has go in Shiro, labeled as hers, where they can be tested without disturbing the list.

## 2. The six tracks applied — what was collapsed before

The old table's columns quietly mixed tracks. Separated:

| Track | What in the serogroup table belongs to it | Rule when reading |
|---|---|---|
| Vaccines | The PCV13/15/20/21 and PPSV23 columns in Copy 3 | Coverage is a vaccine fact only. It says nothing by itself about disease, illness, or infection. |
| Viruses | Nothing in the pneumococcal table itself — co-pathogen/viral associations, where his work raises them, are a separate track | Any serotype↔virus relationship is reported **partial** when it is partial (pneumonia rule), never upgraded. |
| Diseases | His §226 disease groups: meningitis, pneumonia, otitis, bacteremia/sepsis, carriage, outbreak, resistant, persister, replacement, archive (10 groups) | A serotype can sit in several disease groups; that is a grouping, not a diagnosis. |
| Illnesses | How a disease presents as an illness episode — not in the serogroup table | [GAP] in this table; belongs in his illness work, not invented here. |
| Sicknesses | The lived sickness experience — not in the serogroup table | [GAP] in this table, same rule. |
| Infections | Whether an infection with that organism is present in a given case — a laboratory/clinical fact in a case, not a table property | The table groups capsule types; it does not declare infections. |

What this buys: a row can now be read without smuggling. Example — **Group 23 (23A, 23B, 23F):** Vaccine track: PCV13/15/20 cover 23F only; PCV21 covers 23A, 23B (not 23F); PPSV23 covers 23F. Disease track: 23F's place in the pneumonia group comes from Douglas's settled Oct 29 ruling (23F in 7 disease groups, tied with 14), filed as his ruling, not derived from this table. Infection/illness/sickness tracks: [GAP] here, by design.

1. **Ask a clean question of one track** — e.g. "which groups have only partial vaccine coverage?" — and answer from the table without dragging disease or infection claims along. Answer from Copy 3, partial-coverage groups (some but not all members in any listed vaccine): 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 22, 23, 33 — most of the table. Full-coverage groups: 1, 2 (PPSV23), 3, 4, 5, 8 (in later vaccines), 14. That is a vaccine-track fact only.
2. **Show its work** — every row can now carry its Key, so a later reader (or a later Ember after an update) can rebuild *why* a row says what it says. This is the persistence-through-update property Douglas ordered applied to all things today.
3. **Hold disagreement without damage** — the 23F count conflict in his older works (6 groups in the Serology Book vs his ruled 7 in the Corrected Copy) stays visible in the Key column as RULING vs BOOK, old-with-new, instead of one silently overwriting the other.
