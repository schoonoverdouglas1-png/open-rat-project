# Wogahn vs. Dickson — pedigree comparison & investigation
Built Nov 11, 2026, at Douglas's request (voice call). Sources: Doximity profiles for both, OakLeaf Clinics' own Dickson page, NPI/CMS listings. Spelling note: Douglas says "Dixon / Logan" — the records read **Erik Dickson** and **Brent Wogahn**.

## The premise, tested
Douglas: "Doctor Dixon came Iowa." — **The record says no.** Iowa belongs to Wogahn. Dickson's school is the University of North Dakota. The two men's paths are similar in shape (which likely fused them), but the Iowa line is Wogahn's alone.

## Brent Matthew Wogahn, MD — general surgeon
- Medical school: University of Iowa, Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine, Class of 1990
- Residency: Surgery, University of Missouri–Kansas City (St. Luke's program), 1990–1995
- Board: American Board of Surgery — Surgery
- Licenses: MO 1991–1996; WI 1995–2027 (WI #36446)
- Practice: Evergreen Surgical SC, Eau Claire; CMS affiliation: OakLeaf Surgical Hospital, Altoona (1 facility); wider directories list more
- ~35–36 years experience; 2 PubMed papers from residency era (1995, 1996)

## Erik Joel Dickson, MD, FAAFP — family medicine
- Medical school: University of North Dakota School of Medicine & Health Sciences, 1997
- Residency: Family Medicine, University of Wisconsin — listed as Madison by Doximity, as the UW Eau Claire Family Medicine program by OakLeaf (the UW program based in Eau Claire); completed 2000
- Board: American Board of Family Medicine — Family Medicine; Fellow, American Academy of Family Physicians (FAAFP)
- License: WI 1998–2027 (#40111) — Wisconsin only
- Practice: OakLeaf Clinics–Chippewa Falls, his entire 25+ year career (his own statement, CVHC 2025); medical director, OakLeaf urgent cares (Chippewa Falls, Eau Claire, Menomonie, Rice Lake); EMS medical director / first responder, Chippewa County
- Hospital affiliation (OakLeaf's own page): OakLeaf Surgical Hospital, Altoona — the same hospital as Wogahn's CMS affiliation; NPI lists additional affiliations (Mayo Eau Claire, Marshfield Eau Claire, others)
- ~29 years experience

## Comparison analysis
- SAME SHAPE: both MDs, both board-certified, both WI licenses running to 2027, both affiliated with OakLeaf Surgical Hospital, both Upper-Midwest schools, both ~3 decades in, both built their careers in the Chippewa Valley.
- THE FORK: specialty — Wogahn is the surgeon (American Board of Surgery); Dickson is the family medicine doctor (American Board of Family Medicine). Note the mirror: Douglas asked earlier today whether Wogahn was family medicine — that title actually belongs to Dickson.
- GEOGRAPHY: Wogahn traveled — Iowa → Missouri (residency + first license) → Wisconsin in 1995. Dickson barely moved — North Dakota → residency in Eau Claire → Chippewa Falls for his whole career, Wisconsin license only.
- TIMING: Wogahn is the senior by a class: MD 1990 vs Dickson MD 1997 — seven years apart; Wogahn was already licensed in Wisconsin (1995) before Dickson finished medical school.
- EXTRAS: Dickson carries leadership posts Wogahn's listings don't show (FAAFP fellowship, urgent-care medical director, EMS medical director). Wogahn carries residency-era research publications Dickson's listings don't show.

## Investigative notes / gaps
- Dickson residency wording differs by source (UW Madison vs UW–Eau Claire program) — most likely one program, two labels; flagged, not merged.
- Wogahn affiliation lists conflict by source (CMS: 1 facility; directories: several). Dickson's OakLeaf-page affiliation is the single OakLeaf Surgical Hospital line; NPI lists more.
- Neither profile lists undergraduate schooling. Nothing in either record shows the two men trained together or at the same institution at the same time — their overlap is OakLeaf Surgical Hospital and the Chippewa Valley, not a shared school.
