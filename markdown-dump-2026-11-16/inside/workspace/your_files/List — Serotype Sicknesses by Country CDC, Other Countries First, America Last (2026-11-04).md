# List — Serotype Sicknesses by Country CDC, Other Countries First, America Last

**Built:** November 4, 2026, ~3:21 PM CST
**Ordering rule:** other countries first, America last, always.
**Note on "CDC's of their related countries":** Not every country has an agency literally called CDC. The list below gives each country's CDC-equivalent — its national disease-control / health-security agency — and the serotype sicknesses it tracks. Where a country uses a regional body (ECDC for EU/EEA), that body is listed, with the national agency beside it.
**Status:** Reading list, NOT medical advice. Sicknesses = invasive pneumococcal disease (IPD: bacteremia, sepsis, meningitis, bacteremic pneumonia) and non-invasive disease (pneumonia without bacteremia, otitis media, sinusitis, bronchitis), by serotype.

## 1. Europe — ECDC, plus national agencies FIRST

**Agency:** European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC), Stockholm, Sweden — EU/EEA surveillance, TESSy / Surveillance Atlas.
**Sicknesses by serotype, ECDC Annual Epidemiological Report:**
- 2023, EU/EEA: 24,567 confirmed IPD cases.
  - Children <5: most common serotypes 19A, 3, 24F, 10A, 33F, 22F, 8, 38.
  - Age ≥65: most common serotypes 3, 19A, 8, 22F, 6C.
  - <5, serotype known: ~40% PCV13/non-PCV10 serotype, rising over 5 years.
  - ≥65, serotype known: 32% PCV13/non-PCV10, 18% PCV20/non-PCV15, 27% non-PCV/PPV.
- 2022, EU/EEA: 17,700 confirmed IPD cases.
  - Most common, in order: 3, 8, 19A, 22F, 6C, 23B, 9N, 4, 23A, 11A, 15A — 73.9% of typed isolates.
- 2016, EU/EEA: 21,986 confirmed IPD cases.
  - 10 most common: 8, 3, 12F, 22F, 19A, 9N, 15A, 10A, 33F, 11A — 64% of typed isolates.
- 2010, 26 European countries: 21,565 IPD cases, 9,946 serotyped.
  - Most common: 19A, 1, 7F, 3, 14, 22F, 8, 4, 12F, 19F — 59.8% of serotyped isolates.
  - Multidrug resistance: 19A, 14, 19F, 23F. Dual penicillin + erythromycin resistance: 19F, 19A, 14, 15A, 6A, 6B, 9V, 23A, 23F, 24A.

**Britain — UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA), England:**
- Surveillance: IPD cases reported to UKHSA, isolates to UKHSA national reference laboratory for confirmation and serotyping; since 2017, whole-genome sequencing (WGS) instead of phenotypic methods.
- 2017–2020, England: 15,400 IPD cases, 13,749 sequenced. Serotype 8 most prevalent overall (3,074, 22.4%), PCV13 types only 20%.
- 2019–2020 to 2022–2023, England: 5,316 cases in 2019–20, 4,598 in 2022–23. PCV13-type IPD rose 19.4% to 29.7%, mainly due to serotype 3, also 19F, 19A, 4, alongside a decrease in non-PCV13 serotypes 8, 12F, 9N.
- July 2023–June 2024, England: 5,080 confirmed IPD cases, 4,826 serotyped (95.0%). PCV13-serotype IPD 1,402 (29.1%), PCV20-serotype IPD 3,105 (64.3%), including 1,703 (35.3%) additional vs. PCV13.

**Denmark — Statens Serum Institut (SSI), Copenhagen:**
- Quellung serotyping origin — SSI factor sera, the classic pneumococcal serotyping method, used worldwide.
- Denmark mortality cohort, 1977–2007, 18,858 IPD patients: serotypes 31, 11A, 35F, 17F, 3, 16F, 19F, 15B, 10A associated with highly increased 30-day mortality vs. serotype 1, age ≥5.

**Spain — Instituto de Salud Carlos III / Madrid surveillance:**
- CSF 2018–2024, 144 pneumococcal meningitis cases: six serotypes 3, 8, 10A, 23B, 12B/12F, 6C accounted for 54.9%. Serotype 10A 36.4% of PCR-detected culture-negative CSF, 21.9% in children vs. 4.5% adults.

## 2. Asia / South Asia / Western Pacific — national CDCs and reviews

**China CDC / Southwest China hospitals, 2018–2022:**
- 263 pneumococcal disease patients, all ages. Most common serotypes: 19F (17.87%), 19A (11.41%), 3 (8.75%), 23F (6.46%), 6A (5.70%). PCV coverage: PCV10 36.12%, PCV13 61.98%, PCV15 61.98%, PCV20 63.12%, PCV24 64.26%.

**South and Southeast Asia, 2012–2024, 14 countries, 96 studies:**
- Carriage + disease common: 6A, 6B, 14, 15B/15C, 19F, 23F; disease also common: 1, 3, 5, 19A, 15A, 10A, 35B.
- Most common non-PCV13 disease serotypes: 15A (0.6%–21.4%), 15B/C (1.3%–18.0%), 10A (2.5%–14.3%), 35B (0.6%–14.3%).

**Japan / Western Pacific:**
- Post-PCV childhood review: serotype 24F prevalent in Western Pacific and Europe, but not North America. Serotype 38 more prevalent in North America, 12F / 15C / 10A in Europe in that review.

**India / Iraq / Middle East reviews:**
- India: customized multiplex PCR, CDC Atlanta protocol rearranged for India; 408 IPD cases, case fatality 17.8%; pneumonia 39%, meningitis 24.3%, septicaemia 18.4%; predominant serotypes 1, 3, 5, 19F, 8, 14, 23F, 4, 19A, 6B.
- Iraq review: serotype distribution varies by age, resistance genes, disease syndrome and severity; >100 serotypes known, ~23 cause 80–90% of invasive disease.

## 3. Africa — Africa CDC, plus national surveillance

**Africa CDC / GERMS-SA, South Africa, 2024:**
- GERMS-SA: 12,657 laboratory-confirmed bacterial, fungal, parasitic cases, 220+ laboratories. Vaccine-preventable diseases: pneumococcus (all normally sterile sites), Hib, Group B streptococcus tracked. Invasive meningococcal disease increased, driven by serogroups B, W, Y.
- African meningitis belt: serotype 1 pneumococcal meningitis 60%–80% of pneumococcal meningitis in recent studies, higher incidence and case fatality vs. meningococcal meningitis.
- Uganda children <5: most common serotype 6A/6B (40%), followed by 22A, 23F, 14, 19A.
- Malawi (Blantyre): urine pneumococcal antigen (ssUAD, UKHSA assay, 24 serotypes) study to distinguish carriage from disease in children <5 with pneumonia — assay not available in Malawi, samples tested in UK.

## 4. Latin America

**Latin America, post-PCV childhood review:**
- Non-PCV13 serotypes contributed 28.5% of childhood IPD in Latin America in that review, vs. 57.8% North America, 71.9% Europe, 45.9% Western Pacific, 42.7% South Africa, 9.2% Eastern Mediterranean.
- Predominant non-PCV13 serotypes overall: 22F, 12F, 33F, 24F, 15C, 15B, 23B, 10A, 38, rank varying by region.

## 5. Canada — Public Health Agency of Canada (PHAC) / National Microbiology Laboratory

- Canada, adult review: residual / emerging serotype 4 in US and Canada, vs. serotype 8 in Europe and South Africa.
- Canada included in US/Canada PCV20/21 adult rationale; serotype 4 noted as rising in certain US adult populations and covered by PCV20.

## 6. America LAST — United States CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention), Atlanta

**Agency:** CDC, Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP), MMWR.
**Sicknesses by serotype, CDC / ACIP:**
- Pneumococcus: "common bacterial cause of respiratory tract infections, bacteremia" — IPD cases and pneumonia, in CDC MMWR adult recommendations.
- PPSV23 serotypes, CDC MMWR 2023: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6B, 7F, 8, 9N, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15B, 17F, 18C, 19F, 19A, 20, 22F, 23F, 33F.
- PCV20 serotypes, CDC: 1, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7F, 8, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15B, 18C, 19A, 19F, 22F, 23F, 33F — "20 serotypes estimated to cause over 2,000 deaths and more than 65,000 cases of IPD, including bacteremia and meningitis, and community-acquired pneumonia annually in adults aged 50 to 64" in Pfizer / CDC-cited summary; "more than half of IPD cases in people aged 50 to 64 were caused by these 20 serotypes" 2018–2022.
- PCV21 serotypes, CDC ACIP 2024, adults ≥50: 3, 6A, 7F, 8, 9N, 10A, 11A, 12F, 15A, 15B, 15C, 16F, 17F, 19A, 20A, 22F, 23A, 23B, 24F, 31, 33F, 35B — invasive disease and pneumonia, with pneumonia indication excluding 15B (invasive indication includes 15B by OPA criteria).
- Serotype 4, US: "serotype 4 disease, which incidence has been rising in certain populations in the western US" — covered by PCV20, noted in CDC ACIP discussion.
- US childhood post-PCV review: serotype 19A most predominant childhood IPD, 21.8% in PCV7 settings, 14.2% in PCV10/13 settings; non-PCV13 serotypes 42.2% in PCV10/13 settings; 22F most common non-PCV13 serotype, 5% of childhood IPD, followed by 12F, 15C, 24F, 33F (~4% each); 24F NOT prevalent in North America, vs. Europe / Western Pacific; 38 more prevalent in North America.
- CDC disease list, general: invasive pneumococcal disease = bacteremia, sepsis, meningitis, bacteremic pneumonia; non-invasive = pneumonia without bacteremia, otitis media, sinusitis, bronchitis. Serotype determines which sickness predominates, per Part 1 table.

## How to read through this list

1. Start at Europe — longest ECDC series, 2010 to 2023, with year-by-year serotype shifts.
2. Then Britain — UKHSA WGS series, serotype 8 dominance, then serotype 3 rise.
3. Then Asia / Africa / Latin America — same serotypes, different ranks: 19F in Asia, 1 in Africa, 6A/6B in Uganda, 24F in Western Pacific / Europe but not North America.
4. Then America last — CDC ACIP lists, PCV20 / PCV21, serotype 4 western US rise, 19A post-PCV7 rise.
5. The sickness is the same by serotype number everywhere: 3 = pneumonia / high death risk, 10A = meningitis / lethality, 19A = invasive pneumonia / empyema / multidrug resistance, 1 = invasive pneumonia / meningitis belt, 14 = non-bacteremic pneumonia / otitis media, 8 = bacteraemia / invasive pneumonia. Country changes the rank, not the capsule.
